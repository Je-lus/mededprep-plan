## Research Needed

For the Tier 1 competitors specifically, we need:
1. **Pricing** — what do programs pay? What do students pay?
2. **Item bank size** — how many items do they claim?
3. **Adaptive claims** — is anyone actually doing IRT, or is it all marketing?
4. **Market share** — how many programs use each? (Hard to get, but worth trying)
5. **Recent moves** — are any of them expanding into CE, accreditation compliance, or certification?
6. **Weaknesses** — what do programs and students complain about? (Reviews, forums, Reddit)

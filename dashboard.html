Reading Skills Validation — Justin Kern
EMS Reading Skills Assessment · Awaiting updates

Focus: Validating an EMS reading skills assessment. Test developed by founder, handed off to Kern's team for psychometric validation.

Status: Development complete. Validation in progress with Kern's team. No updates received yet.

Potential: Correlate reading comprehension scores with metacognitive calibration data from UIUC study. Reading comprehension may be a mediating variable between domain knowledge and calibration quality. Poorly calibrated students may struggle with reading complex EMS scenarios, not just content gaps.

Why It Matters
  • If reading skills predict calibration, it opens an intervention pathway (remediate reading, improve calibration, improve NREMT outcomes)
  • Adds a second peer-reviewed data thread to support grant applications
  • Supports Company C's diagnostic reporting (cognitive process level L1-L2 may confound with reading skill)

Formalize the bifactor psychometric framework, implement transfer pricing, and launch certification levels. The CE-to-certification item pipeline calibrates items at industrial scale. EMT launch-ready in 3–4 years.

  • Bifactor framework formalized (ECV 0.60–0.80)
  • Transfer pricing (8–15% royalty, phased)
  • All-level competency frameworks → JTA
  • CE-to-cert calibration pipeline live

Stand up the Trust as the ownership layer above The Vault, launch Company E as the cross-entity data product, and harden competitive positioning against Pearson/Ascend consolidation.

  • Wyoming DAPT trust (revocable → irrevocable)

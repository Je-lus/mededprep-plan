Next 90 Days

The three things that matter
Priority 1
Sell
Heather starts generating pipeline immediately. Nationwide relationships. 60% commission, 1099. Zero fixed cost risk. Every program she brings in drives recurring student subscription revenue.
The math: 10 new programs at ~20 students each = ~$5,200/month additional recurring. 50 programs = ~$26K/month. The $200K from 2025 was with deliberate restraint.
Priority 2
Ship CE Content
CAPCE-accredited continuing education courses are in final stages. This unlocks a new revenue stream from working clinicians — not just students — and feeds the certification item calibration pipeline.
Market: 620K+ certified EMS clinicians. ~14M CE hours/yr. At 0.5% penetration = 5.2M item responses/yr for calibration.
Priority 3
Launch CoAssist at ACCREDITCON
Full public release at ACCREDITCON, end of May 2026. Free for programs. No competitor exists in this space. Every program that adopts CoAssist is a program sending students to MedEdPrep.
The vacuum: 57% of programs cite cost, 50% cite time as pain points. CoAEMSP is building their own tool but it serves the accreditor, not programs.

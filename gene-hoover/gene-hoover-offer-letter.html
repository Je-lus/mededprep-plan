RE:  Offer of Employment, Director of Education

Dear Gene,

It is my pleasure to offer you the position of Director of Education with MedEdPrep, LLC. You have shaped our education from its first outline, and there is no one better suited to lead its delivery. This letter sets out the terms of that offer; the enclosed position description details the role.

  1. Position. You will serve as Director of Education. You will be primarily responsible for the delivery of MedEdPrep education across the company's programs, including didactic and skills-laboratory instruction, course content and examination development, and student progression, and for program administration, including regulatory compliance for the programs under your direction, faculty coordination, and clinical partnerships. You will hold the Program Director designation for MedEdPrep's state-designated programs. You will also perform such other duties in support of the company's educational mission as the company may reasonably assign from time to time. You will report to Jeramey Ricks, Chief Executive Officer.
  2. Outreach Duties. From time to time, and when coordinated with you in advance, you may be asked to travel to partner EMS agencies and facilities to deliver on-site training, continuing education, or program presentations on MedEdPrep's behalf. Outreach assignments will be scheduled with reasonable notice and with regard to your primary teaching calendar.
  3. Employment Status. This is a part-time, hourly, W-2 position. Your employment with MedEdPrep is at-will, which means that either you or MedEdPrep may end the employment relationship at any time, with or without cause and with or without notice, although we will each endeavor to provide reasonable notice. Nothing in this letter creates a contract of employment for any fixed period.
  4. Start Date. Your employment in this position will begin on a mutually agreed start date, confirmed in writing.
  5. Compensation. You will be paid $40.00 per hour for all hours worked, through MedEdPrep's regular payroll, less applicable tax withholdings. This position is classified as non-exempt; you will be paid overtime at one and one-half times your regular rate for hours worked over forty in a workweek. Your hours will be flexible and scheduled by mutual agreement. You will record all hours worked—including instruction, preparation, student and faculty communications, and administrative work—through MedEdPrep's timekeeping system.
  6. Travel & Expenses. Travel to outreach and partner sites is part of the position. When the one-way distance to an assigned site exceeds fifty miles, MedEdPrep will reimburse one-way mileage at the then-current IRS standard mileage rate for use of your personal vehicle. Reasonable, pre-approved out-of-pocket expenses will also be reimbursed. Mileage and expense records are submitted through MedEdPrep's internal portal.
  7. Location. Your primary work location is MedEdPrep's Carrollton, Georgia campus, together with clinical-affiliate and partner-agency sites as duties require. Administrative work may be performed remotely where applicable.
  8. Equipment. Where specialized tools, software, or licenses are needed to perform the work, MedEdPrep will provide them or reimburse reasonable, pre-approved costs. Any device you use for MedEdPrep work must be password-protected and encrypted.
  9. Outside Activities. We recognize you maintain other professional roles and clinical practice. You may continue outside employment and clinical work that does not create a conflict of interest with MedEdPrep or interfere with your duties under this letter; you agree to disclose any outside role that could reasonably create such a conflict.
  10. Confidentiality & Student Records. You agree to keep all MedEdPrep proprietary materials, business information, and student records and education data strictly confidential; to use them solely for the purposes of your employment; and to handle student records in accordance with applicable law and program policy. A copy of MedEdPrep's Confidentiality and Non-Disclosure Agreement (the “NDA”) is enclosed with this letter; your execution of it is a condition of employment. The NDA, including its survival provisions, governs alongside this letter; in the event of any conflict regarding confidentiality, the NDA controls. Upon conclusion of your employment, or earlier upon MedEdPrep's request, you will return all MedEdPrep data and materials, and remove any copies from applicable devices and accounts in your possession or control.
  11. Work Product & Intellectual Property. All curricula, course materials, scenarios, examination items, recordings, and other deliverables you create within the scope of your employment are works made for hire and the property of MedEdPrep, LLC. To the extent any of it does not qualify as a work made for hire, you hereby assign all right, title, and interest in it to MedEdPrep, LLC, and agree to sign any documents reasonably necessary to perfect that assignment. Materials you created before your employment with MedEdPrep, or that you create wholly outside the scope of it, remain your property; if you incorporate any such material into a MedEdPrep deliverable, you grant MedEdPrep a non-exclusive, royalty-free, perpetual license to use, adapt, and deliver that material as part of its educational programs. Nothing in this engagement restricts your use of the general skills, methods, and knowledge you develop.
  12. Governing Law. This offer and your employment are governed by the laws of the State of Georgia, without regard to its conflict-of-laws principles.

Gene, this program exists because of the standard you hold it to. I am looking forward to building it with you for a long time.

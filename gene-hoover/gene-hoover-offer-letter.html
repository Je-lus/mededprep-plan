It is my pleasure to offer you the position of Program Director & Educator with MedEdPrep, LLC. Your employment with MedEdPrep is at-will, which means that either you or MedEdPrep may end the employment relationship at any time, with or without cause and with or without notice, although we will each endeavor to provide reasonable notice. Nothing in this letter creates a contract of employment for any fixed period.
  4. Start Date. Your employment in this position will begin on [start date].
  5. Compensation. [Salary of $X per year, paid through MedEdPrep's regular payroll / $X per hour for hours worked / per-course structure], less applicable tax withholdings. [Exempt / non-exempt classification and any overtime language to match.]

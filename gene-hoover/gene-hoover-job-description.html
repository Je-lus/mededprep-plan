MEDEDPREP, LLC

Position Description

Director of Education

Reports to: Chief Executive Officer · Location: Carrollton, Georgia campus, with travel to clinical and partner-agency sites · Status: Part-time, hourly, non-exempt

Purpose

The Director of Education owns the delivery of MedEdPrep education across the company's programs and the administration that keeps the programs under this position's direction in good standing with their regulatory bodies. This position holds the Program Director designation for MedEdPrep's state-designated programs and represents MedEdPrep education to partner agencies in the field.

Primary Responsibilities: Education Delivery

  • Deliver didactic instruction for MedEdPrep courses, including classroom lecture, case-based and scenario instruction, and structured review aligned to the relevant credentialing-examination blueprints.
  • Lead skills-laboratory instruction and oversee lab-day station design, rotation, and student evaluation.
  • Author and review course content: scenarios, knowledge checks, examination items, and supporting materials; serve as a subject-matter authority for MedEdPrep course content.
  • Review student pre-work and assessment results before each class session and adapt instruction to the cohort's demonstrated needs.
  • Support recorded-class and released-summary workflows so every session produces durable student-facing study material.

Primary Responsibilities: Program Administration

  • Own regulatory compliance for the programs under this position's direction, including designation requirements, records, and reporting.
  • Administer student enrollment, progression, remediation, and completion decisions.
  • Coordinate faculty, subject-matter experts, preceptors, and the Medical Director's review cycle.
  • Coordinate clinical rotations and institutional partnerships, including student clinical scheduling and clinical-hour completion tracking.

Secondary Responsibilities: Outreach Education

  • When coordinated in advance, travel to partner EMS agencies and facilities to deliver on-site training, continuing education, and program presentations on MedEdPrep's behalf.
  • Represent MedEdPrep professionally in outreach settings and identify partnership and training opportunities for follow-up.

General

  • Perform other duties as needed at the direction of the company in support of its educational mission.

Qualifications

  • Bachelor's degree.
  • Nationally Registered Paramedic (NRP); CCP-C or FP-C certification.
  • Instructor credentials.
  • Experience in the delivery of education content.
  • Clinical experience working in the field as a provider.
  • Meets Georgia OEMS&T program-director eligibility requirements.

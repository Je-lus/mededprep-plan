Position Description

Program Director & Educator

Reports to: Chief Executive Officer · Location: Carrollton, Georgia campus, with travel to clinical and partner-agency sites · Status: [full-time / part-time, exempt status]

Purpose

The Program Director & Educator owns the delivery of MedEdPrep education, first among it the Critical Care Academy, and the administration that keeps the program in good standing with the Georgia Office of EMS and Trauma (OEMS&T). This position also serves as Lead Critical Care Instructor, a dual role confirmed acceptable with OEMS&T, and represents MedEdPrep education to partner agencies in the field.

Primary Responsibilities: Education Delivery

  • Deliver didactic instruction for MedEdPrep courses, including classroom lecture, case-based and scenario instruction, and structured review aligned to the IBSC CCP-C and FP-C examination blueprints.
  • Lead skills-laboratory instruction and oversee lab-day station design, rotation, and student evaluation.
  • Author and review course content: scenarios, knowledge checks, examination items, and supporting materials; serve as subject-matter authority for critical care transport medicine content.
  • Review student pre-work and assessment results before each class session and adapt instruction to the cohort's demonstrated needs.
  • Support recorded-class and released-summary workflows so every session produces durable student-facing study material.

Primary Responsibilities: Program Administration

  • Own Georgia OEMS&T program compliance, including designation requirements, records, and reporting.
  • Administer student enrollment, progression, remediation, and completion decisions.
  • Coordinate faculty, subject-matter experts, preceptors, and the Medical Director's review cycle.
  • Coordinate clinical rotations and institutional partnerships, including the Tanner Health System clinical affiliation, student clinical scheduling, and clinical-hour completion tracking.

Secondary Responsibilities: Outreach Education

  • When coordinated in advance, travel to partner EMS agencies and facilities to deliver on-site training, continuing education, and program presentations on MedEdPrep's behalf.
  • Represent MedEdPrep professionally in outreach settings and identify partnership and training opportunities for follow-up.

Success Measures

  • Credentialing-examination pass rates that meet or exceed program targets.
  • Continuous OEMS&T compliance with zero findings on designation review.
  • Student clinical-hour completion on schedule for each cohort.
  • Delivered outreach engagements meeting partner-agency expectations.

Qualifications

  • [Credentials on file: certification levels, e.g. NRP, CCP-C or FP-C, instructor credentials, years of critical care transport experience. Do not publish until verified.]
  • Demonstrated critical care transport practice and teaching experience.
  • Meets Georgia OEMS&T program-director eligibility requirements.

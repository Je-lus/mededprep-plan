# Round 2 Agy Readback - Gene Hoover Packet

This packet establishes an at-will, part-time, non-exempt hourly ($40.00/hour) employment agreement engaging Gene Hoover as Director of Education and state Program Director for MedEdPrep, LLC, covering didactic instruction, program administration, and regional outreach. It pairs this operational role with a comprehensive confidentiality, non-disclosure, and intellectual property framework that claims company-developed curricula and exam items as works made for hire, reserves Gene's ownership in his pre-existing work subject to a perpetual company license, and imposes 12-to-24-month post-employment restrictions on confidentiality and business circumvention.

---

### 1. The Deal in Plain English: Job, Pay, Schedule, Status

* **Job Title & Role:** Gene Hoover is being hired as the **Director of Education** for MedEdPrep, LLC, reporting directly to Jeramey Ricks, Chief Executive Officer. In addition to internal executive oversight of education, Gene will hold the official regulatory title of **Program Director** for MedEdPrep's state-designated EMS education programs (fulfilling Georgia Office of EMS and Trauma requirements).
* **Work Locations:** The primary physical reporting station is MedEdPrep's campus in **Carrollton, Georgia**, combined with on-site responsibilities at clinical affiliate facilities and regional partner EMS agencies. Administrative duties may be performed remotely where applicable.
* **Pay & Compensation:**
  * **Hourly Wage:** $40.00 per hour for all compensable hours worked, paid on MedEdPrep's regular payroll cycle subject to applicable statutory tax withholdings.
  * **Overtime:** Classified as non-exempt under the Fair Labor Standards Act (FLSA); paid at one and one-half times the regular rate ($60.00 per hour) for any hours worked in excess of 40 hours in a single workweek.
  * **Compensable Activities:** All hours spent on instruction, lesson preparation, student and faculty communications, and administrative program management are compensable and must be logged in MedEdPrep's timekeeping system.
  * **Travel & Expense Reimbursement:** When travel to an outreach or partner site exceeds a one-way distance of 50 miles, MedEdPrep will reimburse one-way mileage at the prevailing IRS standard mileage rate for personal vehicle use. Reasonable out-of-pocket expenses are reimbursed if pre-approved and submitted via the internal portal.
  * **Equipment & Tools:** MedEdPrep commits to providing or reimbursing reasonable, pre-approved costs for specialized tools, software, or licenses required for the position.
* **Schedule:** Part-time and flexible. Hours are not fixed by a mandatory weekly schedule, shift rotation, or guaranteed minimum; instead, working hours are established by mutual agreement. Outreach training assignments are coordinated in advance with reasonable notice, scheduled with regard to Gene's primary teaching calendar. Employment commences on a mutually agreed start date confirmed in writing.
* **Employment Status:** Part-time, hourly, W-2 employee, non-exempt. Employment is strictly **at-will**, meaning either Gene or MedEdPrep can terminate the relationship at any time, with or without cause, and with or without notice (though both parties commit to endeavoring to provide reasonable advance notice). The packet does not constitute an employment contract for any fixed term.

### 4. Ownership of Teaching Materials: What Belongs to Whom (Before, During, and After Employment)

* **Before Employment:**
  * *What belongs to whom:* Everything Gene created prior to joining MedEdPrep remains his sole and exclusive personal property. MedEdPrep has no initial ownership claim over his historical teaching materials, outlines, lecture decks, or clinical notes.
  * *The licensing catch:* If Gene takes any of his pre-existing teaching materials and incorporates them into a MedEdPrep course deliverable, he automatically grants MedEdPrep a perpetual, royalty-free, non-exclusive, worldwide license to use, modify, adapt, and teach that material across its educational programs forever. Gene still owns the baseline IP, but MedEdPrep gets to use and evolve it without ever paying him royalties.
* **During Employment:**
  * *Company ownership:* Any curriculum, syllabus, case study, simulation scenario, examination item, item bank, video recording, or written deliverable Gene develops within the scope of his employment belongs entirely to MedEdPrep, LLC from the moment of creation as a "work made for hire." MedEdPrep holds 100% of the copyright and intellectual property rights.
  * *Employee's outside creations:* If Gene develops educational materials entirely on his own time, outside his working hours, without using MedEdPrep resources or confidential information, and not for MedEdPrep's course delivery, he owns those materials. However, if he subsequently incorporates them into MedEdPrep courseware, the company's perpetual royalty-free license attaches.
  * *Professional skills:* Gene retains unrestricted personal ownership and use of the general teaching skills, pedagogical methods, and clinical knowledge he develops or refines while working.
* **After Employment:**
  * *Company property stays with the company:* MedEdPrep retains full, perpetual ownership of all courseware, exam banks, recordings, and administrative materials created by Gene within his job scope. Gene cannot take, duplicate, distribute, or teach from MedEdPrep's proprietary materials, and must permanently scrub all copies from his personal laptops, external hard drives, and cloud storage.
  * *Employee keeps personal IP:* Gene keeps full ownership of his pre-existing materials and outside materials; he is expressly not required to delete or surrender materials that belong to him.
  * *Company keeps its license:* MedEdPrep does not have to delete, retire, or rewrite any course materials that contain Gene's pre-existing content. The company's non-exclusive license survives indefinitely, allowing MedEdPrep to continue delivering and adapting those materials without ongoing compensation to Gene.
  * *Future teaching restrictions:* Gene is free to use his general clinical expertise, teaching techniques, and knowledge in future roles, but he cannot use MedEdPrep's confidential item banks or proprietary curricula, nor can he solicit MedEdPrep's partner agencies to circumvent the company for 12 months.

### 6. Ambiguities, Contradictions, and Surprises

#### A. Ambiguous Terms (Could Be Read Two Ways)

1. **One-Way Mileage Reimbursement on Travel Over 50 Miles**
   * *Exact Text:* `"When the one-way distance to an assigned site exceeds fifty miles, MedEdPrep will reimburse one-way mileage at the then-current IRS standard mileage rate for use of your personal vehicle."` (Offer Letter, Section 6)
   * *The Ambiguity:* This phrasing is susceptible to two conflicting interpretations:
     * *Reading A (Literal):* The company literally reimburses only the mileage driven in a single direction. For instance, on a 120-mile round trip (60 miles each way), the company reimburses 60 miles, requiring the employee to absorb the return 60 miles out of pocket.
     * *Reading B (Threshold Trigger):* The term "one-way mileage" was awkwardly drafted to describe the qualifying threshold criteria (the destination is over 50 miles one-way), with the intent that round-trip mileage would be reimbursed once the threshold is crossed. Furthermore, it is ambiguous whether a trip of 45 miles one-way (90 miles round trip) receives zero mileage reimbursement or if local mileage is reimbursed under another unstated standard.

2. **Permissibility of Outside EMS Instruction vs. Conflict of Interest**
   * *Exact Text:* `"We recognize you maintain other professional roles and clinical practice. You may continue outside employment and clinical work that does not create a conflict of interest with MedEdPrep or interfere with your duties under this letter; you agree to disclose any outside role that could reasonably create such a conflict."` (Offer Letter, Section 9) vs. `"Nothing in this section limits the outside employment, clinical practice, and teaching permitted by the Recipient's offer letter."` (NDA, Section X)
   * *The Ambiguity:* Can be read two ways:
     * *Reading A:* Outside EMS teaching (e.g., teaching CE or didactic sessions for another EMS training agency, fire department, or technical college) is considered standard "outside employment" permitted as long as it does not physically conflict with MedEdPrep scheduled hours.
     * *Reading B:* Because MedEdPrep is an EMS education enterprise and the Offer Letter explicitly mentions only "outside employment and clinical work" without naming "teaching," any outside EMS instruction could be treated as an inherent, competitive "conflict of interest," exposing Gene to claims that his outside teaching breaches the agreement.

3. **Confidentiality Scope Regarding Historical/Pre-Signing Contributions**
   * *Exact Text:* `"You have shaped our education from its first outline, and there is no one better suited to lead its delivery."` (Offer Letter, Preamble) vs. `"The term 'Confidential Information' refers to any and all confidential, proprietary, or non-public information exchanged between the Parties on or after the Effective Date, whether directly or indirectly."` (NDA, Section I) vs. `"Materials you created before your employment with MedEdPrep, or that you create wholly outside the scope of it, remain your property..."` (Offer Letter, Section 11)
   * *The Ambiguity:* Can be read two ways:
     * *Reading A:* Outlines, draft curriculum, and pedagogical structures created by Gene during the early founding phase prior to September 21, 2026 are pre-existing works owned by Gene (subject only to MedEdPrep's perpetual license), and any information exchanged prior to September 21, 2026 is strictly excluded from the NDA's definition of "Confidential Information."
     * *Reading B:* Because Gene helped shape MedEdPrep's education from its inception, those initial outlines represent MedEdPrep proprietary material covered under the Offer Letter's broad mandate to `"keep all MedEdPrep proprietary materials, business information... strictly confidential,"` leaving the legal ownership and confidentiality status of his pre-signing developmental contributions unclear.

4. **Absence of Minimum or Projected Working Hours**
   * *Exact Text:* `"This position is classified as non-exempt; you will be paid overtime at one and one-half times your regular rate for hours worked over forty in a workweek. Your hours will be flexible and scheduled by mutual agreement."` (Offer Letter, Section 5)
   * *The Ambiguity:* Can be read two ways:
     * *Reading A:* The contemplation of overtime pay over 40 hours, paired with the comprehensive operational responsibilities outlined in the Position Description (curriculum authoring, lab instruction, clinical rotations, accreditation compliance, Medical Director liaison, outreach), implies a substantial role regularly approaching full-time volume (e.g., 30–40 hours/week).
     * *Reading B:* Because it is designated part-time with hours set purely by "mutual agreement" with no floor, MedEdPrep has no obligation to provide any minimum weekly hours, meaning Gene could receive zero or near-zero hours during academic breaks or low-enrollment periods.

#### B. Contradictory Terms (Two Documents Disagree)

1. **NDA Claims the Offer Letter Permits Outside "Teaching," But the Offer Letter Does Not**
   * *Exact Text (Offer Letter, Section 9):* `"We recognize you maintain other professional roles and clinical practice. You may continue outside employment and clinical work that does not create a conflict of interest with MedEdPrep or interfere with your duties under this letter; you agree to disclose any outside role that could reasonably create such a conflict."`
   * *Exact Text (NDA, Section X):* `"Nothing in this section limits the outside employment, clinical practice, and teaching permitted by the Recipient's offer letter."`
   * *The Contradiction:* NDA Section X creates an express carveout for outside `"teaching permitted by the Recipient's offer letter."` However, the Offer Letter does not permit outside teaching; it only authorizes `"outside employment and clinical work."` The NDA references a specific contractual permission in the Offer Letter that does not exist.

2. **Immediate Return/Deletion of Data vs. Seven-Day Post-Termination System Access Window**
   * *Exact Text (Offer Letter, Section 10):* `"Upon conclusion of your employment, or earlier upon MedEdPrep's request, you will return all MedEdPrep data and materials, and remove any copies from applicable devices and accounts in your possession or control."`
   * *Exact Text (NDA, Section XI):* `"Upon the end of the Recipient's employment, or upon written request from the Disclosing Party, the Recipient must return all Confidential Information and property belonging to the Disclosing Party... All copies or extracts of the Confidential Information on the Recipient's personal devices or accounts must be promptly and permanently deleted."`
   * *Exact Text (NDA, Section IV(d)(v)):* `"...cease all access and return or destroy any stored credentials within seven (7) days of the end of the Recipient's employment for any reason."`
   * *The Contradiction:* The Offer Letter (Section 10) and the NDA's general return clause (Section XI) require immediate cessation of use, prompt surrender of property, and immediate permanent deletion of files upon termination. Conversely, NDA Section IV(d)(v) establishes a 7-day post-termination window within which the former employee is instructed to cease platform access and return/destroy login credentials. One provision demands immediate termination and data removal, while the other contemplates ongoing system access and credential possession for a week following separation.

3. **Split Dispute Resolution and Fee-Shifting Regimes Across the Packet**
   * *Exact Text (Offer Letter, Section 12):* `"Governing Law. This offer and your employment are governed by the laws of the State of Georgia, without regard to its conflict-of-laws principles."` (Silent on arbitration, court venue, and attorney's fees).
   * *Exact Text (NDA, Section VIII):* `"If any controversies, claims, or disputes arising out of or relating to this Agreement cannot be resolved through negotiation, they will be resolved by binding arbitration under the rules of the American Arbitration Association."`
   * *Exact Text (NDA, Section XV):* `"If a legal suit, action, or proceeding, including arbitration, is brought by any party to enforce or to interpret any provision of this Agreement, the prevailing party will be entitled to recover, in addition to any other damages awarded, all costs associated with conducting the suit, action, proceeding, or arbitration and reasonable attorneys' fees."`
   * *The Contradiction:* While contained in separate legal instruments, they govern a unified employment relationship. If an employment dispute arises regarding unpaid wages, overtime, or termination notice under the Offer Letter, it is governed by ordinary Georgia court litigation (or administrative wage claims) with each party bearing their own legal fees under the American Rule. If a dispute touches confidentiality, student records, intellectual property, or non-circumvention, it is forced into private commercial AAA arbitration with mandatory English-rule prevailing party fee-shifting. Because IP and employment duties are intertwined, the documents subject the employee to conflicting procedural forums and financial liabilities.

#### C. Surprising Terms (Terms a Signer Might Not Expect)

1. **Reimbursement for Only "One-Way Mileage" and Total Exclusion of Trips Under 50 Miles**
   * *Exact Text:* `"When the one-way distance to an assigned site exceeds fifty miles, MedEdPrep will reimburse one-way mileage at the then-current IRS standard mileage rate for use of your personal vehicle."` (Offer Letter, Section 6)
   * *Why It Is Surprising:* An employee taking on an educational leadership role that includes mandatory travel to outreach and clinical sites would reasonably expect standard business travel reimbursement—meaning round-trip mileage for all work-related driving. Contractually restricting mileage reimbursement to only "one-way mileage," and completely excluding any reimbursement for travel to sites within 50 miles of the base (which could still entail a 90-mile round trip on the employee's dime), is highly unusual and economically unfavorable for a part-time hourly employee.

2. **Hourly Part-Time Employee Must Personally Indemnify the Employer**
   * *Exact Text:* `"XIV. Indemnification. The Recipient agrees to indemnify and hold the Disclosing Party harmless from all claims, losses, expenses, fees, including attorney fees, costs, and judgments that may be asserted against the Disclosing Party that result from the acts or omissions of the Recipient in breach of this Agreement."` (NDA, Section XIV)
   * *Why It Is Surprising:* Indemnification clauses requiring an individual worker to defend, hold harmless, and personally pay the legal judgments, corporate damages, and attorney's fees of their employer are standard in commercial business-to-business vendor contracts, but are extraordinarily aggressive in an employment packet for a $40/hour W-2 educator. Under this clause, if MedEdPrep is sued or incurs financial losses due to Gene's alleged breach of confidentiality, Gene is personally liable to cover the company's losses and legal defense costs.

3. **Explicit Contractual Prohibition Against Using Cloud-Based AI Tools**
   * *Exact Text:* `"However, the Recipient shall not upload, input, or otherwise provide any Confidential Information to any third-party, web-based, or cloud-hosted AI tool, platform, or system where such data may be stored, processed, or accessible beyond the Recipient's local environment, except tools approved in writing by the Disclosing Party."` (NDA, Section V)
   * *Why It Is Surprising:* While data security regarding proprietary exam questions and student records is standard, most employment agreements rely on general confidentiality clauses. Embedding an explicit technological restriction governing artificial intelligence usage—specifically distinguishing between locally run models and cloud-based AI tools—is an unusually modern and granular restriction that an educator drafting lesson plans might inadvertently violate if using tools like ChatGPT, Claude, or Grammarly to polish exam questions or lecture materials.

4. **Permitting Platform and System Access for Seven Days Post-Termination**
   * *Exact Text:* `"...cease all access and return or destroy any stored credentials within seven (7) days of the end of the Recipient's employment for any reason."` (NDA, Section IV(d)(v))
   * *Why It Is Surprising:* In educational and healthcare environments subject to state oversight and student data privacy laws (e.g., FERPA), industry standard is immediate revocation of system access, platform accounts, and credentials upon an employee's resignation or discharge. Contractually giving an ex-employee 7 days post-termination to voluntarily cease accessing internal systems and destroy credentials is an unexpected and irregular security practice.

5. **Binding AAA Commercial Arbitration and Fee-Shifting for an Hourly Employee**
   * *Exact Text:* `"If any controversies, claims, or disputes arising out of or relating to this Agreement cannot be resolved through negotiation, they will be resolved by binding arbitration under the rules of the American Arbitration Association."` (NDA, Section VIII) and `"the prevailing party will be entitled to recover, in addition to any other damages awarded, all costs associated with conducting the suit, action, proceeding, or arbitration and reasonable attorneys' fees."` (NDA, Section XV)
   * *Why It Is Surprising:* An hourly educator signing an employment offer letter would not typically expect that confidentiality and operational disputes are subject to binding commercial arbitration under the American Arbitration Association (where filing fees and arbitrator fees can cost thousands of dollars per day), coupled with a mandatory fee-shifting clause that requires the loser to pay the winner's attorneys' fees. This creates substantial financial exposure that could deter an hourly employee from defending legitimate rights.

MISREADING-RISK: MEDIUM
While the core job functions and the allocation of intellectual property are clearly structured, the packet presents a moderate risk of misreading because critical financial, operational, and liability terms—specifically the one-way-only travel reimbursement, the discrepancy regarding outside teaching permissions, the lack of defined minimum hours, and the individual employee indemnification obligation—deviate significantly from standard employment expectations and could easily be misunderstood by a casual reader.

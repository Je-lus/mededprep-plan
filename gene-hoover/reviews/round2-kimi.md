# Round 2 Kimi Readback - Gene Hoover Packet

This packet is MedEdPrep, LLC's offer to hire Gene Hoover as a part-time, hourly Director of Education at $40/hour, consisting of an offer letter (the controlling employment terms), a position description (the duties), and a Confidentiality and Non-Disclosure Agreement (a condition of employment that governs alongside the letter).

## 1. The deal in plain English

- **Job:** Director of Education at MedEdPrep, LLC, reporting directly to CEO Jeramey Ricks. The role combines teaching (didactic lectures, skills labs, scenario instruction, exam/content authoring) with program administration (regulatory compliance, student enrollment/progression/remediation, faculty and Medical Director coordination, clinical rotations) and occasional outreach travel to partner EMS agencies. Hoover also holds the "Program Director" designation for MedEdPrep's state-designated programs.
- **Pay:** $40.00 per hour for all hours worked, paid through regular W-2 payroll with normal withholdings. Overtime at 1.5x for hours over 40 in a workweek.
- **Schedule:** Part-time, flexible, hours set by mutual agreement. All time — instruction, prep, student/faculty communications, admin work — must be recorded in MedEdPrep's timekeeping system. No minimum or maximum weekly hours are stated anywhere.
- **Status:** Part-time, hourly, W-2 **employee** (not contractor), classified **non-exempt**, and explicitly **at-will** — either side can end it at any time, with or without cause or notice, and nothing creates a fixed-term contract. The NDA (§XII) repeats that nothing alters the at-will relationship.
- **Other logistics:** Primary location is the Carrollton, Georgia campus plus clinical/partner sites; some admin work may be remote. Start date is "mutually agreed" and "confirmed in writing" — no actual date is committed. Mileage reimbursement only kicks in past a 50-mile one-way threshold. Georgia law governs.

## 2. What the employee is committing to

- **Duties:** Everything in the position description (education delivery, program administration including owning regulatory compliance, outreach) plus a catch-all: "such other duties in support of the company's educational mission as the company may reasonably assign" (offer letter) / "other duties as needed at the direction of the company" (job description).
- **Timekeeping:** Record *all* work hours, including preparation and communications, in the company system.
- **Confidentiality:** Keep all proprietary materials, business information, and student records strictly confidential and use them only for the job (offer letter §10), plus the entire NDA, signing of which is a condition of employment. The NDA adds: no disclosure or copying/modifying except as the job requires; prompt reporting of any unauthorized use; credential hygiene rules (no sharing logins, access only what's needed, no unauthorized downloads, report breaches immediately).
- **AI tool restriction (NDA §V):** May use local AI tools and AI built into company platforms, but must not feed any Confidential Information (documents, recordings, exam items, student data) into third-party/cloud AI tools unless approved in writing.
- **IP assignment:** Everything he creates within the scope of employment — curricula, course materials, scenarios, exam items, recordings, other deliverables — is a work made for hire owned by MedEdPrep, and anything that doesn't qualify as work-for-hire is assigned to MedEdPrep anyway, with an obligation to sign further papers to perfect that assignment.
- **Outside work:** Expressly permitted to keep other professional roles and clinical practice, provided no conflict of interest and no interference with MedEdPrep duties; he must disclose any outside role that could reasonably create a conflict.
- **Non-circumvention (NDA §X):** During the agreement and for 12 months after, he cannot do business with or solicit business contacts found or referred through MedEdPrep in a way that would cut MedEdPrep out of profit or fees, without written approval. (There is no general non-compete and no employee/student non-solicit — this is a narrow anti-circumvention clause, and it expressly does not limit the outside employment the offer letter allows.)
- **Return of property:** On termination or earlier request, return all MedEdPrep data, materials, and equipment, and permanently delete copies from personal devices and accounts (offer letter §10; NDA §XI). Separately, cease all system access and return/destroy stored credentials within 7 days of employment ending (NDA §IV(d)).
- **Indemnification (NDA §XIV):** He personally indemnifies MedEdPrep against all claims, losses, and attorney fees resulting from his breach of the NDA.
- **Arbitration (NDA §VIII):** NDA disputes go to binding AAA arbitration after a negotiation attempt.

## 3. What the company is committing to

- $40/hour for all hours worked, through regular payroll; time-and-a-half overtime past 40 hours/week.
- Flexible scheduling by mutual agreement; outreach travel coordinated in advance with reasonable notice and regard for his teaching calendar.
- Mileage reimbursement at the IRS standard rate when a one-way trip to an assigned site exceeds 50 miles, plus reimbursement of reasonable, pre-approved out-of-pocket expenses.
- Specialized tools, software, or licenses needed for the work will be provided or their reasonable, pre-approved cost reimbursed.
- Nothing else. The packet contains **no benefits whatsoever** — no health insurance, no PTO/vacation/sick leave, no holidays, no retirement contribution, no sick pay, no severance, no guaranteed hours, no fixed term. The only post-employment "promise" running to Hoover is that his pre-existing and outside materials remain his (see next section).

## 4. Ownership of teaching materials

Reading all three documents together, the ownership scheme is:

- **Created before employment:** Remains Hoover's property, outright. The offer letter says so explicitly, the NDA's exceptions clause (§VI(iii)) excludes such materials from "Confidential Information," and the NDA's return clause (§XI) and IP clause (§XIII) both carve out materials he owns under the offer letter. **However:** if he incorporates any of his pre-existing material into a MedEdPrep deliverable, he automatically grants MedEdPrep a "non-exclusive, royalty-free, perpetual license to use, adapt, and deliver that material as part of its educational programs." He keeps ownership, but MedEdPrep keeps the right to use and adapt it forever, for free, even after he leaves.
- **Created during employment, within scope:** Owned by MedEdPrep as work made for hire, with a backup assignment clause for anything that fails the work-for-hire test. "Scope" is broad given the job includes authoring course content, scenarios, and exam items, plus catch-all assigned duties.
- **Created during employment but "wholly outside the scope":** Remains Hoover's (same offer-letter sentence covers pre-existing and wholly-outside materials together), subject to the same perpetual license if incorporated into MedEdPrep deliverables.
- **Created after employment ends:** Not addressed anywhere in the packet. Logically, once employment ends there is no "scope of employment," so new materials would be his — but nothing says this explicitly, and the perpetual license on anything he previously incorporated survives. Separately, the offer letter states that "nothing in this engagement restricts your use of the general skills, methods, and knowledge you develop," so his general expertise is not locked up.
- **One soft spot:** the line between "within scope" and "wholly outside the scope" is undefined, and because he is paid for preparation time, material he develops for his own teaching that resembles MedEdPrep content could be contested.

## 5. What happens when employment ends

- **Offer letter:** Employment is at-will — either side may end it at any time, with or without cause or notice (both sides merely "endeavor to provide reasonable notice"). Upon conclusion of employment, *or earlier upon MedEdPrep's request*, Hoover must return all MedEdPrep data and materials and remove copies from devices and accounts in his possession or control. The work-for-hire/assignment terms and the perpetual license on incorporated pre-existing material survive by their nature.
- **Job description:** Contains **no** termination or separation provisions at all — it is purely a duties/qualifications document. (Notable only in that the "owns regulatory compliance" responsibility has no stated handoff or wind-down obligation.)
- **NDA:** The agreement itself runs for the duration of employment. After employment ends "for any reason": (a) confidentiality obligations continue for **24 months** for ordinary Confidential Information; (b) for **trade secrets (expressly including examination items and item banks) and legally protected student records, the obligation continues indefinitely** — as long as the information remains a trade secret or legally protected; (c) all system access must cease and stored credentials must be returned or destroyed within **7 days**; (d) all Confidential Information and company property must be returned, and copies on personal devices/accounts permanently deleted, except materials Hoover owns under the offer letter; (e) the non-circumvention clause runs **12 months** past the end of the agreement; (f) the arbitration, indemnification, attorney's-fees, and injunctive-relief provisions continue to apply to any breach.

## 6. AMBIGUOUS / CONTRADICTORY / SURPRISING findings

### AMBIGUOUS

1. **One-way mileage reimbursement.** Offer letter: "When the one-way distance to an assigned site exceeds fifty miles, MedEdPrep will reimburse one-way mileage at the then-current IRS standard mileage rate." Read literally, a 120-mile round trip to a site 60 miles away gets only 60 miles reimbursed — the return leg is never covered. It could mean "mileage for each one-way trip" (i.e., round trips fully reimbursed) or "only one direction, ever." An ordinary signer would assume round-trip travel is covered; the text does not clearly say that.

2. **"Wholly outside the scope" of employment.** Offer letter: "Materials you created before your employment with MedEdPrep, or that you create wholly outside the scope of it, remain your property." The job's scope includes authoring course content, scenarios, and exam items plus "other duties... as the company may reasonably assign." If Hoover develops EMS teaching material on his own time for his own purposes, whether it is "wholly outside" a scope this broad is genuinely unclear — and the answer determines who owns it outright.

3. **Status of the curriculum Hoover already built.** The offer letter tells him "You have shaped our education from its first outline," implying substantial course material already exists that he created before this employment begins. The pre-existing-materials clause would make those his property (with MedEdPrep holding a perpetual license once incorporated), but nothing in the packet addresses what legal relationship he created them under previously, or confirms which specific existing materials fall on which side of the line. Both readings — "his, licensed to MedEdPrep" and "already MedEdPrep's" — are available from the text alone.

4. **Which disputes go to arbitration.** NDA §VIII requires binding AAA arbitration for disputes "arising out of or relating to this Agreement," but the offer letter has no arbitration clause at all. A pay or termination dispute under the offer letter presumably goes to court; a confidentiality dispute goes to arbitration; a dispute spanning both (e.g., IP ownership, which lives in the offer letter but is referenced throughout the NDA) could arguably be pulled either direction.

5. **Non-circumvention vs. permitted outside work.** NDA §X bars soliciting "any business contacts found or otherwise referred by the Disclosing Party" for 12 months post-employment, yet Hoover's outreach duties will put him in front of exactly those partner agencies, and the offer letter simultaneously blesses his outside professional roles. The NDA's carve-out ("Nothing in this section limits the outside employment, clinical practice, and teaching permitted by the Recipient's offer letter") helps, but where "permitted outside teaching" ends and "circumventing MedEdPrep with its own referred contacts" begins is not defined.

### CONTRADICTORY

1. **Duration of post-employment confidentiality duties.** The offer letter says that "upon conclusion of your employment" Hoover will "return all MedEdPrep data and materials," with the NDA "including its survival provisions" governing alongside it — but the NDA (§III) keeps non-trade-secret Confidential Information protected for twenty-four (24) months *after* employment ends, while also (§XI) requiring all copies on personal devices be "promptly and permanently deleted" at the end of employment. These are reconcilable (delete the materials, keep the knowledge confidential), but a signer could read the offer letter's clean "return everything at the end" as ending his confidentiality exposure at departure, when the NDA plainly extends it two years — and indefinitely for exam items and student records.

2. Otherwise, the documents are notably careful to defer to each other (the offer letter says the NDA controls on confidentiality conflicts; NDA §§VI, XI, and XIII all carve out the offer letter's ownership terms), so no other direct conflicts found.

### SURPRISING

1. **Personal indemnification by a part-time hourly employee.** NDA §XIV: "The Recipient agrees to indemnify and hold the Disclosing Party harmless from all claims, losses, expenses, fees, including attorney fees, costs, and judgments that may be asserted against the Disclosing Party that result from the acts or omissions of the Recipient in breach of this Agreement." A $40/hour part-timer personally guaranteeing the company's legal costs and judgments is a heavy, one-way commitment that many signers would not expect in an employment NDA.

2. **Binding arbitration and prevailing-party attorney's fees.** NDA §VIII commits disputes to "binding arbitration under the rules of the American Arbitration Association" with a "final" decision, and §XV lets the prevailing party recover "all costs... and reasonable attorneys' fees." Combined with §XIV, an ordinary signer may not realize they are waiving court access for NDA disputes and taking on meaningful fee exposure.

3. **No benefits of any kind for a "Director" role.** The packet promises pay, mileage, expenses, and equipment — and nothing else. No health insurance, PTO, holidays, retirement, severance, or guaranteed hours appear in any of the three documents. For a position titled Director of Education that "owns" regulatory compliance, a signer expecting even minimal benefits will find none promised.

4. **Indefinite confidentiality for exam items.** NDA §III: for "information constituting a trade secret (including examination items and item banks) and for student records protected by law, this obligation continues for as long as the information remains a trade secret or legally protected" — i.e., potentially forever, with no time limit, unlike the 24-month tail on everything else.

5. **A detailed AI-usage clause.** NDA §V forbids putting any Confidential Information into "any third-party, web-based, or cloud-hosted AI tool" absent written approval, while expressly permitting local AI tools. This is unusually specific for a small education company and would surprise a signer who casually uses consumer AI tools for lesson prep.

6. **Automatic perpetual license on his own pre-existing materials.** Offer letter §11: merely by incorporating anything he previously created into a MedEdPrep deliverable, he grants "a non-exclusive, royalty-free, perpetual license to use, adapt, and deliver that material" — irrevocable and surviving his departure. A signer may not expect that folding his own life's-work materials into courses hands the company a forever-license to them.

MISREADING-RISK: MEDIUM - The documents are unusually well cross-referenced and internally consistent, but the undefined "scope of employment" boundary, the literal one-way-mileage wording, and the silent treatment of the curriculum Hoover already built leave real room for a signer to misunderstand who owns what and what travel is reimbursed.

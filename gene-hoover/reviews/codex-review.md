# Codex Review - Gene Hoover Offer + JD

These documents present a credible, respectful offer for a hands-on education leader, and their core terms agree. I would send them after targeted revisions addressing workload boundaries, paid time and travel, and the interaction of confidentiality, existing teaching materials, and personal devices.

## Coherence

The role hangs together: company-wide education delivery, administration and compliance for the programs under this position’s direction, and the state Program Director designation are complementary responsibilities. Delivering continuing education does not itself assign ownership of its accreditation. Keep the deliberate compliance boundary intact.

There are **no direct factual mismatches** in title, CEO reporting line, part-time/hourly status, campus and travel expectations, or Program Director designation. The JD expressly says non-exempt; the offer does too. The JD’s additional detail—enrollment, remediation, clinical scheduling, pre-work review, recordings, and Medical Director coordination—reasonably elaborates the offer’s categories rather than contradicting them. Remote administration appears only in the offer, which is compatible with the JD’s campus location.

There are two meaningful differences in emphasis. The offer protects the primary teaching calendar and promises reasonable outreach notice, while the JD only requires advance coordination (fix W3). The offer’s catch-all is limited by “reasonably,” while the JD’s catch-all is not; both need a clearer connection to the role and agreed working time (W1). Neither difference makes the documents irreconcilable, but consistent wording would prevent the broader JD from becoming the operating rule.

The major missing connection is between responsibility and capacity: who sets priorities when teaching, student issues, clinical placements, and regulatory deadlines compete, and what authority accompanies “owns”? Address that directly without changing the title, rate, or employment model (W1 and W2).

## Candidate Read

The opening and closing are unusually personal in a good way. They recognize Gene’s contribution and professional standing rather than treating him as a replaceable instructor. “Building it with you for a long time” reads as an aspiration, not a tenure promise in the face of the explicit at-will clause; I would keep it. A flexible schedule by mutual agreement and recognition of outside clinical practice are also respectful.

An experienced educator will nevertheless see a substantial operational job behind the title: teaching, preparation, assessment, remediation, faculty coordination, clinical logistics, compliance, and outreach. The likely question is whether he can actually fit the work into agreed paid hours and obtain help when he cannot. “Owns” without a resource/escalation arrangement can sound like accountability for matters he cannot control (W1–W2).

The most personal points of friction are the treatment of teaching materials he already owns, an NDA he is asked to sign but cannot evaluate from these two documents, and a personal-device requirement with no implementation support described (W5–W8). Those deserve clarification precisely because he arrives with substantial expertise and other professional roles.

The qualifications section also feels less considered than the rest: “Instructor credentials” is undefined, while generic experience bullets say little about the critical-care teaching competence the letter praises. This is a precision issue, not evidence that Gene lacks qualifications or that the state designation is improper. Identify the credentials actually required for his assigned programs rather than inventing additional requirements (W10).

## Legal/HR Red Flags

This is a drafting and issue-spotting review, not a legal opinion on enforceability. I reviewed the two supplied documents, not the referenced NDA, company policies, or program approvals.

**Paid time: the promise is sound; the operating instructions are incomplete (W2–W4).** The offer correctly uses the regular rate and a forty-hour workweek, and does not condition overtime payment on approval. Preserve that. Explicitly include preparation, grading, student communications, administration, required meetings, and compensable travel in time reporting. A flexible schedule must not become unpaid evening email or “director availability.” Work the employer permits remains payable even without advance approval. Travel reimbursement pays expenses; it does not pay for time. Ordinary commuting and business travel have different rules, so avoid promising either that all travel is paid or that only classroom hours count. See [DOL, Hours Worked](https://www.dol.gov/agencies/whd/fact-sheets/22-flsa-hours-worked).

**Expenses: clarify mechanics, not an invented legal entitlement (W4).** The IRS mileage reference is a useful contractual measure, not a universal legal reimbursement mandate. Specify the business mileage rate applicable when the trip occurs, distinguish mileage from parking/tolls and other approved expenses, and give a reimbursement deadline. The proposed thirty-day deadline below is a business recommendation, not a claim about Georgia law. Required business costs cannot be shifted to the employee in a way that cuts into legally required minimum wages or overtime. See [DOL, Deductions for Employer Expenses](https://www.dol.gov/agencies/whd/fact-sheets/16-flsa-wage-deductions).

**Confidentiality and the unseen NDA are the strongest legal drafting concern (W5–W6).** “All” business information, “strictly confidential,” and employment-only use are broader than necessary without exceptions for public information and legally protected disclosures. Covered employees have rights to discuss working conditions; whether Gene is an excluded supervisor depends on actual authority, not this title. See [NLRB, Wage Discussions](https://www.nlrb.gov/about-nlrb/rights-we-protect/your-rights/your-rights-to-discuss-wages) and [NLRA, employee and supervisor definitions](https://www.nlrb.gov/guidance/key-reference-materials/national-labor-relations-act).

The offer also lacks the federal trade-secret whistleblower immunity notice. It may appear in the NDA, but that cannot be assumed; omission can cost the employer certain federal exemplary-damages and attorney-fee remedies, rather than automatically invalidating the NDA. W5 supplies notice. See [18 U.S.C. § 1833(b)](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/18/1833).

The NDA priority clause is already confined to confidentiality; it does **not** expressly supersede every employment term. The narrower concern is that conflicting definitions could swallow the offer’s retained general knowledge or the proposed pre-existing-material carve-out. Provide the actual NDA with the offer and make the hierarchy explicit. Do not treat a revised offer as curing conflicting NDA text without comparing both. “Return or permanently destroy” also lets the employee appear to choose destruction of the only copy and promises more than a person may control in backups; use a company-directed transfer/removal process subject to retention obligations (W6).

**IP assignment is generally proportionate, with a material boundary gap (W7).** It is limited to work created within employment scope, uses a present assignment as backup, and preserves general knowledge. Those are strengths. Employee work within scope can be work made for hire; the label alone does not settle disputed ownership. See [U.S. Copyright Office, Works Made for Hire](https://www.copyright.gov/circs/circ30.pdf). Gene’s earlier contribution makes it especially useful to distinguish company-owned work from materials he independently owns and brings into this role. Do not imply that every pre-start-date work belongs to Gene or that an unpaid/off-clock creation automatically falls outside employment scope. Address embedded material by an agreed license, and keep third-party rights intact.

**Outside activities are a clarification issue, not an obvious unlawful noncompete (W9).** The current text affirmatively permits other work, requires disclosure rather than blanket prior consent, and contains no express post-employment restraint. Its weakness is the undefined “conflict,” which could later be read to prohibit the very teaching or clinical relationships being accommodated. Define concrete conflicts and use a discussion/resolution process. Enforceability would depend on the actual restriction and facts; these documents do not justify declaring it either categorically enforceable or void.

**BYOD security is reasonable but incomplete (W8).** Password protection and encryption are appropriate conditions of access. The clause does not itself authorize company inspection or full-device wiping, and should not be treated as such consent. Explain the supported setup, approved storage, incident reporting, and company-device alternative if a personal device cannot meet the requirements. Pair this with the narrower return/removal language in W6; a short offer cannot substitute for an actual device policy.

**At-will wording is essentially sound (W11 is optional polish).** The no-fixed-period sentence, termination without cause or notice, and mutual notice aspiration fit together. Georgia recognizes at-will employment, subject to applicable legal limits; “at will” never authorizes unlawful discrimination or retaliation. See [Georgia DOL, Employer Handbook, p. 43](https://dol.georgia.gov/media/18666/download). Clarifying that reasonable notice is a courtesy removes a minor ambiguity without stripping out the respectful tone. The Georgia governing-law sentence does not displace applicable federal law and needs no rewrite.

The offer need not become a handbook. Give Gene the payroll schedule, fixed workweek boundaries, time-entry instructions, and applicable reimbursement/security policies during onboarding; W2 makes that delivery explicit without inventing company practices. The two deliberate dates simply need completion before sending. No change to the fixed employment model or deliberate benefits omission is warranted.

## Director-Title Tension

“Director” and hourly/non-exempt are compatible. A title does not determine exempt status, so the overtime clause is a strength, not an embarrassing contradiction to hide. See [DOL, Exemption Fact Sheet](https://www.dol.gov/agencies/whd/fact-sheets/17a-overtime). There is no reason to recast the agreed part-time arrangement.

The friction is operational and perceptual: “owns” company-wide delivery can imply continuous availability and authority over people, budgets, and priorities, while the actual terms supply flexible agreed hours and a broad list of hands-on tasks. The documents handle this halfway well: outreach is secondary, advance coordination is promised, and other professional roles are acknowledged. They do not yet explain how work gets reduced, reassigned, or rescheduled when capacity runs out.

The two catch-alls compound that uncertainty. They do not legally turn a non-exempt employee into an unpaid executive or defeat the compensation clause; they do make almost any education-related task seem assignable. W1 ties both to the role and schedule. W2 creates an explicit priority/resource discussion and avoids an implied on-call obligation, while preserving the Program Director responsibilities already accepted. W3 makes the JD’s outreach limits match the letter. This lets “Director” describe real educational leadership without suggesting unlimited labor or promising authority the company has not delegated.

## Wording Improvements

The quotations below reproduce the source prose, preserving its words and punctuation while joining HTML line wraps and decoding entities. Replacements are proposed terms, not statements that these policies already exist. W1–W9 address substantive concerns; W10 is JD precision and W11 optional polish.

### W1 — Align and bound both catch-all duties clauses

**Offer, Position — current:**

> You will also perform such other duties as the company may reasonably assign from time to time.

**Replace with:**

> You may also be assigned reasonable duties consistent with this education leadership role. Additional assignments will be prioritized with the Chief Executive Officer within your mutually agreed working schedule; if the work requires additional hours or support, those needs will be addressed before the assignment proceeds, without limiting payment for all hours worked.

**JD, General — current:**

> Perform other duties as needed at the direction of the company in support of its educational mission.

**Replace with:**

> Perform reasonable additional duties consistent with this education leadership role, prioritized with the Chief Executive Officer within the mutually agreed working schedule and subject to the offer letter’s provisions on additional hours and support.

### W2 — Make paid work, capacity, and authority explicit

**Offer, Compensation — current:**

> You will be paid $40.00 per hour for all hours worked, through MedEdPrep's regular payroll, less applicable tax withholdings. This position is classified as non-exempt; you will be paid overtime at one and one-half times your regular rate for hours worked over forty in a workweek. Your hours will be flexible and scheduled by mutual agreement.

**Replace with:**

> You will be paid $40.00 per hour for all hours worked, through MedEdPrep's regular payroll, less applicable tax withholdings. This position is classified as non-exempt; you will be paid overtime at one and one-half times your regular rate for hours worked over forty in a workweek. Record all work time, including preparation, grading, student and faculty communications, administration, required meetings, and compensable travel. Your hours will be flexible and scheduled by mutual agreement. You and the Chief Executive Officer will agree on anticipated hours, priorities, and any scheduled availability, and review them as program needs change. Seek advance approval for hours beyond the agreed schedule; all hours worked must nevertheless be reported and will be paid. The title does not create an expectation of continuous availability. MedEdPrep will provide its payroll schedule, workweek boundaries, and time-reporting procedures before your first workday.

**JD, Purpose — current:**

> The Director of Education owns the delivery of MedEdPrep education across the company's programs and the administration that keeps the programs under this position's direction in good standing with their regulatory bodies. This position holds the Program Director designation for MedEdPrep's state-designated programs and represents MedEdPrep education to partner agencies in the field.

**Replace with:**

> The Director of Education leads the delivery of MedEdPrep education across the company's programs and is responsible for administration and regulatory compliance for the programs under this position's direction. This position holds the Program Director designation for MedEdPrep's state-designated programs and represents MedEdPrep education to partner agencies in the field. The Director and Chief Executive Officer will document decision authority and available support for faculty coordination, student decisions, and clinical partnerships, consistent with applicable program requirements. When workload or resources threaten required deadlines or educational quality, the Director will promptly raise the issue with the Chief Executive Officer to resolve priorities, staffing, or scheduling; this process does not reduce the position’s Program Director responsibilities.

This retains the scoped compliance duty and state designation; it does not transfer either elsewhere or invent unilateral hiring or budget authority.

### W3 — Carry the offer’s outreach protections into the JD

**JD, Secondary Responsibilities: Outreach Education — current:**

> When coordinated in advance, travel to partner EMS agencies and facilities to deliver on-site training, continuing education, and program presentations on MedEdPrep's behalf.

**Replace with:**

> When coordinated with you in advance, travel to partner EMS agencies and facilities to deliver on-site training, continuing education, and program presentations on MedEdPrep's behalf, with reasonable notice and regard to your primary teaching calendar and mutually agreed working schedule. Record compensable travel and outreach work time in accordance with the offer letter.

The offer’s existing Outreach Duties paragraph can stay.

### W4 — Separate travel wages from expense reimbursement

**Offer, Travel & Expenses — current:**

> Pre-approved travel for outreach assignments and program business will be reimbursed at the then-current IRS standard mileage rate for use of your personal vehicle, together with reasonable, pre-approved out-of-pocket expenses, upon submission of records through MedEdPrep's internal portal.

**Replace with:**

> MedEdPrep will reimburse pre-approved business mileage in your personal vehicle at the IRS standard business mileage rate in effect on the date of travel, plus reasonable, pre-approved parking, tolls, lodging, meals, and other necessary business expenses. Ordinary commuting mileage is excluded unless separately approved. Submit mileage records and expense documentation through MedEdPrep's internal portal; approved expenses will be reimbursed within thirty days after complete documentation is submitted. Necessary expenses that could not reasonably be approved in advance will be reviewed promptly. Separately from reimbursement, compensable travel time will be paid under the Compensation clause and applicable law; ordinary commuting time is generally excluded. Before an outreach assignment, MedEdPrep will clarify how to record the trip’s compensable travel time. Required business expenses will be handled in a manner that preserves legally required minimum wages and overtime pay.

### W5 — Narrow confidentiality and add protected-disclosure language

**Offer, Confidentiality & Student Records, first paragraph — current:**

> You agree to keep all MedEdPrep proprietary materials, business information, and student records and education data strictly confidential; to use them solely for the purposes of your employment; and to handle student records in accordance with applicable law and program policy.

**Replace with:**

> You agree to protect MedEdPrep’s nonpublic proprietary materials and business information and to use them only for authorized work. Information lawfully public or lawfully obtained from an independent source without a duty of confidentiality is excluded. Student records and education data must be accessed, used, and disclosed only as authorized by applicable law and program policy. Nothing in this letter or the NDA restricts lawful reporting to government agencies, cooperation with investigations, obtaining legal advice, disclosures required by law, or legally protected discussion of wages or working conditions; no prior company approval is required for protected disclosures. Under 18 U.S.C. § 1833(b), you have immunity from federal and state trade-secret liability for confidential disclosures to a government official or attorney solely to report or investigate suspected legal violations, and for disclosures in filings made under seal. In an employer-retaliation lawsuit for reporting a suspected violation, you may disclose trade secrets to your attorney and use them in the proceeding if filings containing them are under seal and further disclosure occurs only by court order.

The immunity sentences implement the [statutory notice discussed above](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/18/1833); they do not authorize unlawful access to information.

### W6 — Limit NDA priority and make departure handling workable

**Offer, Confidentiality & Student Records, subclause — current:**

> As a condition of employment, you will execute MedEdPrep's Confidentiality and Non-Disclosure Agreement (the “NDA”). The NDA, including its survival provisions, governs alongside this letter; in the event of any conflict regarding confidentiality, the NDA controls. Upon conclusion of your employment, or earlier upon MedEdPrep's request, you will return or permanently destroy all MedEdPrep data, materials, and copies in your possession or control.

**Replace with:**

> As a condition of employment, you will execute MedEdPrep’s Confidentiality and Non-Disclosure Agreement (the “NDA”), provided with this offer for your review. The NDA, including its survival provisions, governs alongside this letter and controls conflicts solely regarding confidentiality, except that this letter’s protected-disclosure provisions and express exclusions from intellectual-property assignment control over inconsistent NDA terms. The NDA does not modify compensation, employment status, or the permission for outside activities stated here. At MedEdPrep’s request or when employment ends, promptly transfer company records to the designated company repository and return company property. Following that transfer, remove company information from personal devices and accounts as MedEdPrep reasonably directs, subject to legal preservation requirements. MedEdPrep will coordinate removal from backups that cannot reasonably be selectively deleted. You may retain your employment and pay records and materials you lawfully own, without retaining protected student records or unrelated company confidential information.

Use the “provided with this offer” sentence only when actually enclosing the NDA, and reconcile the NDA itself with these terms. Retention of existing personal materials does not authorize taking company-developed course content.

### W7 — Preserve existing IP without giving away company-created work

**Offer, Work Product & Intellectual Property — current:**

> All curricula, course materials, scenarios, examination items, recordings, and other deliverables you create within the scope of your employment are works made for hire and the property of MedEdPrep, LLC. To the extent any of it does not qualify as a work made for hire, you hereby assign all right, title, and interest in it to MedEdPrep, LLC, and agree to sign any documents reasonably necessary to perfect that assignment. Nothing in this engagement restricts your use of the general skills, methods, and knowledge you develop.

**Replace with:**

> All curricula, course materials, scenarios, examination items, recordings, and other deliverables you create within the scope of your employment are the property of MedEdPrep, LLC and, to the extent applicable, works made for hire. To the extent they do not qualify as works made for hire, you hereby assign your right, title, and interest in them to MedEdPrep, LLC and agree to sign documents reasonably necessary to confirm that assignment. This assignment excludes materials you independently own before this employment and materials you independently develop outside its scope; it does not alter any ownership or license rights MedEdPrep already holds. Before incorporating your retained materials into company deliverables, identify them and agree in writing on the license MedEdPrep needs to use, reproduce, adapt, and distribute them as part of those deliverables. Incorporation does not itself transfer ownership of the underlying retained materials. Third-party materials may be used only with appropriate permission or other lawful authority. Nothing in this employment restricts your use of general skills, methods, and knowledge, subject to valid confidentiality obligations that do not extend to those general skills, methods, and knowledge.

The licensing step matters: a carve-out alone could leave the company unable to keep using a course after departure. This also avoids pretending the new offer resolves ownership of everything Gene previously helped build.

### W8 — Make the personal-device requirement implementable

**Offer, Equipment — current:**

> Where specialized tools, software, or licenses are needed to perform the work, MedEdPrep will provide them or reimburse reasonable, pre-approved costs. Any device you use for MedEdPrep work must be password-protected and encrypted.

**Replace with:**

> Where specialized tools, software, or licenses are needed to perform the work, MedEdPrep will provide them or reimburse reasonable, pre-approved costs. Devices used for MedEdPrep work must be password-protected and encrypted under written security instructions supplied by MedEdPrep. Company and student information must be stored and shared only through approved systems. MedEdPrep will provide setup support and a compliant company device or alternative access arrangement if your personal device cannot meet these requirements. Promptly report loss, suspected unauthorized access, or other security incidents. Any company access to or removal of information from a personal device will follow a separately disclosed and agreed process limited to company information, subject to applicable law; this clause does not authorize inspection or deletion of unrelated personal information.

### W9 — Define conflicts without withdrawing permission for other work

**Offer, Outside Activities — current:**

> We recognize you maintain other professional roles and clinical practice. You may continue outside employment and clinical work that does not create a conflict of interest with MedEdPrep or interfere with your duties under this letter; you agree to disclose any outside role that could reasonably create such a conflict.

**Replace with:**

> We recognize you maintain other professional roles and clinical practice. You may continue outside employment, teaching, and clinical work consistent with your agreed MedEdPrep schedule and responsibilities. Disclose outside roles that could reasonably create a material conflict, such as overlapping commitments during agreed work hours, obligations to use or disclose another organization’s confidential information, or a financial interest that could compromise decisions you make for MedEdPrep. Work in the same professional field does not by itself establish a conflict. We will discuss disclosed conflicts promptly and document reasonable measures to resolve them. This clause imposes no restriction on outside work after your employment ends; valid confidentiality and intellectual-property obligations remain applicable.

**Current:**

These are proposed descriptions of relevant experience, not newly asserted state minimums. Keep the degree, certification, and Georgia Program Director eligibility bullets as written; verify the actual credential list operationally rather than adding unverified regulatory detail here.

### W11 — Clarify the notice courtesy (optional)

**Offer, Employment Status — current:**

> Your employment with MedEdPrep is at-will, which means that either you or MedEdPrep may end the employment relationship at any time, with or without cause and with or without notice, although we will each endeavor to provide reasonable notice. Nothing in this letter creates a contract of employment for any fixed period.

**Replace with:**

> Your employment with MedEdPrep is at-will, which means that either you or MedEdPrep may end the employment relationship at any time, with or without cause and with or without notice, subject to applicable law. We each intend to provide reasonable notice when practicable as a professional courtesy; notice is not a condition of ending employment. Nothing in this letter creates a contract of employment for any fixed period.

**VERDICT: SHIP-WITH-TWEAKS**

1. Define workload priorities, decision authority, and escalation when available hours or support are insufficient, and align both catch-all duties clauses.
2. Make time reporting, paid preparation and communications, compensable travel, and expense reimbursement explicit while preserving the existing overtime promise.
3. Reconcile the actual NDA with protected-disclosure language, retained teaching-material rights, and a workable process for company information on personal devices.

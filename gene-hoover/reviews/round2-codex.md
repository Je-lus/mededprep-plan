# Round 2 Codex Readback - Gene Hoover Packet

This is a cold-read comprehension check of the three-document employment packet, based only on its contents. It is not a legal review or a proposal to edit the documents.

## 1. The deal in plain English

Gene is being hired by MedEdPrep, LLC as Director of Education, reporting to CEO Jeramey Ricks. He will lead teaching and program administration and hold the Program Director designation for the company's state-designated programs. This is a part-time, hourly, non-exempt W-2 employee position, not a contractor engagement or a salaried executive position.

Pay is $40 per hour for all work, including preparation, communications, and administration, with hours recorded in the company's timekeeping system. Overtime is one and one-half times the regular rate for hours over 40 in a workweek ($60 per hour at the stated regular rate). Payroll deductions apply. The packet gives no guaranteed weekly hours, fixed weekly schedule, or annual earnings commitment. Hours are flexible and scheduled by mutual agreement; the start date is also to be mutually agreed and confirmed in writing.

The primary location is the Carrollton, Georgia campus, with travel to clinical affiliates and partner agencies. Applicable administrative work can be remote. Outreach is coordinated in advance, with reasonable notice and regard for the primary teaching calendar. Employment is at-will: either side can end it at any time, without cause or notice. Both sides say they will endeavor to give reasonable notice, but there is no fixed employment term or required notice interval. Signing the NDA is a condition of employment.


## 2. What the employee is committing to

- **Education and administration:** Teach classroom, case-based, scenario, and skills-lab sessions; design lab stations and evaluations; author and review content and examination items; review student pre-work and assessments; adapt instruction; and support recordings and released summaries so each session yields student study material. Manage program compliance, designation requirements, records and reports, enrollment, progression, remediation, completion decisions, faculty and preceptor coordination, Medical Director review, clinical rotations, partnerships, scheduling, and clinical-hour tracking. Represent MedEdPrep during outreach and identify opportunities for follow-up. The offer also allows reasonably assigned duties supporting the educational mission. The job description lists degree, certifications, instructor credentials, experience, and Georgia program-director eligibility requirements.
- **Timekeeping and security:** Record all work, protect and encrypt any device used for company work, keep credentials private, limit system access to what duties require, and download/export/copy data only as authorized. Report possible unauthorized use or disclosure promptly; report security breaches immediately and unauthorized releases or other NDA breaches immediately in writing.
- **Confidentiality:** Use proprietary materials, business information, and student data for employment purposes, handle student records under applicable law and program policy, and avoid disclosure or copying/modification unless job duties require it or the company gives prior written consent. Covered information can be oral, observed, or unmarked if reasonably recognizable as confidential. NDA exceptions include company-disclosed public information, information from a third party without a confidentiality obligation, independently created information, disclosures by operation of law, and information both parties exclude in writing. The NDA expressly preserves specified confidential reporting of suspected legal violations and sealed lawsuit disclosures.
- **AI use:** Local AI and company-integrated AI are permitted for work, but providing confidential information to external/cloud AI where it goes beyond the local environment requires written company approval. General, non-specific discussions are permitted.
- **Intellectual property:** Assign employment-scope deliverables to MedEdPrep and sign reasonably necessary assignment paperwork. Retain ownership of pre-existing and wholly outside-scope materials, but grant a perpetual license when incorporating them into company deliverables. Section 4 explains the ownership boundaries.
- **Outside work and solicitation:** Other employment and clinical work may continue if they do not conflict with MedEdPrep or interfere with duties; potentially conflicting roles must be disclosed. There is no general non-compete or blanket employee/customer non-solicit. There is a narrower non-circumvention restriction during the NDA term and for 12 months afterward: without specific written approval, Gene must not pursue company-found or referred business contacts to bypass MedEdPrep and deprive it of profits or fees. It expressly preserves outside employment, clinical practice, and teaching permitted by the offer.
- **Return and financial/dispute obligations:** Return company data, materials, confidential information, and relevant company property, including equipment, at departure or earlier request; remove copies and extracts from devices/accounts. Gene-owned materials are expressly excepted by the NDA. Stored credentials must be returned or destroyed and access ceased within seven days of departure. Gene also accepts breach-related indemnification, negotiation followed by binding arbitration of NDA disputes, and prevailing-party costs and reasonable attorney fees.

## 3. What the company is committing to

MedEdPrep commits to the stated hourly pay for all work and overtime over 40 hours, through regular payroll. It agrees to mutually scheduled flexible hours and a mutually agreed start date confirmed in writing. Outreach will be coordinated with Gene in advance, scheduled with reasonable notice, and take account of his primary teaching calendar.

For an assigned site more than 50 miles away one way, the offer promises reimbursement of **one-way mileage** at the then-current IRS standard mileage rate when Gene uses his own vehicle. It also promises reimbursement of reasonable, pre-approved out-of-pocket expenses, with records submitted through the internal portal. The mileage promise does not say round-trip reimbursement. Needed specialized tools, software, and licenses will be provided or their reasonable, pre-approved costs reimbursed.

The packet promises no health insurance, retirement contribution, paid leave, bonus, severance, guaranteed hours, or minimum duration of employment. That is an absence of a promise in these documents, not a finding about entitlements from any other source. The statement about looking forward to building together for a long time does not override express at-will employment. MedEdPrep promises to endeavor to provide reasonable termination notice but expressly retains the ability to terminate without notice.

The company recognizes Gene's retained ownership of pre-existing and outside-scope materials and unrestricted use of general skills, methods, and knowledge. The NDA requires both sides to attempt negotiation, use binding arbitration for unresolved NDA disputes, and continue their respective NDA obligations during arbitration. Its prevailing-party fee provision can benefit either side. NDA changes require both parties' written signatures. The NDA imposes no matching general promise that MedEdPrep will keep Gene's own information confidential; its operative protection obligations run from Gene to MedEdPrep.

## 4. Ownership of teaching materials

**Before employment:** Materials Gene created before his employment with MedEdPrep remain his. Bringing a lesson, curriculum, or course into the job does not, by itself, assign its ownership. The controlling offer-letter language, under “Work Product & Intellectual Property,” is:

> Materials you created before your employment with MedEdPrep, or that you create wholly outside the scope of it, remain your property; if you incorporate any such material into a MedEdPrep deliverable, you grant MedEdPrep a non-exclusive, royalty-free, perpetual license to use, adapt, and deliver that material as part of its educational programs.

Thus, incorporating his own materials into a company deliverable lets MedEdPrep use, adapt, and teach with them in its educational programs indefinitely without royalties. “Non-exclusive” means Gene does not surrender his own ability to use those materials or license them elsewhere. The license is tied to incorporation and to the company's educational programs; I do not read it as a blanket transfer of everything Gene brings with him. The packet does not identify particular existing works or establish their creation history, so it cannot tell me which actual files fall into this category.

**During employment:** Employment-scope teaching materials belong to MedEdPrep, even if Gene personally authors them. The offer states:

> All curricula, course materials, scenarios, examination items, recordings, and other deliverables you create within the scope of your employment are works made for hire and the property of MedEdPrep, LLC. To the extent any of it does not qualify as a work made for hire, you hereby assign all right, title, and interest in it to MedEdPrep, LLC, and agree to sign any documents reasonably necessary to perfect that assignment.

The distinction is the scope of employment, not simply when or where the work was done. Outside materials must be created **wholly** outside that scope to remain Gene's under the quoted reservation. A combined company deliverable can therefore contain company-owned new work and Gene-owned underlying material that the company licenses. Adaptations made as employment deliverables fall under the company-work rule while the underlying reserved material remains Gene's. The packet does not give a specific allocation for a particular mixed work.

The NDA's Section XIII confirms that contributing ideas does not give Gene ownership of company confidential material:

> The Recipient acknowledges that, as between the Disclosing Party and the Recipient, the Confidential Information and all related copyrights and other intellectual property rights are (and at all times will be) the property of the Disclosing Party, even if suggestions, comments, and/or ideas made by the Recipient are incorporated into the Confidential Information or related materials during the period of this Agreement.

But its express reservation prevents that statement from swallowing Gene's retained materials:

> Nothing in this section alters the ownership of, or the license to, the Recipient's pre-existing and outside materials as described in the Work Product & Intellectual Property provision of the Recipient's offer letter.

**After employment:** Departure does not transfer company-owned teaching materials back to Gene. Nor does it end the expressly “perpetual” company license to Gene-owned material already incorporated into company deliverables. Gene keeps his own reserved materials, and NDA Section XI expressly says:

> Nothing in this section requires the Recipient to delete or surrender materials the Recipient owns as described in the Recipient's offer letter.

He must still return company materials and delete confidential company copies; keeping his own underlying material does not entitle him to retain a whole company-owned deliverable containing it. The offer also states:

> Nothing in this engagement restricts your use of the general skills, methods, and knowledge you develop.

I therefore understand that Gene can continue teaching using his own materials and general expertise, subject to the specific confidentiality and non-circumvention obligations. Expiration of ordinary confidentiality after 24 months is not an ownership transfer or permission to reuse company-owned curricula, recordings, or examinations.

## 5. What happens when employment ends

**Offer letter:** Its Employment Status provision expressly permits either party to end employment at any time, with or without cause and with or without notice. Both will endeavor to provide reasonable notice, but there is no fixed notice period, required cause, fixed term, or severance promise. Its Confidentiality & Student Records provision requires return of all MedEdPrep data and materials and removal of copies from devices and accounts under Gene's possession or control at departure, or earlier company request. It says the NDA's survival provisions operate alongside the letter and the NDA controls a confidentiality conflict. The IP assignment contains no reversion at termination, and the license to incorporated Gene-owned material is perpetual.

**Job description:** It says nothing about termination, at-will status, required notice, severance, or surviving obligations. It describes the role, responsibilities, qualifications, and part-time/hourly/non-exempt status. It adds no separate exit process.

**NDA:** Section XII expressly preserves at-will employment and disclaims a fixed term. Section III states:

> The term of this Agreement will begin on the Effective Date and shall remain in effect for the duration of the Recipient's employment with MedEdPrep. For twenty-four (24) months after the Recipient's employment ends for any reason, the Recipient must continue to protect the Confidential Information that was received during the term of this Agreement from unauthorized use or disclosure; for information constituting a trade secret (including examination items and item banks) and for student records protected by law, this obligation continues for as long as the information remains a trade secret or legally protected.

Ordinary covered information therefore has a 24-month post-employment protection period; trade secrets and legally protected student records can require longer protection while their protected status lasts. The packet itself does not establish the legal status of every particular examination item. The NDA begins September 21, 2026, even though the employment start date is not yet set.

Section X continues non-circumvention for 12 months after the agreement's term ends, which I read with Section III as 12 months after employment ends. Section XI requires return of confidential information and company property upon departure or written request, and prompt permanent deletion of confidential copies or extracts on personal devices/accounts, with an express exception for Gene-owned materials. Section IV(d) gives a seven-day deadline to cease all access and return or destroy stored credentials; I do not understand this as permission to use company systems for personal purposes after departure, because access is limited to job duties.

Company ownership remains under Section XIII, which says “at all times,” subject to Gene's reserved materials. The NDA also provides injunctions and damages, indemnification for breach, arbitration, and prevailing-party fees. These explain the consequences and dispute process for breaches, including breaches of continuing obligations; they have no separately stated survival periods. I would not read the end of employment as erasing liability for a breach, but the packet does not expressly give every NDA section its own post-employment lifespan.

## 6. AMBIGUOUS / CONTRADICTORY / SURPRISING

**AMBIGUOUS**

1. **Whether independently authored on-the-job content is excluded from confidentiality.** NDA Section VI excludes:

   > information independently created by the Recipient, including materials the Recipient created before employment or wholly outside its scope as described in the Recipient's offer letter;

   The offer's Work Product & Intellectual Property provision says:

   > All curricula, course materials, scenarios, examination items, recordings, and other deliverables you create within the scope of your employment are works made for hire and the property of MedEdPrep, LLC.

   I read the ownership assignment as clear. The separate confidentiality question is less certain: “independently created” could mean developed independently of company information, potentially including fresh content written alone as part of the job; or a signer could understand the exception as confined to personally owned, pre-existing/outside-scope content. The word “including” does not expressly limit the exception to those latter categories. This matters to disclosure, even though a confidentiality exception would not itself undo the company's ownership.

2. **Where permitted outside teaching stops and prohibited circumvention starts.** NDA Section X states:

   > During the term of this Agreement, and for twelve (12) months thereafter, the Recipient will not attempt to do business with, or otherwise solicit, any business contacts found or otherwise referred by the Disclosing Party to the Recipient for the purpose of circumventing the Disclosing Party, the result of which would be to prevent the Disclosing Party from realizing or recognizing a profit or fees, without the specific written approval of the Disclosing Party.

   The same section states:

   > Nothing in this section limits the outside employment, clinical practice, and teaching permitted by the Recipient's offer letter.

   The offer's Outside Activities provision states:

   > You may continue outside employment and clinical work that does not create a conflict of interest with MedEdPrep or interfere with your duties under this letter; you agree to disclose any outside role that could reasonably create such a conflict.

   The obvious case of diverting a company engagement is restricted. But if a company-introduced agency separately hires Gene to teach, the packet alone may not tell him whether that is preserved outside work or circumvention depriving the company of a fee. The boundary depends on purpose, the company's opportunity, and what constitutes a conflict; the outside-work exception does not settle that application by itself.

3. **Mileage eligibility and calculation.** The offer's Travel & Expenses provision states:

   > When the one-way distance to an assigned site exceeds fifty miles, MedEdPrep will reimburse one-way mileage at the then-current IRS standard mileage rate for use of your personal vehicle.

   My best reading is reimbursement of the whole outbound distance once the trip exceeds 50 miles, rather than only miles beyond 50, but the text does not expressly distinguish those calculations. It also does not identify whether distance is measured from Gene's home, the campus, or another departure point. It is clear that the stated promise is one-way, not round-trip, reimbursement and that exactly 50 miles does not meet “exceeds fifty miles.”

**CONTRADICTORY**

None found. The documents agree on the role and employment classification; the offer and NDA both preserve at-will status. The NDA expressly preserves the offer's treatment of Gene-owned materials and the license to them. Its confidentiality exceptions and survival period qualify the offer's broad confidentiality language through an explicit priority rule, rather than leaving an unresolved direct conflict. The offer states:

> The NDA, including its survival provisions, governs alongside this letter; in the event of any conflict regarding confidentiality, the NDA controls.

**SURPRISING**

1. **Keeping ownership can still mean giving the company permanent, unpaid use.** The offer's Work Product & Intellectual Property provision states:

   > if you incorporate any such material into a MedEdPrep deliverable, you grant MedEdPrep a non-exclusive, royalty-free, perpetual license to use, adapt, and deliver that material as part of its educational programs.

   Gene may retain title to his own teaching materials yet be unable to treat departure as the end of MedEdPrep's permission to teach with or adapt the incorporated material. The continuing license requires no stated royalty or new approval after departure.

2. **The NDA exposes Gene to financial obligations beyond simply keeping information private.** NDA Section XIV states:

   > The Recipient agrees to indemnify and hold the Disclosing Party harmless from all claims, losses, expenses, fees, including attorney fees, costs, and judgments that may be asserted against the Disclosing Party that result from the acts or omissions of the Recipient in breach of this Agreement.

   NDA Section XV states:

   > If a legal suit, action, or proceeding, including arbitration, is brought by any party to enforce or to interpret any provision of this Agreement, the prevailing party will be entitled to recover, in addition to any other damages awarded, all costs associated with conducting the suit, action, proceeding, or arbitration and reasonable attorneys' fees.

   The indemnity runs from Gene to the company and is tied to breach, with no stated monetary cap. The fee-shifting provision is reciprocal and also covers interpretation disputes. Those commitments could be financially significant for a part-time hourly employee.

3. **An NDA also commits the signer to binding arbitration.** NDA Section VIII states:

   > If any controversies, claims, or disputes arising out of or relating to this Agreement cannot be resolved through negotiation, they will be resolved by binding arbitration under the rules of the American Arbitration Association.

   It further states:

   > The arbitrator's decision will be final, and any court with proper jurisdiction may enter judgment upon it.

   I would understand unresolved NDA disputes to go to binding arbitration, not simply an ordinary lawsuit for a fresh decision. The clause concerns disputes arising out of or relating to the NDA; it does not expressly send every possible employment dispute to arbitration.

4. **The 24-month confidentiality period is not an across-the-board endpoint.** NDA Section III states:

   > for information constituting a trade secret (including examination items and item banks) and for student records protected by law, this obligation continues for as long as the information remains a trade secret or legally protected.

   A signer who remembers only “24 months” could materially misunderstand the continuing restrictions on examination information and student records. Separately, neither the end of that period nor departure ends the company's ownership of employment-scope materials.

MISREADING-RISK: MEDIUM — The core pay, status, and ownership rules are explicit, but a good-faith signer could conflate retained ownership with freedom to withdraw the company's perpetual license, overlook the NDA's financial and post-employment commitments, or misunderstand the confidentiality and outside-work boundaries.

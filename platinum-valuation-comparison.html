Strategic Assessment

  • Technology gap is structural, not incremental. Platinum cannot bolt on IRT, CAT, or psychometric CE without a ground-up rebuild. Their static assessment architecture is a 24-year technical debt ceiling.
  • Compliance tooling creates a wedge. CoAssist (CoAEMSP 2023) has no market alternative. Every program that adopts it becomes a MedEdPrep customer across the full stack.
  • The CE market is wide open. 40+ CE providers exist, but zero offer adaptive or psychometric-driven continuing education. MedEdPrep is building into a category of one.
  • Platinum's moat is distribution, not technology. The Pearson/Brady deal is powerful but creates dependency. If textbook bundling shifts or programs demand adaptive tools, the moat erodes.
  • At 3 years, MedEdPrep's IP value is 14–73% of Platinum's total estimated valuation with zero revenue applied. Revenue activation could shift this dramatically.
  • 1M+ metacognition-tagged responses represent a unique dataset that no competitor in the EMS education market possesses. This is a durable data moat.

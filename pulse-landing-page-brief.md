## Strategic Context

**Pulse is free.** The goal is not revenue from Pulse — it's penetration into every EMS classroom in America. Pulse is the top-of-funnel engagement tool that creates organic demand for MedEdPrep's paid test prep platform at mededprep.com.

**The model:**
1. Instructor discovers Pulse (AccreditCon, word of mouth, LinkedIn)
2. Signs up for free, runs games in their classroom
3. Students get hooked on the engagement loop
4. Both instructor and students discover they want deeper study tools, more content, analytics
5. Natural upgrade path to MedEdPrep test prep (paid)

**Social proof comes from MedEdPrep, not Pulse.** Pulse is new. MedEdPrep is established — 10,600+ questions, 1.55M+ student responses, 100+ EMS programs. The landing page positions Pulse as "from the team behind MedEdPrep" and leverages the parent brand's credibility.

### How It Works

**Section header:** *"Running a Pulse Session Takes 60 Seconds"*

**Step 1: Pick Your Content**
Choose from EMS questions mapped to NREMT domains and cert levels — EMR through Paramedic. Filter by topic or difficulty. Or use your own.

**Step 2: Launch the Game**
Students join on their phones with a session code. Choose your mode: Classic for deep review, Lightning for fast recall, Team Battle for group competition. Project the leaderboard. Watch the room come alive.

**Step 3: See the Data**
Your dashboard shows performance by NREMT content area, by student, over time. Know which students missed every cardiac question before they do.

**Closing line:** *"No training required. No IT department. No budget approval. It's free."*

**Card 3: NREMT-Mapped Content**
*"Every question aligned to what they'll actually see on the Registry."*
Questions organized by the NHTSA EMS Education Standards taxonomy, by certification level, by cognitive complexity. Not a generic quiz bank with a medical skin. Built from the ground up around EMS competency domains.

**Card 4: The Duolingo Effect, But for EMS**
*"Between classes, students practice on their own."*
Flashcards powered by spaced repetition. Daily streaks. Achievement patches. Quick 5-minute sessions that fit between shifts. You see who's practicing and what they're struggling with — even when class isn't in session.

**Card 5: Beyond Multiple Choice**
*"Because real EMS decisions aren't always A, B, C, or D."*
Image identification for 12-leads. Hotspot tapping for anatomy. Ordering questions for procedure sequencing. Triage sort for MCI prioritization. The NREMT is moving beyond simple MCQ — your students should be practicing that way now.

### FAQ

**Q: Is Pulse really free?**
Yes. Free for instructors, free for students. No credit card, no trial period, no catch. We built Pulse to be in every EMS classroom — not behind a paywall.

**Q: Do I need to create all my own content?**
No. Pulse comes with EMS questions mapped to NREMT domains and cert levels. You can also add your own questions if you want to customize for your program.

**Q: Does it work on student phones?**
Yes. Pulse is mobile-first — built for the phones your students already have. Works on any modern browser, iOS or Android. Students join with a session code, no app install required for games.

**Q: Can I use this for NREMT exam prep?**
Absolutely. Every question is mapped to the NHTSA EMS Education Standards taxonomy. Your analytics show performance against the same domains that appear on the Registry exam.

**Q: What's the difference between Pulse and MedEdPrep?**
Pulse is the free classroom engagement and study tool — live games, flashcards, and quick analytics. MedEdPrep is the full test prep platform — adaptive exams, diagnostic reports, NREMT readiness prediction, and school management tools. They work great together, but Pulse stands on its own.

**Q: I'm not tech-savvy. Is this complicated?**
If you can share a link, you can run Pulse. Instructors launch a session, students scan a code. The whole setup takes under a minute. No IT department needed.

## Competitive Positioning (Internal — Not for Page)

**What to say:**
- "The only platform that connects live classroom games to solo study"
- "Built for EMS from the ground up — not a generic quiz tool with medical questions"
- "Free for every classroom" (no competitor in EMS is doing this)
- "10 question types that match what the NREMT is moving toward"

**What NOT to say:**
- Don't name competitors (brand rule from LinkedIn campaign)
- Don't claim to "replace" Kahoot/Quizlet — position as "the one tool that does both"
- Don't oversell features that aren't shipped yet (Scenario Chains, Drug Calc, Protocol Pathways are built but hidden — gate with "coming soon" if referenced)

**Likely objections and responses:**

| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "I already use Kahoot" | Kahoot stops at 4-choice MCQ and disappears when class ends. Pulse has 10 EMS question types and students keep studying between classes. |

| "We can't afford another tool" | It's free. |
| "Another platform to manage" | Students join with a code on their own phones. Nothing to install. No IT required. |

## Brand Design System

Reference: `ce-microlearning-research/ce-platform/.pulse-reference/Pulse_Brand Style Guide/Pulse_Brand Style Guide.pdf` (40 pages)

### Logo

**Primary logo:** Shield+EKG icon + "PULSE" (Lemon Milk Light, all-caps) + "BY MEDEDPREP" (Mint Green, smaller)

**Variants available:**
- Horizontal (icon left, text right) — use in nav bar
- Stacked (icon above text) — use for hero or centered layouts
- Icon Mark only (shield+EKG) — use for favicon, app icon, small contexts
- Logotype only (text, no icon) — use when icon is shown separately

**Clear space:** Defined by height of the "P" in PULSE. Maintain on all sides.
**Minimum size:** 35px height

**Color rules:**
- "PULSE" is white on dark backgrounds, dark on light backgrounds
- "BY MEDEDPREP" is always Mint Green (#36C98D) — this is how the parent brand stays visible

### Colors

**Primary palette:**
| Name | Hex | RGB | Use |
|------|-----|-----|-----|
| Black | #000000 | 0, 0, 0 | Backgrounds, dark sections |
| Mint Green | #36C98D | 54, 201, 141 | Primary accent, CTAs, "BY MEDEDPREP", section headers |
| Aqua Teal | #01CDAB | 1, 205, 171 | Secondary accent, hover states, gradients |

**Secondary palette:**
| Name | Hex | RGB | Use |
|------|-----|-----|-----|
| Yellow | #F5C542 | 245, 197, 66 | Gamification highlights, badges, streak callouts |
| Dark Gray | #333333 | 51, 51, 51 | Body text on light backgrounds |
| Light Gray | #CDCDCD | 205, 205, 205 | Borders, dividers, subtle backgrounds |

Each primary color has tint scales (shown in style guide) for backgrounds and hover states.

**Signature background:** Dark-to-green gradient with grain/noise texture — used on all section dividers and dark sections throughout the brand. This is the most recognizable visual element of the Pulse brand.

### Typography

**Primary typeface (headlines/logo):** Lemon Milk Light — all-caps, geometric, sharp. Used for the PULSE wordmark and hero headlines.

**Secondary typeface (everything else):** Poppins (Google Font, free)

| Role | Weight | Size Range |
|------|--------|-----------|
| Headline 1 | Poppins Bold | 32-72px |
| Headline 2 | Poppins SemiBold | 32-72px |
| Subhead | Poppins Medium | 20-32px |
| Body | Poppins Regular | 12-20px |

### Brand Patterns

Two repeating patterns for background texture:
1. **Shield grid** — Rows of the shield+EKG icon mark, mint green strokes on dark gradient
2. **EKG waveform** — Repeating heartbeat waveform lines, mint green on dark gradient

Use subtly at low opacity in section backgrounds to add depth without distracting from content.

### Iconography

10 custom icons in the brand system (mint green, consistent stroke weight, rounded edges):
- Notifications, Live Session, Review, Accuracy
- Leaderboard, Achievement, Progress Tracker, Analytics
- Level Up, Timer

Use these on the feature cards and how-it-works steps.

### Existing Brand Taglines (from style guide)

- **"Ready When It Matters Most."** — Emotional/aspirational hero line
- **"Where Learning Meets Competition"** — Functional positioning (shown on presentation slides)
- **"Train. Track. Perform."** — Canonical three-beat tagline (shown on wall mockup, stationery)

### Landing Page Visual Direction

**Hero section:** Dark gradient background with grain texture. Horizontal logo top-left. Large headline in Poppins Bold (white). Subheadline in Poppins Regular (white/light gray). Mint Green CTA button. Product screenshot or video on right side.

**Alternating sections:** Dark (gradient+grain) for hero, trust bar, feature showcase, final CTA. White/light for problem statement, how it works, FAQ. This creates visual rhythm and breathing room.

**Feature cards:** Dark cards (#000 or #333) with Mint Green accent borders or icon fills. Poppins SemiBold headings, Regular body. Screenshots embedded where possible.

**CTA buttons:** Mint Green (#36C98D) background, white Poppins SemiBold text. Large (48px+ height for mobile touch targets). Full-width on mobile.

**Trust bar / stats:** White section, dark text, large Mint Green numbers for the stat callouts (10,600+ / 1.55M+ / 100+).

**EKG waveform as decorative element:** Used subtly as a section divider or background element (see letterhead and conference badge in style guide for reference). Works as a visual signature that reinforces the medical/EMS context.

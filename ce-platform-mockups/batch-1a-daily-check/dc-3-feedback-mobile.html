Clinical Rationale

ST elevation in leads II, III, and aVF indicates an inferior wall myocardial infarction. The inferior wall is supplied by the right coronary artery (RCA) in approximately 85% of patients. The RCA also typically supplies the SA node and AV node, which is why inferior MIs may present with bradycardia or heart blocks.

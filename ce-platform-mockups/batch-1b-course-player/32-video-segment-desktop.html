Segment 1: Initial Assessment

Dr. Sarah Chen, MD, FACEP Clinical Instructor • EMS Specialist

In this opening segment, we dive deep into the first critical moments of contact with a potential cardiac patient. We will explore the nuances of the visual assessment, primary survey prioritization, and the clinical indicators that differentiate stable angina from acute myocardial infarction (AMI).

Learning Objective

Identify early subtle cues of cardiogenic shock during initial patient survey.

Key Focus

Integration of subjective pain history with objective vital trend analysis.

Notes & Key Points

2:15 High Yield

STEMI recognition criteria: ST elevation ≥1mm in 2+ contiguous leads. Correlate with clinical presentation immediately.

4:30 Key Concept

Right-sided ECG leads (V4R) essential when inferior STEMI suspected. Avoid nitrates if right ventricular involvement is confirmed.

7:45 Clinical Pearl

Door-to-balloon time target: <90 minutes for primary PCI. EMS notification of receiving facility "STEMI Alert" reduces this interval significantly.

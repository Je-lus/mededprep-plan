You arrive on scene to find a 72-year-old female in acute respiratory distress with bilateral rales, JVD, and pink frothy sputum. Her BP is 88/54. What is your priority intervention?

Administer sublingual nitroglycerin

close

Initiate CPAP at 10 cmH2O

check_circle

Establish bilateral IV access...

Perform a 12-lead ECG...

Incorrect Correct Answer: B

Rationale

This patient presents with signs of cardiogenic pulmonary edema and hypotension (88/54). CPAP is the priority to improve oxygenation and decrease work of breathing.

Nitroglycerin is strictly contraindicated when the systolic BP is less than 90 mmHg as it will further reduce preload and cardiac output.

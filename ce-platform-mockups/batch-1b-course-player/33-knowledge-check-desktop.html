Correct Answer: B
LO 1.3: Hemodynamic Management

menu_book Rationale

The patient presents with signs of cardiogenic pulmonary edema. While Nitroglycerin is standard for reducing preload, it is strictly contraindicated when systolic BP is below 90 mmHg (this patient is 88/54). Priority management must focus on ventilatory support with CPAP to drive fluid out of the alveoli and improve oxygenation without further compromising hemodynamic stability.

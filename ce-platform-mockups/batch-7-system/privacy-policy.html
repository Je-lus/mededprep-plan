Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 15, 2026 Effective Date: May 15, 2026

MedEdPrep, LLC ("we", "our", "us") operates the Pulse CE platform. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our Service. We are committed to protecting the privacy of EMS professionals who use our platform for continuing education.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Identity data: Name, email address, NREMT certification number (optional)
  • Professional data: Certification level (EMT, AEMT, Paramedic), state of licensure, agency affiliation
  • Account credentials: Email and hashed password (we never store plaintext passwords)

Learning & Performance Data

As you use the platform, we collect:

  • Course progress, completion status, and assessment scores
  • Question-level response data (answer selections, response times, confidence ratings)
  • Spaced retrieval performance and retention metrics
  • Competency domain scores and adaptive difficulty levels

Technical Data

  • Device type, operating system, browser version
  • IP address and approximate geolocation (city/state level)
  • Session duration, page views, and interaction patterns

3. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We may share data with:

  • CAPCE — We report completed CE activities (name, certification number, hours earned) to CAPCE as required for accreditation compliance
  • Your EMS Agency — If your account is sponsored by an agency, we share your course completion and CE credit data with authorized agency administrators
  • Service Providers — Third-party providers who assist with hosting, analytics, payment processing, and email delivery, bound by data processing agreements
  • Legal Requirements — When required by law, subpoena, or to protect the rights and safety of our users

4. Learning & Performance Data

Your learning data is central to the Pulse CE experience. We use psychometric models to personalize your education. Here is how we handle this sensitive data:

  • Anonymized Research: We may use de-identified, aggregated learning data for psychometric research and platform improvement. Individual responses are never identifiable in research outputs.
  • No Sale of Learning Data: Your performance data is never sold to third parties, recruiters, or employers beyond your sponsoring agency (if applicable).
  • Data Portability: You may request an export of your complete learning history and performance data at any time.

7. Security Measures

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data:

  • TLS 1.3 encryption for all data in transit
  • AES-256 encryption for sensitive data at rest
  • SOC 2-aligned access controls and audit logging
  • Regular security assessments and penetration testing
  • Role-based access control for internal staff

No system is 100% secure. If you become aware of any unauthorized access to your account, contact us immediately at security@pulsece.com.

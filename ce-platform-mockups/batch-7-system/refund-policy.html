Refund Policy

Last updated: May 15, 2026 Effective Date: May 15, 2026

At Pulse CE, we want every EMS professional to feel confident investing in their continuing education. This Refund Policy outlines when and how refunds are issued for our subscription plans and individual course purchases.

30-Day Money-Back Guarantee

All new subscription plans include a 30-day money-back guarantee. If you're not satisfied, request a full refund within 30 days of your initial purchase — no questions asked.

1. Refund Eligibility at a Glance

Purchase Type Refund Window Conditions Status
New subscription (first purchase) 30 days Fewer than 3 courses completed Full Refund
Subscription renewal 7 days No courses started in new period Full Refund
Individual course (not started) 14 days Course not accessed Full Refund
Individual course (in progress) 14 days Less than 30% completed Partial Refund
Individual course (completed) N/A CE credits issued Non-Refundable
Agency/group plan 14 days Custom terms apply See Agreement

2. Subscription Plans

New Subscribers — If you are a first-time subscriber, you have 30 calendar days from the date of your initial payment to request a full refund, provided you have completed fewer than 3 courses during that period.

Renewal Refunds — For automatic renewal charges, you may request a full refund within 7 days of the renewal date, provided you have not started any new courses during the renewed billing period.

Mid-Cycle Cancellation — If you cancel your subscription mid-billing-cycle, your access continues until the end of the current paid period. No partial refunds are issued for unused time within a billing cycle.

Tip: Manage your subscription

To avoid unwanted renewal charges, cancel your subscription at least 48 hours before your billing date via Settings > Billing > Manage Plan.

3. Individual Course Purchases

  • Not Started: Full refund available within 14 days of purchase if the course has not been accessed
  • In Progress (under 30%): A 70% refund is available within 14 days of purchase if you have completed less than 30% of the course content
  • Completed or Credits Issued: No refund is available once the course is completed or CE credits have been issued to your account

5. Earned CE Credits

Once CE credits have been issued and reported to CAPCE, the associated course purchase is non-refundable. This is because:

  • CAPCE accreditation fees are incurred per certification issued ($0.31 per credit hour)
  • Credits cannot be "unissued" once reported to CAPCE
  • Your completion record is part of the permanent accreditation record

6. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, use one of the following methods:

  • Self-Service: Go to Settings > Billing > Request Refund (available for eligible purchases)
  • Email: Send your request to billing@pulsece.com with your account email and order/transaction ID
  • Live Chat: Contact our support team via the Help Center during business hours (Mon-Fri, 8am-8pm EST)

7. Processing & Timing

Approved refunds are processed within 5-10 business days. Refunds are returned to the original payment method used for the purchase. If your original payment method is no longer available, we will work with you to arrange an alternative.

9. Contact Us

For questions about this Refund Policy or to request a refund:

  • Email: billing@pulsece.com
  • Help Center: pulsece.com/help
  • Live Chat: Available Mon-Fri, 8am-8pm EST

Popular Questions

How do I earn CE credits?

CE credits are earned by completing approved continuing education courses on the Pulse CE platform. Each course specifies the number of CE hours (CEH) awarded upon completion. You must watch or complete all required lesson content and pass the post-assessment with a minimum score of 70% to receive credit. Credits are automatically reported to CAPCE within 24 hours of completion, and you can download your certificate immediately from your dashboard.

What certifications are accepted?

Pulse CE is accredited through CAPCE (Commission on Accreditation for Pre-Hospital Continuing Education). Our courses are accepted for EMT, AEMT, and Paramedic recertification through NREMT. Many state EMS offices also accept CAPCE-accredited CE. Check with your state EMS office for specific requirements.

How does the adaptive learning system work?

Pulse CE uses psychometric-driven adaptive technology to personalize your learning experience. The system tracks your performance across EMS knowledge domains and uses spaced retrieval to surface questions at optimal intervals. Courses adapt in difficulty based on your demonstrated competency, ensuring you spend time on areas that need the most improvement.

Can I access courses offline?

Yes. Pulse CE supports offline access for downloaded course content. You can download lessons for offline study via the mobile app. Your progress syncs automatically when you reconnect. Note that post-assessments and certificate generation require an internet connection.

How do I download my certificate?

After completing a course and passing the assessment, your certificate is generated automatically. Navigate to Dashboard > CE Credits > Completed Courses, then click the download icon next to the relevant course. Certificates are available as PDF files and include your CAPCE activity number.

What is the refund policy?

We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee on subscription plans. If you've completed fewer than 3 courses and are not satisfied, contact support for a full refund. Individual course purchases are non-refundable once the course has been started. See our full Refund Policy for details.

How do I update my payment method?

Go to Settings > Billing > Payment Methods. You can add, remove, or update your credit/debit card or other payment methods. Changes take effect on your next billing cycle. If a payment fails, you'll receive an email notification with a link to update your information.

Who do I contact for technical issues?

For technical issues, use the Live Chat feature (available Mon-Fri, 8am-8pm EST) or email support@pulsece.com. For urgent issues affecting course access during your study time, include your device type, browser version, and a screenshot of any error messages. Our support team typically responds within 2 hours during business hours.

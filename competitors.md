## 1. EMS-Specific Test Prep (B2C -- Primary Competitors)

These are companies where the primary product is selling NREMT exam prep directly to individual EMS students.

### EMTprep.com

**URL:** emtprep.com
**Founded:** ~2011
**Headquarters:** Boise, ID
**Certifications covered:** EMT, Paramedic

**What they offer:**
- Practice exams styled after NREMT format (CAT-style variable length)
- Video lessons covering all NREMT content areas (300+ videos)
- Audio lectures (podcast-style, for commute studying)
- Study guides and written content
- Flashcards
- Pass guarantee (refund if you fail NREMT after completing their course)

**Pricing:**
- Monthly: ~$39.99/month
- 6-month: ~$149.99 (~$25/mo)
- 12-month: ~$199.99 (~$17/mo)
- Lifetime access options also available
- Paramedic-level pricing is higher than EMT

**Strengths:**
- Strong brand recognition among EMS students -- likely the most-searched B2C EMS test prep
- Video content is well-produced and covers the full NREMT curriculum
- Pass guarantee is a compelling marketing hook
- Multi-modal learning (video + audio + practice tests + flashcards)
- Good SEO presence for "EMT test prep," "NREMT practice test," etc.
- Active social media and community engagement

**Weaknesses:**
- No adaptive testing. Practice exams are fixed-form with random question selection.
- No IRT or psychometric rigor -- items are author-written, not calibrated
- No diagnostic reporting beyond "you got 72% in cardiology"
- No institutional/instructor channel (B2C only)
- No data moat -- question bank is content, not measurement
- No classroom engagement features
- No accreditation compliance tools

**How MedEdPrep differentiates:**
- Adaptive engine (IRT-based) vs. random question selection
- 10,600+ psychometrically calibrated items vs. uncalibrated question bank
- Multi-dimensional diagnostic reporting vs. simple % correct
- Dual B2B + B2C channel vs. B2C only
- Confidence/metacognition data capture
- Pulse for classroom engagement
- CoAssist for institutional wedge

### Limmer Education / LC Ready / EMTReview.com

**URL:** limmereducation.com, lcready.com, emtreview.com
**Owner:** Daniel Limmer (co-author of Brady's *Emergency Care* textbook, 14th ed.)
**Headquarters:** Northeast US
**Employees:** ~13
**Certifications covered:** EMT, Paramedic, NREMT remediation

**What they offer:**
Limmer Education operates a multi-product ecosystem, all leveraging Dan Limmer's brand authority as Brady's lead EMT textbook author:

- **LC Ready** -- A student monitoring/assignment platform. Instructors assign work, track completion, and monitor student engagement. Built on Ruby on Rails. Not a testing platform per se -- more of a courseware companion.
- **EMTReview.com** -- Practice questions and review materials for NREMT exam prep. Historically one of the earliest EMS test prep sites.
- **NREMT remediation** -- Targeted content for students who have failed NREMT and need guided review before retaking. This is a niche that Limmer owns due to textbook credibility.
- **CE courses** -- CAPCE-accredited continuing education (per-course pricing, $15-75). CAPCE Provider Spotlight recipient Feb 2026.
- **"7 Things EMS" podcast** -- 26 episodes, dormant since June 2025. Functioned as a CE delivery vehicle.

**Pricing:**
- LC Ready: Institutional pricing (per-program or per-student, not publicly listed)
- EMTReview: Per-course or subscription model (~$20-50 range)
- CE: Per-course, $15-75

**Estimated revenue:** $500K-1.5M

**Strengths:**
- Brand authority from Brady textbook authorship -- Limmer is a household name in EMT education
- Established in the NREMT remediation niche, which is a high-intent, high-value segment
- CAPCE accredited for CE delivery
- Multi-product ecosystem (prep + monitoring + CE)
- Personal relationships with hundreds of program directors through decades of textbook conferences
- LC Ready gives instructors a reason to engage between exam days

**Weaknesses:**
- Technology is dated. LC Ready is a Rails monolith. Not mobile-first. Limited real-time features.
- No adaptive testing -- no IRT, no CAT, no psychometric calibration
- Limited item bank size relative to MedEdPrep
- No diagnostic reporting depth
- No classroom game/engagement features
- No accreditation compliance tooling
- "7 Things EMS" podcast has been dormant -- content momentum stalled
- Products feel disconnected (EMTReview vs. LC Ready vs. CE courses are separate experiences)
- Small team (13 people) limits engineering velocity

**How MedEdPrep differentiates:**
- Adaptive engine vs. fixed-form review questions
- Unified platform vs. disconnected products
- Pulse fills the engagement role LC Ready attempts, but with gamification and real-time competition
- CoAssist is free and standalone vs. no compliance offering
- 10,600+ calibrated items vs. smaller uncalibrated bank
- Modern tech stack vs. aging Rails monolith

**Strategic note:** Dan Limmer operating independently from Pearson/Brady is interesting. He clearly sees the textbook publisher as insufficient for ed-tech innovation. A Pearson acquisition of Limmer Education would be a trigger signal to watch (see `competitor-deep-dive-brady-pearson.md`).

### Platinum Educational Group / EMSTesting

**URL:** emstesting.com, platinumed.com
**Founded:** 2002
**Headquarters:** Jenison, MI (Grand Rapids metro)
**CEO:** Kris Kazmierczak
**Employees:** ~25
**Certifications covered:** EMR, EMT, AEMT, Paramedic

**Full deep dive:** `03-market-landscape/competitor-deep-dive-platinum.md`

**What they offer:**
- **EMSTesting** -- Instructor exam bank and assessment platform (flagship product)
- **Platinum Planner** -- Clinical scheduling, attendance tracking, competency management
- **AccredAssist** -- CoAEMSP accreditation compliance and self-study report tooling (~$475)

**Pricing:**
| Component | Price |
|-----------|-------|
| School subscription (Full Access) | $500/year |
| Student access -- EMR | $26/student |
| Student access -- EMT | Est. $30-50/student |
| Student access -- Paramedic | Est. $50-105/student |
| Admissions/entrance testing | $5/student |
| AccredAssist | ~$475 (requires subscription) |

**Estimated programs:** Hundreds nationally. Many program directors have used EMSTesting for 10+ years.

**Strengths:**
- Deep instructor adoption and muscle memory (24-year head start)
- Purpose-built for EMS instructors -- workflow is fast and familiar
- Large pre-validated question bank curated over 20+ years
- Ecosystem breadth (testing + clinicals + accreditation)
- Established conference relationships and distribution
- Platinum Planner free-for-schools model is smart distribution
- Pearson relationship (some Pearson EMS programs bundle/recommend EMSTesting)
- CAPCE accredited (Nov 2025) for workshops/conferences

**Weaknesses:**
- **No real adaptive testing.** Markets "CAT" but uses Classical Test Theory (P-values, KR-20, point-biserial). No published IRT parameters. "Ask them what IRT model they use. If they can't say '2PL' or '3PL,' it's not IRT."
- **No student-facing tools.** Students cannot see correct answers after exams. Students cannot purchase independently. No study tools, flashcards, practice modes.
- **Limited analytics.** Score breakdown by content area only. No multi-dimensional diagnostics, confidence data, learning velocity, predictive readiness.
- **No classroom engagement.** No Pulse equivalent. Invisible between exam days.
- **AccredAssist requires Platinum subscription.** Not standalone. Creates resentment among programs that want compliance tools without switching testing.
- **No CE delivery.** CAPCE accreditation is for workshops/conferences only -- NOT online CE delivery.
- **Aging technology.** Functional but not modern UI. No real-time features.

**How MedEdPrep differentiates:**
- Adaptive engine (IRT-based) vs. CTT with "CAT" marketing
- 10,600+ items with 1.55M responses vs. ~5,000 estimated items
- $26/student flat vs. $500 school fee + $26-105/student + $475 AccredAssist = 3-5x cost advantage
- B2B + B2C vs. B2B only
- CoAssist (free, standalone) vs. AccredAssist ($475, requires subscription)
- Pulse classroom engagement vs. nothing
- Multi-dimensional diagnostic reporting vs. content-area score breakdown

**This is MedEdPrep's most direct competitor for displacement.**

## 2. Broader Test Prep with EMS Products

These companies are not EMS-specific. They offer test prep across many industries/certifications and happen to have an EMS/NREMT product line.

### Pocket Prep

**URL:** pocketprep.com
**Type:** Multi-discipline mobile test prep app
**EMS Products:** EMT, Paramedic NREMT practice tests

**What they offer:**
- Mobile-first practice test app covering 100+ professional certifications
- EMS is one of many verticals (nursing, fire, law enforcement, IT, trades, etc.)
- Daily practice questions
- Performance tracking
- Study reminders

**Pricing:**
- Free tier with limited daily questions
- Premium: ~$14.99-19.99/month or ~$59.99-99.99/year per certification

**Strengths:**
- Excellent mobile UX -- best-in-class app experience among EMS test prep competitors
- Multi-discipline platform means larger engineering budget spread across all verticals
- Daily question habit loop is effective for engagement
- Affordable pricing
- Available on iOS and Android app stores

**Weaknesses:**
- EMS is a tiny fraction of their business -- no deep EMS expertise
- Questions are not psychometrically calibrated for EMS specifically
- No adaptive testing (claims "smart study" but it's spaced repetition, not IRT)
- No institutional/instructor tools
- No diagnostic reporting at the NREMT domain level
- Generic platform applied to EMS, not built for EMS
- No clinical or compliance tools

**How MedEdPrep differentiates:**
- EMS-specific vs. generic multi-discipline platform
- IRT-based adaptive engine vs. spaced repetition scheduling
- Institutional tools + B2C vs. B2C only
- Deep diagnostic reporting vs. basic performance tracking
- 10,600+ EMS-calibrated items vs. generic question bank
- Full ecosystem (Pulse, CoAssist, CE) vs. standalone practice app

### Kaplan

**URL:** kaplan.com
**Type:** Major test prep company (MCAT, USMLE, NCLEX, bar exam, etc.)
**EMS Products:** Limited -- historically offered EMT/Paramedic review materials

**What they offer:**
- Kaplan's EMS presence is minimal. They have offered practice tests and study materials for NREMT at various times, but EMS is not a focus area.
- Primary healthcare test prep focus is nursing (NCLEX) and medical school (MCAT, USMLE, COMLEX).

**Pricing:** Varies; EMS-specific products have been priced at $50-150

**Strengths:**

**Weaknesses:**
- EMS is too small to warrant meaningful investment from Kaplan
- Current EMS offerings are basic and not prioritized
- No institutional EMS tools
- No EMS-specific expertise or relationships

**Threat level:** Very low. Kaplan entering EMS seriously would require a strategic decision that the market size does not justify. If they did, the 12-24 month development timeline gives MedEdPrep runway.

## 4. Instructor-Facing Testing (B2B -- School-Facing)

These competitors sell primarily to EMS programs and instructors. MedEdPrep competes directly in this space with its institutional testing products.

### Platinum Educational Group / EMSTesting

**Strongest match.** See Section 1 above and full deep dive at `03-market-landscape/competitor-deep-dive-platinum.md`.

**Key B2B features:**
- Instructor exam bank with custom test builder
- Custom question creation (mix bank items with instructor-authored items)
- Pre-validated final exams
- Online testing with timed sessions, randomized questions/answers
- Score reports by content area
- Admissions/entrance testing ($5/student)
- "CAT" (marketed as adaptive -- actually CTT-based)
- Platinum Planner for clinical scheduling (free for schools, students pay)
- AccredAssist for CoAEMSP accreditation compliance (~$475)

**Displacement strategy:** Wedge with CoAssist (free) and B2C student adoption. Run parallel pilots. Convert through demonstrated diagnostic superiority. See `competitor-deep-dive-platinum.md` for full strategy.

### Jones & Bartlett / Public Safety Group / Fisdap

**Full deep dive:** `03-market-landscape/competitor-deep-dive-jb-fisdap.md`

**Corporate structure:**
```
Ascend Learning ($2B+, KKR-backed)
  |-- Jones & Bartlett Learning (JBL)
  |     |-- AAOS "Orange Book" (EMT textbook)
  |     |-- Nancy Caroline (Paramedic textbook)
  |     |-- Navigate platform (digital courseware + test banks)
  |
  |-- Public Safety Group (PSG)
  |     |-- Fisdap (acquired ~2017)
  |     |-- Recert.com (CE platform, NAEMT partnership)
```

**Key B2B features:**

*Navigate:*
- Digital textbook + chapter-aligned test banks
- Instructor assignments and quizzes
- Gradebook and basic reporting
- Publisher-locked to JBL textbooks
- NOT a standalone assessment platform

*Fisdap:*
- Entrance exams (~$30-40/student) with predictive validity claims
- Unit exams (~$40-60/student per bundle)
- Comprehensive/readiness exams (~$35-50/student)
- Clinical tracking (encounters, skills, shift logging)
- Scheduling (shift assignment, conflict detection)
- Learning prescriptions (post-exam remediation guidance)
- Appendix G tracking for CoAEMSP

**Total student cost:** $400-550+ for textbook + Navigate + full Fisdap bundle

**Key weakness:** Navigate and Fisdap are architecturally separate. Separate logins, separate data models, separate gradebooks, separate support channels. This fragmentation is MedEdPrep's primary exploit vector.

**Displacement strategy:** Position as complementary layer. "Keep Fisdap for field logs. Use MedEdPrep for exams, adaptive practice, and diagnostics." Wedge through CoAssist (Appendix G auto-population that Fisdap cannot match). Do NOT force a binary choice.

### Brady / Pearson / MyLab Brady

**Full deep dive:** `03-market-landscape/competitor-deep-dive-brady-pearson.md`

**Corporate structure:**
- Brady is a publishing imprint within Pearson Higher Education
- Brady authors: Limmer, Bledsoe, Mistovich, Dickinson (the real brand)
- MyLab Brady is a vertical instance of Pearson's MyLab platform
- Pearson revenue: ~$4.7B. EMS is <0.5% of that.

**Key B2B features:**
- Chapter-aligned test banks tied to Brady textbooks
- Homework and practice assignments
- Pretest/posttest sequencing (marketed as "adaptive" -- NOT IRT)
- Dynamic Study Modules (confidence-based flashcard scheduling)
- LMS integration (Canvas, Blackboard, D2L, Moodle)
- Gradebook and basic reporting

**Pricing:** Bundled with textbook purchase. Effective student cost: $44-280 depending on access type.

**Key weakness:** Publisher-locked. Only works with Brady textbooks. Cannot serve as standalone assessment. No comprehensive/entrance/exit exams. No diagnostic reporting. No B2C path. "Adaptive" is pretest/posttest chapter sequencing, not IRT. Pearson's EMS "adaptive testing" is reportedly Platinum's product distributed through Pearson's channel.

**Displacement strategy:** Position alongside, not against the textbook. "Use Brady for content delivery, use MedEdPrep for assessment and diagnostics. If you switch textbooks next year, your MedEdPrep data still works."

**Threat timeline:** Lowest near-term probability. EMS is too small for Pearson to justify dedicated engineering. Watch for Platinum acquisition signals.

## 5. Adjacent but Not Equivalent

These products overlap with parts of MedEdPrep's footprint but are not direct head-to-head competitors.

### LC Ready (Limmer Education)

**Type:** Student monitoring/assignment companion
**Not equivalent because:** LC Ready is primarily an instructor tool for assigning work and tracking student completion. It is not a testing platform, does not have an exam bank, and does not deliver assessments. It fills the "courseware companion" niche, not the "assessment and diagnostics" niche.

**Overlap:** Competes for instructor attention/adoption. Programs using LC Ready may feel they already have a "digital tool" and resist adding MedEdPrep. Counter: "LC Ready tracks assignments. MedEdPrep diagnoses competency gaps. They solve different problems."

---

### EMS1 Academy / Lexipol

**Type:** LMS and CE delivery platform for agencies
**Not equivalent because:** Lexipol (which owns EMS1 Academy) serves agencies, not initial education programs. Their products are for working EMTs/paramedics doing continuing education and compliance training, not for students preparing for initial certification.

**Overlap:** When MedEdPrep launches its CE platform, Lexipol becomes a direct CE competitor. In the initial education market, there is no overlap. For CE competitive analysis, see Section 6 below and `entity-ce-market.html`.

---

### Ally by EMS World (HMP Global)

**Type:** CE platform tied to EMS World media brand
**Parent:** HMP Global
**Not equivalent because:** CE/recertification platform, not initial education testing
**Common misconception:** Often confused as a sister product to JEMS Training. They are NOT related -- JEMS is Clarion Events (Blackstone), Ally is HMP Global. They are competitors.

---

### NAEMSE Testing Platform

**Type:** National Association of EMS Educators testing tools
**Not equivalent because:** Primarily a professional association resource, not a commercial testing platform. Offers some exam development guidance and accreditation resources for educators.

## 7. Key Market Corrections

Common misconceptions that appear in casual competitive analysis. Get these right.

| Common Belief | Reality |
|--------------|---------|
| Bound Tree Medical/EDU is owned by Henry Schein ($12B) | Owned by **Sarnova** ($989M) / Patricia Industries / **Investor AB**. Henry Schein is a competitor, not a parent. |
| JEMS Training and Ally by EMS World are sister products | **Different parents.** JEMS = **Clarion Events** (Blackstone). Ally = **HMP Global**. They are competitors, not siblings. |
| Bound Tree EDU CE is entirely free | Only **5 courses free**, then **$159.99/year** (same as FOAMfrat direct). It is a freemium funnel for Sarnova's supply business. |
| Platinum/EMSTesting has real adaptive testing | Markets "CAT" but uses **Classical Test Theory** (P-values, KR-20, point-biserial). No published IRT parameters. No theta estimation. No adaptive item selection. |
| MyLab Brady has adaptive testing | **No.** Their "adaptive" feature is pretest/posttest chapter sequencing and confidence-based flashcard scheduling. Not IRT. Not CAT. Pearson's EMS "adaptive testing" offering is reportedly Platinum's product resold through Pearson's channel. |
| Fisdap uses IRT/adaptive testing | **No.** Fisdap explicitly uses Classical Test Theory. Fixed-form, fixed-length exams. Their own documentation states this. |
| Navigate and Fisdap are integrated | **No.** Separate platforms, separate logins, separate data models, separate billing channels. Both owned by Ascend Learning but architecturally independent. |

## 8. MedEdPrep's Unique Position

No competitor in the EMS education market combines ALL of:

1. **IRT-based adaptive testing engine** -- Live in production. Domain-based with IRT infrastructure building toward full CAT. Real theta estimation and item-level calibration.

2. **10,600+ psychometrically calibrated items with 1.55M response records** -- This is not a question bank, it is a calibrated measurement instrument. Estimated 2x the bank size of the next-largest EMS competitor (Platinum).

3. **Dual B2B + B2C channel** -- Sells to both institutions ($26/student, 100+ programs) and individual students. Competitors are almost always one or the other.

4. **Multi-dimensional diagnostic reporting** -- Domain, cognitive level, confidence, timing, stamina, streak analysis. Goes far beyond "72% in cardiology."

5. **Confidence/metacognition data** -- 1M+ responses tagged High/Medium/Low confidence. Enables diagnostic analysis of what students *think they know* vs. what they *actually know*. No competitor captures this.

6. **Classroom engagement platform (Pulse)** -- Live classroom games, leaderboards, XP, streaks, flashcards, study tracking. Free Trojan horse into classrooms. No direct competitor in EMS-specific classroom engagement.

7. **Accreditation compliance tools (CoAssist)** -- Free, standalone, works with any testing platform. CoAEMSP Appendix G auto-population. No competitor offers this for free.

8. **CE platform (coming)** -- First CAPCE-accredited provider with adaptive learning, psychometric diagnostics, and interactive assessment. Zero current CE providers offer this combination.

9. **School management portal (Portal)** -- MedEdPrep Portal serves as CRM, student management, and program admin. Full-stack institutional tooling.

10. **Psychometric expertise** -- Founder has deep psychometric background (IRT, CTT, adaptive testing, bifactor modeling). Most EMS ed-tech companies were built by educators or publishers, not psychometricians.

11. **Full-stack ownership** -- 15 codebases across the entire EMS education value chain at ~$2,500/month infrastructure cost. No external vendor dependencies.

12. **Publisher-agnostic** -- Works with any textbook (Brady, J&B, Elsevier, or none). This is an asymmetric advantage against publisher-locked competitors.

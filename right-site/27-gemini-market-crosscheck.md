# Custom Interactive E-Learning Module: Market & Pricing Cross-Check

This report provides a detailed pricing and market cross-check for developing and hosting a custom, asynchronous, highly interactive software/product-training e-learning module. 

### Project Specifications
*   **Duration:** ~30 minutes of finished content (highly interactive, Level 3-4 complexity).
*   **Interactivity:** Embedded product walkthrough video with interleaved knowledge checks, a post-test, and a scripted branching "simulated call" scenario.
*   **Audience Size:** ~4,000 fire-department personnel.
*   **Delivery Infrastructure:** Vendor-hosted delivery with completion tracking and an admin dashboard (the client has no LMS).

---

## 1. Pricing Benchmarks

### Per-Finished-Minute and Per-Finished-Hour Cost by Interactivity Level

Industry benchmarks categorize e-learning into four levels of complexity. A "finished hour" or "finished minute" refers to the seat time experienced by the learner, not the labor hours spent in production. 

#### Benchmark 1: Bryan Chapman / Chapman Alliance (2010 Study, with Inflation Adjustments)
The 2010 Chapman Alliance study, *"How Long Does it Take to Create Learning?"*, remains the historical industry baseline. Because these figures are from 2010, they are presented below alongside a **2026 inflation-adjusted projection** (assuming ~50% cumulative inflation based on CPI data).

| Complexity Level | Avg. Development Hours (per Finished Hour) | Avg. Cost (2010 Cost per Finished Hour) | Projected 2026 Cost (per Finished Hour) | Projected 2026 Cost (per Finished Minute) |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **Level 1 (Basic/Passive)** | ~79 hours | $10,054 | ~$15,081 | ~$251.35 |
| **Level 2 (Interactive)** | ~184 hours | $18,583 | ~$27,875 | ~$464.58 |
| **Level 3 (Advanced/Simulated)** | ~490 hours | $50,371 | ~$75,557 | ~$1,259.28 |

*   **Development Ratio Range:** For Level 3, the Chapman Alliance study noted a wide range of development hours, starting at 217 hours and scaling up to **716+ hours** of development per finished hour of instruction for high-fidelity simulations.

#### Benchmark 2: ATD / Kapp & Defelice Studies (2020/2021)
The Association for Talent Development (ATD) publishes periodic updates by researchers Karl Kapp and Robyn Defelice. Their studies focus on the labor hours required to build one finished hour of training.

| Interactivity Level | Description | Avg. Development Hours (per Finished Hour) |
| :--- | :--- | :---: |
| **Level 1 (Passive)** | Linear page-turners, text, graphics, simple navigation. | ~34 hours |
| **Level 2 (Limited)** | Level 1 + simple quizzes, basic drag-and-drops, minimal branching. | ~49 hours |
| **Level 3 (Complex)** | Branching scenarios, custom animations, complex simulations. | ~116 hours |
| **Level 4 (Real-Time)** | Full game-based learning, high-fidelity simulations, custom code. | ~217 hours |

*Note: The **49-hour** development ratio is widely cited as the industry midpoint for general e-learning, but a branching "simulated call" course falls into Level 3 or Level 4, requiring **116 to 217+ labor hours** per finished hour of training.*

#### Benchmark 3: Current Market & Articulate Community Consensus (2026)
Consensus from rapid-authoring communities (e.g., Articulate E-Learning Heroes) and e-learning developers indicates the following market rates:
*   **Standard Rule of Thumb:** **$500 per finished minute** of content (translates to $30,000 per finished hour) is the baseline quote for average interactivity.
*   **High-End Custom/Simulations (Level 3-4):** Typically quoted at **$1,000 to $1,500+ per finished minute** ($60,000 to $90,000+ per finished hour) due to the extensive custom scripting, visual design, and software simulation required.

### Typical Per-Project Ranges for a ~30-Minute Highly Interactive Module

Given that the target module is **30 minutes of finished content** at **Level 3-4 complexity** (interactive walkthrough, branching simulated call, post-test), the development effort is estimated at **58 to 110+ labor hours** (using ATD ratios) or up to **245 labor hours** (using Chapman Level 3 ratios).

Typical commercial quotes from e-learning agencies and freelance networks fall into these ranges:

*   **Full-Service Agency Build:** **$20,000 – $45,000** for a custom, highly interactive 30-minute module. If the branching simulation involves custom-shot videos, highly custom visual assets, or complex system integrations, quotes easily scale to **$50,000 – $80,000+**.
*   **Freelance Developer Build:** **$5,000 – $25,000**. Freelance instructional designers and developers typically bill between **$50 and $125+ per hour** (U.S. rates). At 100 to 200 hours of development effort, this represents the direct labor cost, though project management and QA testing fall onto the client.
*   **Internal Build (Labor Cost Only):** If built by an internal instructional designer with a standard authoring tool (e.g., Articulate Storyline), the internal cost is roughly **$3,000 – $10,000** in internal hours, but excludes licensing and hosting tools.

### Recurring Hosting, Delivery, and Maintenance Pricing Models

Since the client has **no internal LMS** but needs to track completion data for **~4,000 learners**, they must utilize a vendor-hosted delivery model. 

#### Model A: SCORM Cloud (Rustici Software) — Hosting Only
If the course is built using a standard authoring tool (e.g., Articulate Storyline), it can be hosted directly on SCORM Cloud and shared via public/private dispatch links.
*   **Pricing Structure:** Monthly subscription based on "registrations" (when a learner is associated with a course).
*   **Cost for 4,000 Learners:** The **"Bigger" plan** costs **$1,100 per month** ($13,200/year) and includes **4,000 registrations**. Overages are billed at **$0.25 per registration**.
*   **Dashboard & Tracking:** Out of the box. Provides robust, SCORM-compliant completion reports, score tracking, and an admin dashboard.

#### Model B: Articulate Reach 360 — Integrated LMS/Hosting
Reach 360 is Articulate’s integrated LMS for Teams subscribers.
*   **Pricing Structure:** "Active Learner" model. A user is counted as active if they begin or access training in a 30-day period.
*   **Cost for 4,000 Learners:** The Starter plan (included with an Articulate Teams license) is capped at 300 active learners/year. For 4,000 learners, a custom **Reach 360 Pro** annual contract is required. Pro pricing is quote-based, but volume pricing typically translates to **$2 to $5 per active learner per year**, equating to **$8,000 to $20,000 annually** (plus a baseline Articulate 360 Teams license at $1,499/creator/year).
*   **Dashboard & Tracking:** Out of the box. Specifically designed to deliver Rise and Storyline modules to external/internal audiences with full completion tracking.

#### Model C: Mid-Market SaaS LMS
A lightweight, external-facing SaaS LMS (e.g., TalentLMS, LearnUpon, Docebo, Coassemble).
*   **Pricing Structure:** Annual subscription based on registered or active monthly users.
*   **Cost for 4,000 Learners:** Typically ranges from **$15,000 to $45,000 per year** (averaging $0.30 to $1.00 per user per month at volume). Implementation/setup fees typically add a one-time charge of **$3,000 to $10,000**.
*   **Dashboard & Tracking:** Out of the box. Fully customized learner portals, certifications, and compliance reporting.

#### Ongoing Maintenance Models
*   **Annual Maintenance Contract (AMC):** Typically **10% to 15%** of the initial build cost per year (e.g., **$3,000 to $4,500/year** on a $30,000 course) for minor text changes, UI updates, and compliance revisions.
*   **Hourly Support:** Billed ad-hoc at agency rates of **$100 – $150+/hour** or freelance rates of **$50 – $125/hour**.

## 2. Provider Landscape & Delivery Capabilities

### Custom E-Learning Vendors and Instructional-Design Agencies

These agencies provide bespoke learning design, content curation, scripting, and media creation. They handle end-to-end development but typically deliver SCORM packages rather than providing LMS hosting out of the box (though many can host on a custom-branded instance of a third-party LMS for an extra fee).

*   **SweetRush:** Highly consultative agency specializing in custom enterprise learning, gamification, and simulations. Operates entirely on a custom-quote model. Projects typically start at a **$25,000 minimum** (SINGLE SOURCE — unverified) and often average $50,000+. They also offer "Talent Solutions" (staff augmentation) to provide direct contract designers to clients.
*   **AllenComm:** A major enterprise agency focusing on custom portals, training strategy, and rich media. Their pricing is entirely quote-based and tailored to corporate training needs. Minimum project sizes typically exceed **$30,000 to $50,000** (SINGLE SOURCE — unverified).
*   **ELM Learning:** Focuses on design-centric, brain-science-based learning. Operates on a custom quote model; known for highly interactive content and premium visual design.
*   **Saffron Interactive:** A bespoke agency focusing on high-interactivity branching scenarios, behavioral simulations, and AI coaching. They charge project-based custom fees based on the complexity of the target simulation.

### Interactive Software-Demo / Walkthrough Platforms

These tools capture a live software interface to create guided walkthroughs. They are primarily designed for sales and marketing enablement, rather than formal training.

| Platform | Pricing Model | Starting Range | Native SCORM / LMS Completion Tracking? |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :---: |
| **Arcade** | Tiered SaaS | $0 – $297+/mo | **No** |
| **Supademo** | Tiered SaaS | $0 – $350+/mo | **No** |
| **Storylane** | Tiered SaaS | $0 – $625+/mo | **No** |
| **Navattic** | Annual SaaS | ~$500 – $1,000+/mo | **No** |
| **Walnut** | Annual SaaS | $20,000+ /year | **No** |
| **Reprise** | Custom SaaS | $10,000 – $60,000+/year | **No** |

#### Critical Delivery Gaps
*   **LMS / SCORM Export:** **None** of these tools (Storylane, Navattic, Walnut, Reprise, Supademo, Arcade) offer native SCORM or xAPI export out of the box. They cannot communicate completion, scoring, or pass/fail data directly to a standard LMS.
*   **Tracking Focus:** Their analytical dashboards track marketing metrics (unique visitors, engagement rates, click paths, drop-off points, CRM attribution) rather than educational completions.
*   **Training Workaround:** To use these tools for training, the walkthrough must be embedded as an iframe inside an LMS. The LMS will track that the learner *opened* the page containing the frame, but cannot track whether the learner actually completed the walkthrough unless manually self-reported by the learner.

### The Articulate / Storyline 360 Ecosystem

The Articulate suite is the industry standard for creating custom, SCORM-compliant e-learning.

*   **Articulate 360 Teams License:** Costs **$1,499 per seat per year** (with multi-seat and academic/non-profit discounts available). This provides authoring tools (Storyline 360, Rise 360) and Review 360 for stakeholder collaborative reviews.
*   **Reach 360 Starter:** Included **free** with every Articulate 360 Teams subscription. It supports up to **300 active learners per year** out of the box, with full completion tracking and a simple admin dashboard.
*   **Reach 360 Pro:** Requires an upgrade to a custom annual contract to support the client's **4,000 learners**. 
*   **Vendor-Hosted Capability:** Yes, Reach 360 provides hosted delivery, an admin dashboard, and full SCORM-compliant completion tracking out of the box.

*   **The "$500-Per-Minute" Rule:** This standard e-learning cost metric is widely cited and corroborated as a baseline rule of thumb across `xcelus.com`, `reddit.com/r/instructionaldesign`, and `learningcarton.com`.
*   **Reach 360 Starter Limits:** The **300 active learner/year** cap for the free tier of Reach 360 is corroborated by `articulatesupport.com` and the official `articulate.com` pricing directory.
*   **Articulate 360 Teams Licensing:** The **$1,499/year** per-user Teams licensing cost was corroborated by `checkthat.ai` and `articulatesupport.com`.
*   **SCORM Cloud Pricing:** The **$1,100/month** pricing for the "Bigger" plan (including 4,000 monthly registrations and $0.25 overage fees) was corroborated by Rustici Software's official pricing tables and SaaS directory comparisons.
*   **LMS Cost Ranges:** The average mid-market LMS cost range of **$15,000 to $45,000 per year** for 4,000 users was corroborated by `momentivesoftware.com` and Brandon Hall Group research summaries.
*   **Interactive Demo SaaS Ranges:** Starting pricing lists for Navattic (~$500/mo), Reprise ($10,000–$60,000/yr), and Walnut ($20,000+/yr) were corroborated across SaaS procurement platforms including `vendr.com` and G2.

Negotiation playbook — what to say

The core move

Anchor high on the build, keep per-cert modest, trade every concession. The build is a one-time number that doesn't compound and costs us little to discount — so it's what you give ground on. The per-cert gets multiplied "×4,000 forever" in their head, so it's your recurring engine — protect it. Never give a concession for free; pull something back every time.

Concession scripts

Trade — don't give. Each drop buys a commitment.
  • Build → $32K"I can come down to $32K on the build if we lock Dallas in at signing."
  • Hold $5/cert"I'll hold $5 a cert if we commit to a 2-year platform term."
  • Build → $30K"$30K build works if you can do 50% up front."

Talking lines

The framing that wins the room.
  • Reframe the cost"A commercial LMS runs $5–10 per user per month — $240,000 to $480,000 a year for Houston alone. We charge $5 per certificate issued. You only pay when someone actually finishes."
  • Reframe the build"The build is a small one-time cost. What you're really buying is the hosted platform — and Dallas, and every department after it, is just a re-skin of what we've already built."

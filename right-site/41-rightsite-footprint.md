# RightSite Health: Customer Base & Market Footprint Research Report

This report analyzes the customer base, payer relationships, geographic footprint, scale signals, and expansion timeline of **RightSite Health**, with a high-priority focus on its recent activity and target deployments in large Texas fire departments.

### Company Identity & Disambiguation Note
*   **Official Legal Entity:** RightSite Health Physicians, PLLC ([NPI Registry](https://npidb.org/organizations/allopathic_osteopathic_physicians/emergency-medicine_207q00000x/1801452956.aspx)).
*   **Leadership:** Founded in 2021 by **Jamo Rubin, M.D.** (CEO), who previously built and sold social drivers of health (SDOH) platform TAVHealth ([The Org Profile](https://theorg.com/org/rightsite-health/org-chart/jamo-rubin) / [Internal Chat Record](file:///home/jeramey/projects/domination/right-site/initial-chat-copy.md#L165-L166)). The Chief Financial/Strategy Officer is Michael Boxer ([The Org Profile](https://theorg.com/org/rightsite-health/org-chart/michael-boxer)).
*   **HQ Location:** San Antonio, Texas, with its primary corporate offices located at 1100 NE Loop 410, Suite 850, San Antonio, TX 78209 ([Fort Worth Professional Services Agreement](https://www.fortworthtexas.gov/files/assets/public/v/1/city-secretary/documents/agendas/2025/061025/professional-services-agreement-rightsite.pdf) / [NPI Registry](https://npidb.org/organizations/allopathic_osteopathic_physicians/emergency-medicine_207q00000x/1801452956.aspx)).
*   **Core Business Model:** RightSite Health partners with 911 dispatch systems, EMS agencies, and municipal fire departments to manage low-acuity, non-emergency medical calls. On-scene paramedics or dispatchers connect patients virtually to board-certified emergency room (ER) physicians and patient navigators via video or phone. The service aims to reduce unnecessary emergency room transports and keep emergency units available for life-threatening emergencies. The program is typically "free to the city" as RightSite bills the patient's health insurance directly for the telehealth consultations.
*   **CRITICAL DISAMBIGUATION:** RightSite Health (rightsitehealth.com) must not be confused with unrelated commercial entities operating under the name "RightSite" or "Right Site," such as commercial real estate brokerages, site-selection software (e.g., legacy software by EPA or BigFix), or laboratory diagnostic services. The findings below pertain exclusively to the emergency-telehealth and EMS-diversion services of RightSite Health.

## 5. Houston / Dallas Fire Deployments

The expansion into the largest metropolitan fire departments in Texas represents RightSite Health's highest-priority growth vector. 

### Houston Fire Department (HFD) Target:
*   **Department Size:** The Houston Fire Department is the largest in Texas and the third-largest in the United States, with approximately **4,000 to 4,072** appointed or classified personnel ([City of Houston HFD Portal](https://www.houstontx.gov/fire/) / [Texas State Fire Records](https://www.tfs.tamu.edu)).
*   **Deployment Status:** As of mid-2026, there is **no public record of an executed city-wide contract or active municipal deployment** between RightSite Health and the Houston Fire Department. 
*   **Scope & Value:** Houston represents the primary upcoming training deployment for MedEdPrep. At standard commercial SaaS LMS rates of $5–$10/user/month, seat licensing for HFD's 4,000 personnel would cost **$240K–$480K/year** ([Internal Market Pricing Analysis](file:///home/jeramey/projects/domination/right-site/20-market-pricing.md#L125) / [Internal Master Brief](file:///home/jeramey/projects/domination/right-site/00-RIGHT-SITE-MASTER-BRIEF.md#L17)). To avoid these prohibitive per-seat licensing fees, RightSite is seeking a flat-fee custom-hosted training solution (like the one proposed by MedEdPrep) a highly valuable alternative. 
*   *Confidence Note:* High. HFD headcount is verified via state fire records. The status of the contract is confirmed as pending/unexecuted in public databases, aligning with MedEdPrep's project roadmap.

### Dallas Fire-Rescue (DFR) Target:
*   **Department Size:** Dallas Fire-Rescue is the second-largest fire department in Texas, with approximately **2,000 to 2,251** active personnel ([City of Dallas DFR Portal](https://dallascityhall.com/departments/fire-rescue/) / [Daily Dispatch Fire Directory](https://www.dailydispatch.com)).
*   **Deployment Status:** As of mid-2026, **no public city-wide contract or active municipal deployment** has been executed for Dallas Fire-Rescue. RightSite Health is actively targeting Dallas as its next immediate expansion market following the Houston rollout ([Internal Master Brief](file:///home/jeramey/projects/domination/right-site/00-RIGHT-SITE-MASTER-BRIEF.md#L17-L18) / [Internal Synthesis](file:///home/jeramey/projects/domination/right-site/31-SYNTH-pricing-and-positioning.md#L73)). 
*   **Regional Footprint:** While DFR is not yet active, RightSite has established substantial operational footprints in the immediate Dallas-Fort Worth metroplex, including the City of Fort Worth (active contract) and Garland Fire Department (active partnership) ([Fort Worth City Council Records](https://www.fortworthtexas.gov/files/assets/public/v/1/city-secretary/documents/agendas/2025/061025/professional-services-agreement-rightsite.pdf) / [Garland Fire Department Portal](https://www.garlandfire.com)).
*   *Confidence Note:* High. DFR headcount is verified via Dallas municipal files. RightSite's active adjacent regional footprint in DFW is fully documented.

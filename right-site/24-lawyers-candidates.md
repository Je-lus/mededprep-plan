# Contract Kit — Candidate Attorneys / Firms (Metro Atlanta + ~1 hr NW Georgia)

**Purpose:** Find counsel to paper the RightSite Health e-learning engagement — a technology/SaaS services deal needing a **Master Services Agreement (MSA)**, **Statement of Work (SOW)**, **IP ownership/assignment + license-back** terms, **white-label / hosting** provisions, and a **professional-services agreement** suitable for a small Georgia company. Target practice: technology transactions / SaaS / software licensing / commercial contracting.

**How to read the fit column:** RightSite is a small-company, one-off-then-recurring training-content build. Best fit = a boutique or small/mid firm (or solo) that drafts SaaS/MSA/SOW/IP kits on flat or modest hourly fees — not a global firm whose rates and conflict checks suit enterprise software M&A. Big firms are listed because they have the deepest Atlanta tech-transactions benches and are useful if the deal grows or for a one-time template build, but they are flagged as likely overkill/over-budget for a small shop.

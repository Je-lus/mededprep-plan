# 22 — Our Prior Pricing Research (What MedEdPrep Already Knows About What This Work Is Worth)

**Question this file answers:** Has MedEdPrep already done pricing or market research that tells us what the RightSite-style ask (a custom-built, client-hosted, interactive/trackable e-learning module with admin login + certificates) is worth?

**Short answer — read this first:**
> **No.** MedEdPrep has done a *lot* of pricing work, but every dollar figure on record is for **MedEdPrep's own products sold to EMS students and programs** (subscriptions, per-student institutional access) or for **grant-coordination admin fees**. There is **NO prior internal research, quote, proposal, or price sheet for building a bespoke e-learning module *for an external client to host and brand as their own training*.** This is a net-new service line for the company. We are pricing this from a blank slate and should not pretend an internal precedent exists. (See "The honest gap" below, and the external benchmark in the final section to anchor the number.)

## 2. The honest gap (state this plainly to the founder)

MedEdPrep has **never priced a "we build your training module, you host/brand it, you get an admin dashboard" engagement.** Specifically missing from all internal records:
- No price for a **one-time custom build** (video reassembly + interactive overlays + interleaved knowledge checks + branching sim + certificate).
- No price for **hosting/maintenance as a recurring line** for an external client.
- No price for **admin-seat / completion-reporting access** sold to an org that has no LMS.
- No precedent for **clean-room reproduction of a client's UI** as billable scope.

The closest *asset* precedent (the `/gtc-demo/` → `/mededprep-demo/` → demo.mededprep.com interactive-video-with-tracking build) was built **for MedEdPrep's own sales/onboarding use and never sold or priced as a client deliverable.** So we have the *capability* proven and the *cost-to-build mostly sunk*, but **zero pricing history** for offering it as a service.

---

## 3. External benchmark to anchor the number (since we have no internal one)

Because no internal figure exists, the only defensible anchor is the custom-e-learning-development market. **Corroborated across 3+ independent sources**, finished interactive e-learning runs:
- **~$5,000 for a simple template-based module, up to $50,000+ for one finished hour** of richly interactive content (AllenComm; bluecarrot.io both give the **$5k → $50k/finished-hour** range).
- Development effort: **~2 hours per finished minute (120:1)** for Storyline/Captivate-style interactivity (Christy Tucker), and **~90 hours for a 30–40 min mildly interactive module** (r/elearning practitioner consensus) — i.e., the RightSite ask (interleaved questions + a branching live-call sim = high interactivity) sits toward the **upper** end of effort per minute.

**Implication for the RightSite quote (framing, not a committed price):** A single high-interactivity module with a branching simulation, custom UI reproduction, hosting, and an admin/reporting dashboard plausibly lands in the **mid-four to low-five figures for the build**, plus a **recurring hosting/admin fee** — and MedEdPrep's marginal build cost is unusually low because the interactive-video + tracking + certificate engine **already exists** (gtc-demo/mededprep-demo). That gap between market build-price and our near-zero marginal rebuild cost is the margin opportunity. **Flagged:** the specific dollar figure for *this* deal is not yet researched anywhere internally — it must be set fresh.

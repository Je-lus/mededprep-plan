# Market + Pricing Cross-Check: Custom Asynchronous Interactive Product Training

Research date: 2026-06-19

Scope: a vendor-built, asynchronous, interactive product-training module for about 4,000 fire-department personnel. Assumed deliverable is roughly 30 minutes of learner seat time with embedded walkthrough video, interleaved knowledge checks, a post-test, a scripted branching simulated call, completion tracking/admin reporting, and vendor-hosted delivery because the client has no LMS.

Attribution convention: source names in the tables map to the URLs listed in **Sources touched**. Figures marked **SINGLE SOURCE** were found from only one independent source in this sweep or from a vendor's own page without an independent numeric corroboration.

## Executive readout

For this scope, a credible market budget is best treated as two line items:

1. **Custom module build:** likely **$25,000-$90,000** for a 30-minute branching/interactive course in mainstream vendor packaging, with premium full-service agency quotes plausibly **$60,000-$120,000+** if the build includes custom video, bespoke visual design, branching call simulation, accessibility/QA, and multiple review cycles. This is triangulated from Chapman/Defelice development ratios, Learning Carton's $25,000-$40,000 complex course package, Blue Carrot's $5,000-$50,000 per finished hour and simulation examples, and SweetRush's $120,000-$180,000 per hour for Level 3 high-complexity custom eLearning.
2. **Hosted delivery + tracking:** not included in most content-build quotes unless the vendor resells or bundles a platform. Expect a hosted LMS/lightweight distribution subscription or add-on. Public signals range from **$3,600/year for Articulate Reach Pro's 1,200 active-learner starting tier** to **$15,000+/year for larger LMS deployments**, with 4,000 learners usually pushing into sales-quoted enterprise tiers.

The highest-risk pricing variable is the **branching call simulation**. SweetRush explicitly notes a call-center scenario where a 10-minute learner experience required **five times** as much content because all branch paths had to be written and produced. That dynamic maps directly to the "scripted simulated call" requirement.

## Cost Model for the Fire-Department Training Scenario

### Assumptions

- **Seat time:** 30 minutes.
- **Complexity:** Chapman Level 3 / Defelice full engagement because of branching simulated call, interleaved checks, post-test, and custom product walkthrough.
- **Audience:** 4,000 personnel, likely distributed and possibly seasonal/rotating. If all train during one rollout month, active-user pricing spikes.
- **Delivery:** vendor-hosted LMS or lightweight LMS required; product-tour tools alone are insufficient.

### Recommended budget envelope

The following ranges are a synthesis from the sourced benchmarks above, not direct vendor quotes for this exact project.

| Line item | Lean / lower-market | Mainstream full-service | Premium / high-complexity |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Discovery, scripting, instructional design, storyboards | Included in below or **$5,000-$15,000** | **$10,000-$25,000** | **$25,000+** |
| 30-minute interactive module build | **$25,000-$40,000** | **$40,000-$90,000** | **$90,000-$120,000+** |
| Product walkthrough media / demo capture | **Included to $5,000** if simple video/screen capture | **$5,000-$20,000** if edited video plus interleaved checks | **$20,000+** if custom product sandbox/HTML demo platform/professional video |
| Branching simulated call | **Included in complex course floor** if modest branch count | **$10,000-$35,000** incremental if scripts, paths, VO, scoring, remediation are substantive | **$35,000+** if many branches, actors, custom UI, analytics, accessibility |
| Hosted delivery + admin dashboard | **$3,600-$15,000/year** depending on active learner tier and platform | **$12,000-$30,000/year** likely for 4,000-person rollout if enterprise LMS or Reach quote | **$30,000-$70,000+/year** for enterprise LMS, integrations, SSO, multi-portal, support |
| Maintenance / updates | Change orders or small retained hours | Annual update bank recommended | Larger retainer if product changes often |

The most procurement-defensible single-number planning range is **$50,000-$120,000 first-year total**, split roughly between **$40,000-$90,000 build** and **$10,000-$30,000 hosting/tracking/platform**. A low-end vendor could come in below this; a premium agency or enterprise LMS bundle can exceed it.

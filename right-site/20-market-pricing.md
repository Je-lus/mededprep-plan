# Market Pricing Benchmarks — Custom Async Interactive Product-Training E-Learning

**Purpose:** Establish what a RightSite-style training module (~30 min, highly interactive — branching call simulation + interleaved knowledge checks + interactive video + hosted admin tracking + certificate) costs on the open market, so MedEdPrep can price its proposal with a defensible anchor. All figures below are corroborated by 2+ independent sources unless explicitly flagged as **[SINGLE SOURCE]**.

---

## 1. The Two Pricing Languages: "Per Finished Hour/Minute" and "Per Project"

The eLearning industry quotes custom development two ways. Both reduce to the same underlying driver: **how many development hours go into one finished hour of seat time, which is set almost entirely by interactivity level.**

- The dominant industry metric is **cost per finished hour of instruction** (AllenComm explicitly calls this "the industry standard for pricing eLearning custom development").
- "Per finished minute" is the same number divided by 60 and is how smaller projects (like a 30-min module) get quoted.
- The single biggest swing factor in every source is **interactivity level** — described by AllenComm as "the single largest cost variable."

---

## 2. Development-Hour Ratios by Interactivity Level (the engine behind the price)

The canonical reference is the **Chapman Alliance study** (Bryan Chapman, 2010; ~250 organizations, ~4,000 learning professionals). It is old but still cited universally as the industry baseline. Modern agencies cite a compressed "80–300 hours per finished hour" band.

| Level | Description | Dev hours per finished hour |
|---|---|---|
| **Level 1 — Basic** | Content pages, text, test questions, graphics, basic audio/video; "click-next" PPT-style | ~49 hrs (low) / **~79 hrs avg** (Chapman); modern agencies cite ~72 hrs min |
| **Level 2 — Interactive** | Interactive exercises, animation, more sophisticated programming | **~184:1** (Chapman / modern agencies) |
| **Level 3 — Advanced** | Individualized interactions, **simulations, avatars, gamification, branching** | Up to **~716:1** (Chapman high end); modern agencies cite ~184+ hrs for "highly interactive" |
| Level 4 — Custom | All bells and whistles, full simulation + systems integration | Highest; rolled into the 300-hr cap by modern shops |

- Chapman ratios: corroborated across eLearning Industry, Christy Tucker / Experiencing Elearning, Thinkdom, and the original CEDMA PDF.
- Modern "80–300 hrs per finished hour" band: AllenComm + Bluecarrot.
- **Note the naming inconsistency:** Chapman uses Levels 1–3; modern agencies often add a Level 4. RightSite's ask (branching sim + avatars-of-action + gamified decisions) lands squarely in **Level 3** under either scheme.

**Why this matters for a 30-min module:** A 5-minute branching scenario can cost *more* than a 15-minute linear module. Per-minute averages collapse for short, high-interactivity work — "a 3-minute microlearning scenario can take as much design time as 10 minutes of standard content" (Bluecarrot). RightSite is short AND high-interactivity, the most expensive quadrant per minute.

## 3. Cost Per Finished Hour by Interactivity Level

Two independent framings, both consistent:

**Framing A — by interactivity tier (eLearningSolutionsLab):**

| Level | Cost per finished hour |
|---|---|
| Level 1 (Basic) | $5,000 – $10,000 |
| Level 2 (Intermediate) | $10,000 – $20,000 |
| Level 3 (Advanced) | $20,000 – $35,000 |
| Level 4 (Custom) | $35,000 – $50,000+ |

**Framing B — by customization style (Bluecarrot):**

| Style | Cost per finished hour |
|---|---|
| Template-based modules | ~$10,000 |
| Tailored modules | ~$15,000 |
| Fully experiential (advanced simulations) | ~$20,000 |

The two framings overlap cleanly at the Level 2–3 boundary (~$15K–$20K/finished hour for genuinely interactive work). A common rule-of-thumb anchor cited across sources: **$500 per finished minute = ~$30,000 for a 60-min course** as a baseline that scales up or down with interactivity (a $20/min vendor is shipping templates; a $120/min vendor is shipping scenario-driven, accessibility-compliant, multimedia work).

## 5. Media / Production Add-Ons (per finished minute)

RightSite is handing over raw demo footage + an existing presentation that needs "snipping and reassembly," so video production is a real line item:

| Element | Cost |
|---|---|
| AI voiceover (narration) | $2 – $5/finished min (Bluecarrot); $0 – $50/finished hr (eLSL) |
| Human voiceover | $20 – $150/finished min (Bluecarrot); pro US $300 – $500/finished hr (eLSL) |
| Synthetic AI avatar video | ~$50/finished min |
| 2D custom animation | ~$500/finished min (Bluecarrot); $3,000 – $8,000/min (eLSL — **flag: eLSL figure is an order of magnitude higher, likely full-custom**) |
| 3D animation | well beyond $500/min |
| Custom graphics | $50 – $150 per asset |

For RightSite, most "video" is **screen-capture of the real platform UI (or our clean-room HTML illusion)** — far cheaper than animation. The expensive part is the *interactivity authoring*, not the footage.

### Agency hourly rates (for build-vs-buy math)
- Instructional Designer: $50–$100/hr
- Multimedia Producer: $60–$150/hr
- Project Manager: $75–$150/hr

## 6. Recurring Hosting / Seat / Retainer Models (what "we host it + admin login" adds)

RightSite has **no LMS of their own** and wants a hosted admin dashboard with user list + completion stats. On the open market they would either (a) buy a SaaS LMS seat plan, or (b) pay a vendor to host. Benchmarks:

**SaaS LMS — per-user / flat-fee plans:**
| Platform | Pricing |
|---|---|
| TalentLMS | Core $69/mo, Grow $109/mo, Pro $179/mo, Enterprise custom; **per active user**, ~10% annual discount. Strong certification tracking. |
| LearnWorlds | $24/mo (Starter) → $249/mo (Learning Center); **flat monthly fee**, not per-user |
| Generic SaaS LMS | $5 – $10 per user / month (Bluecarrot) |

**Vendor-hosted / enterprise tiers (annual):**
| Tier | Annual cost |
|---|---|
| Commercial LMS (small) | $3,000 – $10,000/yr (AllenComm) |
| Mid-market LMS | $15,000 – $60,000/yr (AllenComm) |
| Enterprise LMS | $30,000+/yr (Bluecarrot); $60,000 – $200,000+/yr (AllenComm) |
| Open-source LMS (with staff/infra) | $20,000 – $80,000/yr (AllenComm) |
| Custom-built LMS | $200,000 – $500,000 + 12–24 mo build (AllenComm) |

**Authoring-tool licenses** (if RightSite tried to DIY): Articulate 360 ~$1,499/user/yr; Adobe Captivate ~$33.99/mo/user.

**Maintenance:** annual maintenance for custom-built apps/platforms runs **15–25% of original build cost** (AllenComm) — a standard recurring-revenue anchor for a hosted-platform retainer.

### The seat-math problem that sells the platform
Houston ~4,000 personnel; Dallas next. At a generic $5–$10/user/mo SaaS LMS, **4,000 seats = $20,000–$40,000/month = $240K–$480K/yr** just in seat licensing — before any content. That number is the strongest argument for a **flat-fee hosted MedEdPrep platform with unlimited seats + admin dashboard**, priced as an annual platform fee or per-department retainer rather than per-seat. It also reframes our content build as cheap relative to the seat-license alternative.

## 7. Translation: What RightSite's Module Would Cost on the Open Market

**The module (one ~30-min, Level 3, branching-sim + interleaved-questions + interactive-video + certificate):**

- A 30-min module at the **Level 3 / advanced-simulation** rate ($20,000–$35,000 per *finished hour*) = **~$10,000–$17,500** for content alone on a per-finished-hour basis.
- But short + high-interactivity inflates per-minute cost, and the branching simulation is the priciest element. Cross-checking against project ranges: a 60-min scored simulation is **$20,000–$23,000** (agency); a custom simulation/game-based project starts at **$40,000+** (agency). A focused 30-min branching sim realistically lands at **~$15,000–$30,000** on the open market from a competent agency, **$8,000–$18,000** from a freelance/small team.
- **Defensible open-market anchor for the RightSite content build: $15,000–$30,000** (agency), with simple-template comparators at the low end ($5K–$9K) and full game-based sims at the high end ($40K+).

**The hosted platform + admin login (the recurring layer):**

- Comparable commercial-LMS small/mid tiers: **$3,000–$60,000/yr.**
- Per-seat SaaS at Houston scale would be **$240K–$480K/yr** — which is why a flat-fee or per-department model is both more attractive to RightSite and more profitable/predictable for MedEdPrep.
- A maintenance/hosting retainer benchmarked at **15–25% of build cost** = roughly **$3,000–$7,500/yr** on a $15K–$30K build, scalable upward as departments are added.

**Bottom line for the proposal:**
- Content build (one module): anchor **$15K–$30K**, justified as Level 3 branching simulation work.
- Hosted platform + admin dashboard + certificates: recurring **flat annual platform fee** (anchored against $3K–$60K/yr commercial LMS tiers and the avoided $240K+/yr per-seat cost at fire-department scale).
- MedEdPrep's edge: the **gtc-demo → mededprep-demo → demo.mededprep.com** pattern (interactive video + interleaved questions + admin tracking + issued certificates) is already built, so our marginal cost is far below an agency building from zero — letting us price *at* market while delivering *above* market.

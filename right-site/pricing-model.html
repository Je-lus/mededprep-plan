Assumptions & notes
  • Build fee is one-time, charged in Year 1 only.
  • Per-department rollout fee is charged in the year a department launches — except the first department (Houston), whose initial standup is covered by the build fee. Every later department triggers one rollout fee in its launch year.
  • Launch-year certs for a department = headcount × completion rate (the onboarding wave).
  • Subsequent-year certs for an active department:
    • Recert OFF (onboarding model): headcount × turnover% × completion rate — only new hires certify.
    • Recert ON: headcount × completion rate (everyone re-certifies each year). To keep the model clear, recert ON does not add separate new-hire churn on top — the full headcount already captures everyone.
  • Platform floor is charged every year once at least one department is live.
  • MedEdPrep revenue = RightSite cost. They are the identical dollar figure shown from two sides. The perspective toggle only changes labels and framing — never the math.
  • LMS alternative = cumulative active personnel × LMS rate × 12 months — what RightSite would pay a commercial per-seat LMS to serve the same people. Savings = LMS cost − our cost.
  • Parametric expansion (optional) adds a new department of the chosen average headcount on the chosen cadence starting in the chosen year, through Year 10.

These are illustrative planning numbers for internal modeling only — not a quote or commitment.

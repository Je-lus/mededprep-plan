# Contract-Kit Counsel — Fit Analysis & Top Recommendations

**Need being scored:** A tech-transactions attorney (or small/boutique firm) to build a **reusable MSA + SOW + NDA + IP-ownership/assignment + license-back kit** for MedEdPrep's hosted custom-training business — first instance is the RightSite Health e-learning module (we host it, they get an admin login + completion tracking; we build a clean-room HTML "illusion" of their platform for training). The kit must be **template-grade and reusable** across future hosted-training deals, **founder-friendly**, priced for a **small Georgia company**, and ideally from counsel **in/near metro Atlanta**.

**What "good fit" actually requires here (the rubric):**
1. **Tech-transactions / SaaS-licensing depth** — must be comfortable drafting IP ownership + license-back, white-label/hosting, and data/admin-access terms, not just a generic MSA. (This is the clause set that separates a real fit from a general business lawyer.)
2. **Reusable-template mindset** — we want a *kit* we can re-skin per client, so a lawyer who already drafts **form agreements** is worth far more than one who bills a bespoke contract each time.
3. **Small-company economics** — flat-fee or modest hourly; no AmLaw-50 rate card for a template build.
4. **Founder-friendly + near Atlanta** — responsive to a founder-led small shop; metro Atlanta or ~1 hr NW.
5. **Clean-room awareness** — we reproduce a client's UX for training without copying code; counsel should be fluent in the IP-infringement / "look-and-feel" line so our license-back and indemnity language is sound.

Scoring is weighted toward (1) and (2) — they are the hardest to find and the highest-risk if missing.

- The named contact, **Gerry Balboni**, is independently corroborated (LinkedIn) as an *"experienced corporate lawyer for investors & tech companies — venture capital, M&A, and licensing software, SaaS, & Cloud"* at Krevolin & Horst. Two independent sources (firm page + his own profile) agree on the SaaS/licensing specialization.
- **Mid-size, not mega-firm:** Chambers-recognized (the firm's own page surfaces a "2026 Chambers USA — six partners / two practice areas recognized" banner, confirming current standing) but boutique enough to take a small founder-led client. This is the sweet spot between the Marietta generalists (cheap but thin on SaaS/IP) and the AmLaw firms (deep but over-budget) — it is the **only candidate found that satisfies rubric #1 AND #2 AND #3 simultaneously.**
- Atlanta-based (rubric #4).

**Likely fit concerns:**

**First-contact angle:** Email Balboni directly, lead with the match to *his own page's language*: "We're a small Georgia ed-tech company that hosts custom interactive training for enterprise clients. We need a **reusable form-agreement kit** — MSA + SOW + IP-ownership-with-license-back + white-label/hosting + admin-data terms — exactly the 'form agreements for software licensing and the provision of professional services' your SaaS page describes. First client is live; the kit will be reused per deal. Can you scope this as a flat or capped fee?" Naming his page's phrasing signals we did our homework and shortcuts the qualification call.

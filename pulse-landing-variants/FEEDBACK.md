## Hero + Nav

**Base:** Codex hero, with Opus and Sonnet elements mixed in.

**What works (Codex):**
- "Your Students Already Have Their Phones Out" headline — visually linked to the mockup
- The live session image / question format mockup — shows what the product actually looks like
- The phone element alongside the projector view — dual-device feel

**What to change:**
- Remove the session code / code element from the mockup
- The phone should be an ANIMATION on loop, not static — use brand animation assets (EKG waveform, shield pattern)
- Button contrast: white text on mint green (#36C98D) is hard to read — use dark/black text on green buttons instead. This applies to ALL CTAs across the entire page.

**Steal from Opus:**
- "Train. Track. Perform." tagline placement in the hero

**Steal from Sonnet:**
- General layout structure (but simplified — Sonnet was too busy)

## All Sections — Global Rules

These apply everywhere, not just one section:

1. **NO "100% FREE" badges/banners.** Pulse is free NOW but will have paid features later. Say "free" in the copy naturally but do NOT splash "100% FREE" as a badge, banner, or pill. That locks us into a promise we'll break.

2. **Button contrast fix.** White on mint green doesn't work. Use dark text (#000 or #111) on mint green buttons, or find a high-contrast pairing. Every CTA on every section.

3. **Pulse has its OWN question bank** — completely independent from MedEdPrep's 10.6K items. Do NOT claim shared content, shared item counts, or "powered by MedEdPrep's question bank." The MedEdPrep connection is about the TEAM and EXPERTISE, not shared questions.

4. **Trust bar / credibility rework.** Can reference MedEdPrep's track record and the team's experience, but not specific item counts from the other product.

5. **Don't surface backend/psychometric infrastructure** as marketing copy. IRT, bifactor models, metacognition data — that's internal moat, not student-facing messaging.

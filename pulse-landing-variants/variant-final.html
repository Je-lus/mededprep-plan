FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Pulse really free?

Yes. No credit card, no trial period.

The core classroom experience will always be free. Live games, leaderboards, student analytics, and the built-in question bank are included at no cost.

We plan to offer optional premium features in the future for instructors and students. Think: advanced game modes, expanded flashcard libraries.

The Pulse you use today to run your classroom? That stays free.

Does it work on student phones?

Yes. Pulse is mobile-first. It's built for the phones your students already have. Works on any modern browser, iOS or Android.

Students join with a session code or QR scan. The full experience works right in the browser with nothing to install. Native apps for the App Store and Google Play are in development.

What cert levels does it cover?

EMR through Critical Care. Every question in the bank is tagged by certification level, so you can filter content to match exactly where your students are.

Instructors can also set each student's exam level individually for more targeted analytics.

Can students see each other's scores?

On the leaderboard, students see each other under fun auto-generated nicknames, not real names (unless a student sets their nickname to their actual name). The competition is real, but nobody's put on the spot.

Instructors can view actual student names and detailed performance breakdowns in their analytics dashboard.

Do I need to create all my own content?

No. Pulse comes with a built-in EMS question bank mapped to NREMT domains and cert levels. You can run a session with zero prep.

Want to customize? You can also add your own questions to tailor content for your specific program.

Can I use this for NREMT exam prep?

Absolutely. Every question is mapped to the NHTSA EMS Education Standards taxonomy. Your analytics show performance against the same content domains that appear on the Registry exam.

Pulse is a reinforcement tool, not a substitute for dedicated test prep. But it's a powerful complement to your classroom instruction.

Is there a student limit per session?

No limit. Whether you have 8 students or 200, Pulse handles it.

We've designed the platform to scale from small skills labs to large lecture halls and conference events.

What's the difference between Pulse and MedEdPrep?

Pulse is the free classroom engagement and study tool: live games, flashcards, streaks, and quick analytics.

MedEdPrep is the full test prep platform with adaptive exams, diagnostic reports, NREMT readiness prediction, and school management tools.

They work great together, but Pulse stands on its own.

I'm not tech-savvy. Is this complicated?
If you can share a link, you can run Pulse. Launch a session, share the code, and your students are playing in under 60 seconds. No training, no setup wizard, no onboarding call.

We love hearing from instructors. Pulse is in active development and instructor feedback directly shapes what we build next.

Drop us a line at support@mededprep.com. We read every message.

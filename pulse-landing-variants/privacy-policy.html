Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 21, 2026 · Last Updated: May 21, 2026

Pulse by MedEdPrep ("Pulse," "we," "us," or "our") is a free, web-based gamified education platform for Emergency Medical Services (EMS) classrooms, operated by MedEdPrep. We take the privacy of our users -- instructors, students, and program administrators -- seriously.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, how we use and protect it, and what choices you have. It applies to all users of Pulse at pulse.mededprep.com.

The short version: We collect only what we need to run Pulse. We never sell your data. Your educational performance data stays between you, your instructor, and us.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide directly

  • Instructor accounts: Name, email address, and institution or program affiliation when you register. Instructors also select their EMS certification level (EMR, EMT, AEMT, Paramedic, etc.).
  • Student accounts: Display name, email address, and optionally a phone number when you create a persistent account. Students also provide their EMS certification level. A fun EMS-themed nickname and avatar are automatically generated for you (these are not based on biometric data). Students may also join game sessions using only a session code and a display name, without creating a full account.
  • School affiliation: When you join via a session code, QR code, or school invite code, you may be automatically affiliated with an instructor's school or program. This determines which instructor can view your performance data.
  • Session participation: Quiz answers, response times, scores, streaks, and other gameplay data generated during live sessions and solo study.
  • Conference mode participants: If you participate in a Pulse session at a conference or event, you can play anonymously using a temporary session token and display name. No account is required. If you choose to provide your email address after the session (to convert to a full account), we will collect it at that time.

Engagement and progress data

  • Performance metrics: Scores, accuracy rates, and response times broken down by NREMT content area, subtopic, and learning objective.
  • Study progress: Flashcard box levels (spaced repetition progress), correct streaks, and review schedules.
  • Engagement data: XP (experience points), daily streaks, achievement unlocks, prestige levels, and leaderboard rankings.
  • Virtual currency: Pulses (an in-app currency with no real-world monetary value), transaction history, and streak freeze purchases.

Information collected automatically

  • Device and browser information: Device type, operating system, browser type and version, screen resolution, and language preferences.
  • Usage data: Pages visited, features used, session duration, referring URLs, and general interaction patterns.
  • Network information: IP address (used for security and approximate geographic region -- we do not track precise location).

Device permissions

  • Camera (optional): If you use the Pulse mobile app to scan a QR code to join a session, we request camera access. This is used solely for QR code scanning and no images or video are captured, stored, or transmitted.

Information we do not collect

  • Payment information: Pulse is free. We do not collect credit card numbers, billing addresses, or any financial information.
  • Precise geolocation: We do not access your device's GPS or fine-grained location services.
  • Social media profiles: We do not require or access social media accounts.
  • Biometric data: Avatars are generated algorithmically from a random seed, not from photos or biometric data.
  • Health or medical information: Pulse is an educational tool. We do not collect health records, patient data, or clinical information of any kind.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Deliver the Pulse experience: Run live game sessions, display leaderboards, track streaks, and power spaced-repetition flashcards.
  • Provide performance analytics: Show instructors how their students perform by NREMT content area, by session, and over time -- so they can adjust their teaching.
  • Improve the platform: Understand how Pulse is used so we can fix bugs, improve features, and build what classrooms actually need.
  • Communicate with you: Send account-related notifications (such as session invitations or platform updates). We will never send unsolicited marketing emails.
  • Maintain security: Detect and prevent fraud, abuse, or unauthorized access.

We do not use your data to build advertising profiles. Pulse does not serve ads, and we do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to advertisers or data brokers. Period.

3. How We Share Your Information

We share your information only in the following limited circumstances:

  • With your instructor: If you are a student participating in Pulse sessions, your instructor can see your display name, quiz responses, scores, and performance analytics. This is the core purpose of Pulse -- giving instructors visibility into how their students are doing.
  • With service providers: We use a small number of trusted third-party services to operate Pulse:
    • SendGrid (email delivery): We use SendGrid to send account-related emails such as welcome messages, password resets, and session invitations. SendGrid processes recipient email addresses and tracks delivery status (sent, delivered, bounced). We do not use SendGrid for marketing emails.
    • Sentry (error monitoring): We use Sentry to detect and fix software errors. When an error occurs, Sentry may capture technical context including your user ID, email address, and role to help us diagnose the issue. This data is used solely for debugging and is retained by Sentry for up to 90 days.
    • Cloud hosting: Pulse is hosted on secure cloud infrastructure. Our hosting provider processes data on our behalf under strict contractual obligations.
    These providers access data only as needed to perform their services for us.
  • With MedEdPrep: Pulse is built and operated by MedEdPrep. We may share data with our parent organization for platform improvement, support, and research into educational effectiveness. MedEdPrep is bound by this same Privacy Policy.
  • When required by law: We may disclose information if required by a valid legal process such as a subpoena, court order, or government request.
  • In aggregated or de-identified form: We may share aggregated, non-identifiable data for research, reporting, or educational purposes (for example, overall pass-rate trends by content area). This data cannot be used to identify any individual student.

We do not sell your personal information. Not now, not ever. We do not share your data with advertisers, data brokers, or any third party for their own marketing purposes.

4. Educational Records and FERPA

Because Pulse is used in educational settings, some of the data we process -- particularly student performance data -- may qualify as "education records" under the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA).

Here is how we approach FERPA compliance:

  • School official exception: When an EMS program or institution directs its instructors and students to use Pulse, we act as a "school official" with a legitimate educational interest. We use student data only for the educational purposes authorized by the institution.
  • Direct control by instructors: Instructors control which students participate in their sessions and what data is generated. We do not independently contact students or use their education records for purposes unrelated to the educational services we provide.
  • No re-disclosure: We do not disclose personally identifiable information from education records to third parties except as described in this policy and as permitted under FERPA.
  • Institutional agreements: If your EMS program or institution requires a data-sharing agreement, addendum, or FERPA compliance letter, we are happy to work with you. Contact us at support@mededprep.com.

For program directors and administrators: We understand the importance of protecting student records. If you need documentation for your compliance team, please reach out. We will provide what you need.

5. Data Security

We take the security of your data seriously and implement appropriate technical and organizational safeguards, including:

  • Encryption in transit: All data transmitted between your browser and our servers is encrypted using TLS (HTTPS).
  • Password security: Passwords are hashed using bcrypt with a high work factor. We never store or have access to your plain-text password.
  • Access controls: Access to personal data is restricted to authorized personnel who need it to operate and maintain Pulse. Instructor analytics are scoped to their own school's students only.
  • Security headers and rate limiting: We use industry-standard security headers and rate limiting to protect against common web attacks.
  • Secure authentication: Session tokens are stored as secure, HTTP-only cookies and expire after 7 days of inactivity.

No system is perfectly secure. While we work hard to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security. If we become aware of a security breach that affects your personal information, we will notify affected users and, where applicable, the relevant institution in accordance with applicable law.

6. Children's Privacy

Pulse is designed for EMS education, and the vast majority of EMS students are adults (18 and older). Pulse is not intended for children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

In the limited cases where minors between 13 and 17 may be enrolled in EMS education programs, we rely on the educational institution and the instructor to obtain any necessary parental or guardian consent before allowing those students to participate.

If you believe we have inadvertently collected information from a child under 13, please contact us at support@mededprep.com and we will promptly delete it.

7. Cookies and Tracking

Pulse uses a minimal number of cookies to operate:

  • Authentication cookie: A secure, HTTP-only cookie that keeps you logged in. This cookie contains a signed session token and expires after 7 days. It is essential for the platform to function.

We do not use advertising cookies, third-party tracking pixels, or cross-site tracking of any kind. We do not participate in interest-based advertising networks. There are no optional or analytics cookies to accept or reject.

Our error monitoring service (Sentry) may use local storage for performance telemetry. This data is used solely for debugging and is not shared with advertisers.

8. Data Retention

We retain your information only as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy:

  • Active accounts: Your data is retained for as long as your account is active.
  • Session-only participants: If a student joins a session with only a display name (no account), their gameplay data is associated with that session and the instructor's records. This data is retained for the duration the instructor maintains their account.
  • After account deletion: To request account deletion, email us at support@mededprep.com. We will remove your personal information within 30 days. Some data may be retained in anonymized, aggregated form for research and platform improvement.
  • Backup and legal holds: Backup copies may persist for a limited period after deletion, and data subject to a legal hold will be retained as required.

If you are an instructor and want to request deletion of all student data associated with your sessions, contact us and we will work with you.

9. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have certain rights regarding your personal information:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correction: Ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Deletion: Request that we delete your personal information, subject to legal and contractual obligations.
  • Data portability: Request your data in a commonly used, machine-readable format.
  • Opt out of communications: Unsubscribe from non-essential emails at any time using the link provided in those emails.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at support@mededprep.com. We will respond within 30 days.

For students whose data is managed by an institution: If your EMS program directed you to use Pulse, you may need to contact your program or institution to exercise certain rights over your education records, as FERPA grants those rights to the institution.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technology, legal requirements, or for other operational reasons.

When we make changes, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. For material changes, we will provide additional notice -- such as an in-app notification or email to registered instructors.

We encourage you to review this page periodically. Your continued use of Pulse after a change to this policy constitutes acceptance of the updated terms.

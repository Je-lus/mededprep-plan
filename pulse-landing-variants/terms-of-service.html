1. Description of the Service

Pulse is a free, web-based, gamified education platform designed for Emergency Medical Services (EMS) classrooms. The Service provides:

  • Live game sessions — Instructors launch multiplayer quiz games (Classic, Lightning, Survival, Team Battle, Jeopardy, and more) that students join from their phones via session codes or QR codes.
  • Solo study — Spaced repetition flashcards and practice questions for individual review between classes.
  • Engagement features — XP, streak tracking, achievement patches, leaderboards, and an in-app currency called "Pulses." Pulses are earned through participation and can be spent on streak freezes. Pulses have no real-world monetary value and are not redeemable for cash.
  • Analytics dashboards — Instructor-facing tools showing performance by NREMT content area, by student, and over time.
  • Question bank — A built-in EMS question bank with multiple question types (multiple choice, true/false, select-all, image identification, ordering, hotspot, and more), plus the ability for instructors to import their own questions.
  • Conference mode — A special mode for trade shows and events where participants can play anonymously using temporary session tokens without creating an account.

Pulse is mobile-first and browser-based. No app download is required. Students access sessions through any modern web browser.

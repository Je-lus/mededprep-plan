Frequently Asked Questions

Is Pulse really free?
Yes. Free for instructors, free for students. No credit card, no trial period, no catch. We built Pulse to be in every EMS classroom — not behind a paywall.
Do I need to create all my own content?
No. Pulse comes with EMS questions mapped to NREMT domains and cert levels. You can also add your own questions if you want to customize for your program.
Does it work on student phones?
Yes. Pulse is mobile-first — built for the phones your students already have. Works on any modern browser, iOS or Android. Students join with a session code, no app install required for games.
Can I use this for NREMT exam prep?
Absolutely. Every question is mapped to the NHTSA EMS Education Standards taxonomy. Your analytics show performance against the same domains that appear on the Registry exam.
What's the difference between Pulse and MedEdPrep?
Pulse is the free classroom engagement and study tool — live games, flashcards, and quick analytics. MedEdPrep is the full test prep platform — adaptive exams, diagnostic reports, and school management tools.
I'm not tech-savvy. Is this complicated?
If you can share a link, you can run Pulse. Instructors launch a session, students scan a code. The whole setup takes under a minute. No IT department needed.

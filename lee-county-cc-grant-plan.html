Critical Care Paramedic Cohort — Funding & Proposal Plan

How Lee County EMS can fund ~13 medics through a Critical Care Paramedic certification using an outside grant — built around the captain's exact ask: a named grant + a named program = high approval odds.

Prepared for: Sam & the Lee County cohort Employer: Lee County (county EMS) Cohort: ~13 paramedics Program: MedEdPrep CCP

The recommended play

Florida EMS County Grant funding a MedEdPrep accredited CCP-C cohort — money Lee County already receives every year, with no match and no deadline.

Lee County automatically receives an annual EMS Trust Fund county distribution (§401.113(2)(a), F.S.) with no application window, no competitive scoring, and no cash match. Training is a documented allowable use: a Florida county (Levy) put "Paramedic tuition and travel for instruction" directly on its county-grant budget page. The path is simply a budget line — "Training – CCP-C tuition (MedEdPrep)" — plus a Board of County Commissioners resolution certifying the cohort "improves and expands" the EMS system. This satisfies the captain's ask cleanly: a named grant (EMS County Grant) + a named program (MedEdPrep CCP-C), where the department controls the money.

✅
Verified by follow-up research (June 2026): Three eligibility unknowns were run down. County Grant — confirmed fundable for training, with FL precedent. Matching Grant — confirmed it has a dedicated training scoring track; strong fallback. AFG and CareerSource — both turned out to be blocked for a county-government EMS division (see ranking below). The plan is now built on the two Florida DOH paths.
📋
The one soft spot on the County Grant: funds must "improve and expand" EMS and cannot cover "on-going costs." A one-time cohort that adds a new clinical capability (critical-care transport) fits; a recurring annual CE line would not. One email to FL DOH settles the judgment call — see Section 2.

Executive Summary

3 sentences
Sample language "This proposal requests department sponsorship to send a cohort of 13 Lee County paramedics through a Critical Care Paramedic (CCP-C) certification course delivered by MedEdPrep, a Georgia state-licensed and accredited critical care training program, with the cost offset by an external grant (FEMA Assistance to Firefighters Grant; alternate: Florida EMS Matching Grant). Outside funding has already been identified, which materially improves approval odds and reduces net department cost to roughly the grant match plus in-kind instructor and backfill time. The result is a new in-house critical-care interfacility transport (CCT-IFT) capability, improved retention, and CAPCE-accredited CE hours that double as Florida recertification credit."

Funding Source — the named grant

Primary — Florida EMS County Grant "Lee County already receives an annual EMS Trust Fund county distribution (§401.113(2)(a), F.S.) that may be spent to 'increase existing levels of EMS.' This proposal places a one-time 'Training — CCP-C tuition (MedEdPrep)' line on the county's EMS County Grant budget (Form DH 1684), funding a new critical-care transport capability rather than a recurring operating cost. There is no cash match, no competitive scoring, and no application deadline; it requires only a Board of County Commissioners resolution certifying the funds improve and expand the county pre-hospital EMS system and do not supplant current expenditures. (Precedent: Levy County funded paramedic tuition this same way.)"
Alternate — FL EMS Matching Grant (DH 1767) "If the County Grant is unavailable, the department applies to the Florida DOH Bureau of EMS Matching Grant (75% state / 25% department cash match), which has a dedicated training scoring track (Item 12). A licensed Lee County EMS agency is eligible, and the county's own EMS medical director signs the continuing-education affirmation (Item 9). The application will be framed on patient-outcome impact (critical-care/interfacility transport quality), which is how training projects are scored. Next window: FY2027-28, ~Q3 2026."

4 What MedEdPrep must produce to be the fundable program

This is the half MedEdPrep controls — and the packet is useless without it. ★ = gating asset.

Georgia state school license + accredited CC program documentation The credential that makes you fundable now
This — not CAPCE — is what establishes MedEdPrep as a named, accredited training program for the Florida grants and the CCP-C credential pathway. Provide the GA license/accreditation number and a short description.
CAPCE accreditation In final stage — not a blocker
In its last revisions. Neither grant nor the CCP-C credential depends on it. Its specific value: once issued, completions auto-report as Florida recert CE via CEBroker. Mark "in progress, expected [date]" in the packet; the cohort can start without it.
SAM.gov Unique Entity ID (UEI) No longer needed
Was mandatory for AFG — but AFG is now ruled out. Both Florida grants are state-administered and don't require SAM.gov. Skip unless a federal path reopens.
IBSC/BCCTPC curriculum-alignment letter
States the curriculum maps to the CCP-C (and optionally FP-C) exam blueprint and graduates are CCP-C exam-eligible.
One-page capability statement
Company overview, SAM.gov UEI, NAICS code, core competencies, pass-rate / past-performance data if available.
Detailed course outline with CEH mapping
Module-by-module contact hours mapped to Florida recert (30 hrs biennial incl. 2 hrs pediatric); note CEBroker auto-reporting begins once CAPCE finalizes.
Cohort pricing sheet
Per-medic and volume/cohort rate (13 students); state whether the IBSC CCP-C exam voucher is bundled or quoted separately.
Signed W-9 + proof of insurance
Standard vendor paperwork the department's grants/finance office will require.
Bundle into one turnkey vendor packet
So the department can paste it straight into the application. Mark any pending items (CAPCE #, UEI) "in progress, expected [date]."

5 Open questions — who has to confirm

The three eligibility unknowns have now been researched and resolved ✓. What remains is internal-to-Lee-County confirmation plus one DOH email.

✓ RESOLVED — FL DOH County & Matching Grants

Both fund training. The EMS County Grant can carry a CCP-C tuition line (Florida precedent: Levy County). The Matching Grant has a dedicated training scoring track (Item 12); county EMS is an eligible applicant; 75/25 cash match; the county's own medical director signs. Only open item: one email to confirm the "one-time enhancement vs. ongoing cost" call on the County Grant and pin the FY2027-28 Matching window.

✕ RESOLVED — FEMA AFG: not eligible

AFG's applicant definition requires fire-based or nonprofit EMS and excludes "local agencies / subsets of a governmental entity." A county-government third-service EMS division is neither. CCP training cost was eligible; the applicant is not. Removed from the plan. (Only theoretical workaround: a Lee County fire-based agency applies and includes county medics where permissible — not worth pursuing as primary.)

✕ RESOLVED — CareerSource / WIOA: not eligible

Triple-blocked: Florida IWT guidelines expressly exclude "county governmental entities"; cert/exam-prep and test fees are disallowed costs; and ITA funding would require MedEdPrep on the FL ETPL (≈2-yr in-state operating history, which a young Georgia company can't meet). Removed from the plan.

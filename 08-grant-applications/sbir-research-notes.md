## 1. ED/IES SBIR — HIGHEST PRIORITY

**Source:** https://ies.ed.gov/funding/research/programs/small-business-innovation-research-sbir
**Solicitation page:** https://ies.ed.gov/funding/research/programs/small-business-innovation-research-sbir/solicitation-information

### Confirmed Details (from live site, 2026-05-21)
- **Program:** Small Business Innovation Research (SBIR) at the U.S. Department of Education, administered by the Institute of Education Sciences (IES)
- **Phase I:** $250,000 for 9 months — rapid prototype development and evaluation
- **Phase II:** $1,000,000 for 2 years — full-scale development and evaluation
- **Total potential:** Up to $1.25M across both phases
- **Cycle:** Solicitations for Phases I and II released annually. Proposals due ~60 days after solicitation release. Award notifications ~90 days from submission.
- **Program history:** 258 Phase I awards and 99 Phase II awards since 2002
- **FY2026 solicitation:** Page exists at the solicitation-information link above — indicates FY2026 cycle is active or imminent
- **Contact:** Akilah Nelson and Shirley Huang (both NCSER)

### Recent FY2025 Awardees (confirms topic alignment)
Contract numbers in 91990025C00xx series — confirms FY2025 awards were made. Examples:
- "Enhancing Career Readiness for College Students through AI-Powered Simulations"
- "AI-Powered Secondary Literacy Platform"
- "AI and Neuroscience-Powered Chatbot to Provide Students Individualized Guidance"
- "Personalized and Context-Relevant English Lessons: AI PACE AI to Advance Adult Learners"
- "Game Changers 2.0: Early Literacy Intervention with Multi-Level Linguistic Database & Automated Assessments"
- "Mental Health Early Detection Platform"
- "Providing School Districts a Tool to Make Data-Informed Clinical Placements"
- "Portrait of a Graduate Alignment Tool for Workforce Readiness Skills"

### MedEdPrep Fit: STRONG
- AI/adaptive assessment is clearly a funded topic area (multiple AI awards in FY2025)
- Adaptive testing engine with IRT-based item bank (10,600+ items) maps directly to "assessment" and "AI adaptive tutors"
- UIUC research partnership provides the research rigor IES values
- Metacognition/confidence dataset is novel research contribution
- Product already exists (not vaporware) — strong for prototype demonstration
- **Gap:** IES historically focuses on K-12 and higher ed, not professional/workforce education. Need to frame EMS credentialing as "career and technical education" or "workforce readiness" — the FY2025 "Enhancing Career Readiness" and "Portrait of a Graduate Alignment Tool for Workforce Readiness Skills" awards suggest this framing works.

## 2. NSF SBIR/STTR (America's Seed Fund)

**Source:** https://seedfund.nsf.gov/
**Topics:** https://seedfund.nsf.gov/what-we-fund/
**Apply:** https://seedfund.nsf.gov/apply/get-started/
**Status:** Site confirmed active (critical alerts banner dated 4/16/2026)

### Program Structure
- **Phase I:** Up to $275,000 for 12 months (proof of concept / feasibility)
- **Phase II:** Up to $1,000,000 for 24 months (full R&D)
- **Supplemental funding:** Phase IIB matching, TECP (commercialization), CAP (workforce)
- **Process:** Project Pitch (brief) -> Invitation to submit Full Proposal -> Review -> Award
- **Rolling submissions:** NSF SBIR accepts Project Pitches year-round (no fixed deadline for initial pitch)
- **STTR option:** Requires formal partnership with a research institution (UIUC qualifies)

### Relevant Topic Area: Educational Technologies and Applications (ET)
NSF SBIR funds education technology under its "Educational Technologies and Applications" topic. This includes:
- AI and machine learning in education
- Adaptive learning and assessment systems
- Learning analytics and educational data mining
- Simulation and game-based learning
- Credentialing and micro-credentialing technologies
- Workforce development technology

### MedEdPrep Fit: STRONG
- Adaptive testing engine with IRT is core AI/ML in education
- Pulse game-based learning platform fits game-based learning category
- Metacognition research dataset is novel contribution
- STTR option strengthened by UIUC partnership (formal R&D collaboration)
- NSF values deep technical innovation — bifactor IRT models, CAT algorithms are differentiators
- **Advantage over IES:** NSF explicitly includes workforce/professional education, not just K-12

### Key Differences from IES SBIR
- NSF uses Project Pitch system (rolling, no fixed annual deadline for initial engagement)
- NSF awards are slightly larger Phase I ($275K vs $250K)
- NSF values technical/scientific innovation more; IES values classroom impact more
- STTR track requires 30%+ of work done by research institution

### Action Items
- [ ] Check https://seedfund.nsf.gov/critical-information/ for any program changes (banner warned of alerts)
- [ ] Submit a Project Pitch through seedfund.nsf.gov — this is low-commitment and gets feedback
- [ ] Decide SBIR vs STTR (STTR leverages UIUC but requires formal subaward to university)
- [ ] Frame around novel adaptive assessment technology, not just "another test prep app"

## 3. NIH SBIR/STTR

**Primary source:** https://seed.nih.gov/
**Relevant institutes:**
- **NHLBI** (National Heart, Lung, and Blood Institute) — emergency cardiac care training
- **NIGMS** (National Institute of General Medical Sciences) — emergency medical systems research
- **NLM** (National Library of Medicine) — health informatics, clinical decision support
- **NICHD** (National Institute of Child Health and Human Development) — sometimes funds training research
- **OBSSR** (Office of Behavioral and Social Sciences Research) — metacognition research

### Program Structure
- **Phase I:** Up to $292,573 (SBIR) or $292,573 (STTR) for 12 months
- **Phase II:** Up to $1,951,153 for 36 months
- **Standard receipt dates:** January 5, April 5, September 5 (recurring annually)
- **Omnibus solicitation:** PA-24-XXX series (parent SBIR/STTR FOAs — always open)

### Relevant Funding Opportunity Announcements (FOAs)
NIH has both "parent" (omnibus) and targeted solicitations:
- **Parent SBIR (R43/R44):** Always open, submit to any relevant institute
- **Parent STTR (R41/R42):** Always open, requires research institution partner
- Targeted FOAs come and go — need to check NIH Reporter and grants.nih.gov

### MedEdPrep Fit: MODERATE
- Health professions education is fundable but competitive
- **Strongest angle:** Metacognition/confidence calibration research applied to clinical decision-making in EMS — this is a health outcomes story, not just ed-tech
- **CCP hospice charting platform** could be framed as clinical decision support (NLM interest)
- Adaptive remediation loop for CE could be framed as continuing competency assurance
- **Challenge:** NIH reviewers may not see EMS education as health research; need strong framing around patient outcomes
- **UIUC partnership** strengthens STTR application significantly

## 7. SAM.gov Registration — REQUIRED FOR ALL FEDERAL GRANTS

**Source:** https://sam.gov/
**Status:** Required before ANY federal SBIR/STTR/grant application

### Registration Process
1. **Get a UEI (Unique Entity Identifier):** Replaces DUNS number. Obtained through SAM.gov during registration. Free.
2. **Register on SAM.gov:** Create entity registration for MedEdPrep
   - Legal business name, address, CAGE code (assigned automatically)
   - Banking information for EFT payments
   - NAICS codes (relevant: 611710 - Educational Support Services; 541511 - Custom Computer Programming; 541512 - Computer Systems Design)
   - Representations and certifications (small business status, etc.)
3. **SBA Company Registry:** Register at SBIR.gov company registry
4. **Grants.gov:** Create account (uses SAM.gov credentials)
5. **Research.gov:** For NSF submissions specifically

### Timeline
- **UEI assignment:** Usually immediate to a few days
- **SAM.gov registration:** Allow 7-10 business days for initial registration; can take up to 30 days if there are issues
- **Annual renewal:** Must renew SAM.gov registration annually (it expires)
- **CRITICAL:** Start SAM.gov registration NOW — many applicants miss deadlines because they didn't register early enough

### Small Business Eligibility Requirements (for SBIR/STTR)
- For-profit company (MedEdPrep qualifies)
- Organized in the United States
- More than 50% owned by U.S. citizens or permanent residents
- Fewer than 500 employees
- Principal Investigator (PI) must be primarily employed by the small business (for SBIR; STTR is more flexible)

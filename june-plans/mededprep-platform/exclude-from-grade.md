# Exclude Questions from Grade

**Priority:** P0
**Requested by:** Charles Folt (Kansas EMS Educator)
**Touches:** Instructor Exams, Exit Exams, High Stakes Exam Form

## Problem

Charles Folt wants to supply his own questions and have us build his exams, but some questions need to be "beta" — included in the exam for data collection but NOT factored into the student's displayed grade until they're proven reliable.

## Proposed Solution

- Add a toggle/button on the left side of each question in the exam editor
- When toggled, that question is excluded from grade calculation
- On the student list page, the grade adjusts to reflect only non-excluded questions
- On the High Stakes Exam Form, populate the "Modifications made to exam (if any)" section with a list of excluded questions

## Scope

- Exam editor UI (add exclude toggle per question)
- Grade calculation logic (skip excluded questions)
- Student list page (recalculated grade display)
- High Stakes Exam Form (auto-populate excluded questions into modifications section)

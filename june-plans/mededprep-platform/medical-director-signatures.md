# Medical Director Digital Signatures

**Priority:** P0
**Requested by:** Medical director (from recent meeting)
**Touches:** High Stakes Exam Form, Instructor Exams, Exit Exams

## Problem

Medical directors need a way to formally review and sign off on exams. Currently there's no digital signature or review trail on the High Stakes Exam Form.

## Proposed Solution

- Add a "Review & Sign" button accessible to medical directors on exam pages
- When clicked, the medical director's digital signature and timestamp are recorded
- This signature persists on the High Stakes Exam Form permanently
- Same capability for the Program Director role
- No tracking of how long they reviewed or what they looked at — just the sign-off

## Deliverables

- Medical Director review/sign button on exam view
- Program Director review/sign button on exam view
- Digital signature + timestamp display on High Stakes Exam Form
- Persistent storage of signatures (not clearable)

## What CoAssist Does That JetPack Doesn't (and Shouldn't)

JetPack is purpose-built for accreditors. It is the right tool for CoAEMSP's internal workflow. These are program-side needs that fall outside that scope:

| Program Need | What CoAssist Provides |
|---|---|
| **Compliance readiness** | AI-powered gap analysis against 80+ pre-loaded CoAEMSP standards |
| **Self-study preparation** | AI-assisted narrative drafting from program data |
| **Personnel management** | Credential tracking for 8+ roles with expiration alerts |
| **Student outcomes** | NREMT pass rates, retention, placement -- with 70% threshold alerts |
| **Clinical operations** | Site/affiliate management, preceptor credentials, agreement tracking |
| **Advisory committees** | Meeting management, QR check-in, minutes, action items |
| **Graduate & employer surveys** | Automated distribution and response analytics |
| **Site visit readiness** | 40+ item prep checklist with countdown roadmap |
| **Appendix generation** | Auto-generated Appendices E, F, G, H |

These are the day-to-day operations that produce the data CoAEMSP needs. JetPack receives the output. CoAssist manages the work that creates it.

## Better Data In, Better Decisions Out

When programs are organized, CoAEMSP benefits directly:

- **More consistent submissions** -- CoAssist standardizes how programs collect and organize data, reducing variance in what JetPack receives
- **Fewer incomplete filings** -- Gap analysis catches problems before submission, not after
- **Accurate outcome data** -- Automated tracking replaces manual spreadsheets and guesswork
- **Faster review cycles** -- Well-prepared self-studies require less back-and-forth

JetPack's value to CoAEMSP increases when the data flowing into it is clean, complete, and consistently formatted. CoAssist is how that happens at scale.

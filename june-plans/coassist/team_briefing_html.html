01. Background

What Is JetPack?

JetPack is an accreditation management platform built by Virtual Atlantic, a company that has spent the better part of the last decade building software exclusively for accrediting bodies. Their tagline: "The Only Accreditation Management Platform Designed Exclusively for Accreditors."

  • Clients: Exclusively accrediting bodies (AACP, IACBE, NAAB, etc.). Zero program-level clients.
  • EMS Experience: None. Their current verticals are pharmacy, business education, architecture, and Christian schools.
  • Pricing: Enterprise, quote-only. Comparable competitors list at $24,000/month.
From JetPack's Website

"Accreditors and the end users they accredit are completely different types of entities with very different needs; VA knows that."

02. The News

The CoAEMSP Announcement

On June 1, 2026, CoAEMSP sent an official email announcing JetPack as their mandatory AMS. The rollout will be iterative, with full implementation anticipated in Summer 2027.

Dr. George Hatch, CoAEMSP Executive Director

"CoAEMSP is excited to take this important step to create a comprehensive data management strategy that improves efficiency and value for programs and accreditation teams alike."

03. Core Concept

Accreditor vs. Program

This is the core of our strategy. JetPack serves the ACCREDITOR. CoAssist serves the PROGRAM. These are fundamentally different products serving different sides of the exact same process.
JetPack is The IRS

Where you submit

The system that receives your forms, processes them, tracks your compliance, and issues official decisions.

CoAssist is TurboTax

Where you prepare

The tool that organizes your data, tracks your students, flags problems before you file, and generates the documents.

The IRS building a better portal does not put TurboTax out of business. It makes TurboTax more necessary, because now there is a strict, structured digital system programs must prepare for.

CoAssist Program Workspace

  • Manages daily student outcomes (NREMT, retention)
  • Tracks clinical sites, preceptors, and expirations
  • Runs AI-assisted gap analysis against 80+ standards
  • Generates the self-study and auto-builds appendices
Export / Data Handoff

JetPack Accreditor Portal

  • Receives organized submissions from programs
  • Assigns peer reviewers and site visitors
  • Manages board deliberations and decisions
  • Monitors cross-program benchmarking

05. Strategy

The Opportunity

Before this announcement, EMS programs had no mandatory digital submission system. Accreditation was spreadsheets, emails, and Word documents.

After this announcement, every single EMS education program in the country is about to face a mandatory new digital system. Summer 2027 is a hard deadline. Program directors must now organize their data, map it to standards, and transition from manual workflows to a structured digital process.

This isn't a threat—it's a growth catalyst. Our entire addressable market just got a mandatory deadline. CoAssist is the free answer to helping them meet it.

# Graduate Retention Calculations

**Priority:** P1
**Touches:** CoAssist — student tracking, reporting

## Problem

CoAEMSP has a rule: if a student drops out within the first 10% of class time, they do not count against the program's graduation rate numbers. CoAssist doesn't currently account for this.

## Changes Needed

1. **"Students Started" field** — Should reflect students present on day one of class, not just enrolled
2. **Add clarifying text** to the "Students Started" and related fields explaining what counts
3. **10% retention threshold** — If a student drops within the first 10% of class time, they are excluded from graduation rate calculations
4. **Graduate percentage calculations** — Adjust to exclude early drops per the 10% rule

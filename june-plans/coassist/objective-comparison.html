Readout

The products are not mirror-image competitors. JetPack is strongest on accreditor workflow orchestration; CoAssist is strongest on program-side preparation, operations, and compliance maintenance.

JetPack advantage Peer review, site visitor assignment, and commission decision workflow are accreditor-side functions CoAssist was not designed to own.
CoAssist advantage Program operations, evidence preparation, AI drafting, and compliance gap analysis sit before the submission event.
Corrected cross-program view CoAssist supports per-program deep-dive compliance and an admin dashboard with cross-program visibility.

Strategic Lead Areas

Side-by-side view of differentiated strengths.

Where JetPack Leads

These are areas where JetPack serves accreditor-side needs CoAssist was not designed to address.

  • Peer review coordination: assigning reviewers and managing review workflows (vainc.com/meet-jetpack).
  • Commission/board decision workflows: programs do not make accreditation decisions (vainc.com/meet-jetpack).
  • Deeper accreditor-level trend analysis: JetPack is positioned for accreditor-wide benchmarking; CoAssist has cross-program compliance monitoring via its admin dashboard (vainc.com/meet-jetpack).
  • Multi-accreditation-type support: CE/CME, programmatic, and institutional accreditation. CoAssist is EMS-specific by design (vainc.com/meet-jetpack).
  • SSO: on CoAssist's roadmap, not yet built (Software Advice).
  • Salesforce/QuickBooks integration: not central to EMS program operations (Software Advice).

Where CoAssist Leads

These are areas where CoAssist serves program-side needs JetPack was not designed to address.

  • AI-assisted self-study narrative drafting: accreditors receive documents; they do not help programs write them.
  • AI-powered gap analysis: identifies compliance risks before submission.
  • Student outcome tracking with threshold alerts: internal program operations data.
  • Personnel and credential management: 8+ roles with expiration tracking.
  • Clinical site, affiliate, and preceptor management: program-side field operations.
  • Automated graduate and employer surveys: including 11-domain RAM support.
  • Auto-generated appendices: E, F, G, and H are prepared by programs.
  • Cross-program admin dashboard: per-program deep-dive compliance plus cross-program monitoring for administrators.
  • Free pricing: JetPack is enterprise software sold to accreditors at enterprise pricing.

Methodology Notes

Interpretation rules used in this comparison.

  1. JetPack features are sourced from public materials only. No screenshots of the program-facing portal exist publicly. Listed JetPack features come from accreditor-side marketing and listing pages.
  2. CoAssist features are based on the built product.
  3. "No" in the JetPack column for program operations features means "not part of JetPack's stated purpose or feature set." It does not mean JetPack's portal definitely cannot display this data; there is simply no evidence it does, and Virtual Atlantic positions these as outside its scope.
  4. "Unknown" means no public source confirms or denies the capability. These are not counted as wins or losses for either product.
  5. This comparison does not include internal technical metrics such as test counts, architecture details, or HIPAA compliance claims, because those are not relevant to what each product does for its users.

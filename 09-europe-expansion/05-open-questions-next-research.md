# Open Questions & Next-Round Research Briefs

**Date:** 2026-06-19

## Highest-value digs

### A. UK market sizing (the prize)
- Exact paramedic population (HCPC register count + trend), plus EMTs/ECAs/student paramedics.
- Who actually *buys* education: NHS ambulance trusts, universities running paramedic degrees, private/independent ambulance providers, individual registrants.
- Procurement reality: how trusts/universities buy LMS/CPD (frameworks, tender processes, budget cycles).
- Pricing norms: what subscription/seat/site-license prices the market bears.

### B. Entry model decision: B2B platform vs B2C content
- For B2B: do UK schools/trusts want a platform they load their own JRCALC content into? Who are the incumbent LMS vendors there?
- For B2C: realistic willingness-to-pay for re-authored CPD content given free NHS eLfH baseline.

### C. Content re-authoring cost & path
- Scope of clinical delta US → UK (JRCALC) and US → Ireland (PHECC): how much is net-new vs adaptable.
- Textbook/source landscape for UK/Ireland paramedic content (licensing vs original authoring).
- What a "European expert paramedic" hire/contractor looks like — role, cost, where to find (the EMS2027 floor is one channel).

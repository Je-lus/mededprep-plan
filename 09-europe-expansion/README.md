# 09 — Europe Expansion

*Created 2026-06-19. Source: research session scoping EMS2027 (Ireland) attendance + whether MedEdPrep can branch into Europe.*

**Status:** Early scoping. No commitment, no spend yet. This folder exists to hold sorted research so we don't re-derive it and so future research agents have a foundation to build on.

**Bottom line (one paragraph):** MedEdPrep *can* branch into Europe, but only into the English-speaking, paramedic-led ("Anglo-American model") cluster — **UK first, Ireland second**. The US accreditation moat (CAPCE/NREMT) does **not** transfer. The winning entry is **platform-first** (the instructor-first LMS travels; US clinical content does not). EMS2027 in Killarney, Ireland (May 10–12, 2027) is a cheap, well-targeted way to scout exactly this segment.

**Founder direction on record (2026-06-19):** Jeramey is OK with taking the *platform* and **re-authoring the content from scratch** for the European market — new clinical guidelines, different textbooks, recruiting a European expert paramedic / doing the research himself. Explicitly framed as a **later** initiative, not a now thing. See `04-entry-strategy-and-fit.md`.

# EMS2027 (European EMS Congress) — Event Profile & Budget

**Date:** 2026-06-19
**Classification:** Internal expansion scouting
**Status:** 2027 registration & exhibitor pricing not yet published — figures below are baselined off EMS2025 and comparable congresses.

**Bottom line:** EMS2027 is the European EMS Congress, held for the first time in Ireland — an English-speaking, paramedic-led country, i.e. our exact target buyer in one room. Attending as a scouting trip for 4 people runs **~$12–13k**; attending as a vendor/exhibitor runs **~$18–23k all-in**. Language is a non-issue: the congress is English-only.

---

## 1. The event

- **What it is:** The European EMS Congress, run by **EMS Europe**. Rotates around Europe annually since Copenhagen 2016. EMS2025 (Stockholm) drew **1,200+ delegates from 47 countries** — a real, sizable international event (research, workshops, networking, plus the "European EMS Championship" skills competition).
- **When / where:** **Mon May 10 – Wed May 12, 2027**, at the **Gleneagle, Killarney, Co. Kerry, Ireland**. First time in Ireland. Hosted with Ireland's National Ambulance Service; supported by Fáilte Ireland and the Kerry Convention Bureau.
- **Status:** Early-bird tickets "available online soon." Full 2027 site, registration prices, and exhibitor prospectus not yet live.

## 2. Language — confirmed English-only

English is the **sole official language** of the European EMS congresses. All presentations must be delivered in English; **no simultaneous interpretation** is provided. The EMS Championship is run in English too. Even though the congress rotates through non-English countries, English is the working language because the audience is pan-European. **Zero language barrier** for us to attend, exhibit, or present.

## 3. Being a vendor / exhibitor (from EMS2025 structure)

- Exhibitor space is staged next to catering/poster areas — breaks and meals are deliberately placed in the exhibit hall to drive booth traffic.
- Options run from **single-booth displays up to multi-booth blocks**, plus sponsorship tiers.
- **Congress registration is included in exhibitor packages** (typically 1–2 staff passes; extra staff cost more).
- A sponsorship/exhibition prospectus with pricing is published once the cycle opens. EMS2025's secretariat was Meetx (Sweden); 2027 will likely run through an Irish secretariat / Kerry Convention Bureau.
- **No 2027 booth pricing exists yet.** Comparable European medical congresses: a basic exhibitor package ~**€4,000–€8,000+**, scaling with size/sponsorship.
- **Action to unlock real numbers:** email EMS Europe now to get on the prospectus list (early = better booth placement).

## 4. Budget — 4 people, 4 rooms

Assumes US departure, May = shoulder season, euro converted ~$1.08. Closest airport is **Kerry (KIR, ~15 min)** but limited routes; most US travelers fly **Dublin or Shannon** then train/drive (Dublin→Killarney ~3.5 hrs; Shannon ~1.5 hrs).

## 5. Logistics notes

- **No visa** for US citizens for short Ireland stays. Ireland is **not** in Schengen and has **not** launched an ETA — no pre-clearance fee. Just **passports valid 6+ months**. (Don't confuse with the UK's new ETA.)
- The Gleneagle is venue *and* hotel (258 rooms). Book the 4 rooms through the **official congress block** the moment it opens — a 1,200+ delegate event fills Killarney.
- Biggest budget swing is the **booth package** — get the prospectus to firm it up.

# European EMS Market Landscape

**Date:** 2026-06-19
**Classification:** Internal expansion scouting

**Bottom line:** "Europe" is not a market — it's ~32 fragmented national systems split across two clinical models. Our realistic addressable market is the **English-speaking, paramedic-led ("Anglo-American") cluster — UK first, Ireland second**, with Netherlands / Finland / Malta / Nordics as long-tail. Competition exists but is fragmented and dated; there is no dominant modern, instructor-first platform.

---

## 1. Two models — and only one fits us

| Model | Who delivers prehospital care | Countries (approx.) | Fit for MedEdPrep |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Anglo-American** | Paramedic-led ("bring patient to doctor") | ~14: UK, Ireland, Netherlands, Finland, Malta, Nordics (partial) | **Good** — content built for paramedics |
| **Franco-German** | Physician-led ("doctor goes to patient") | ~27: Germany, France, Italy, Spain, Austria, much of central/southern EU | **Poor** — care is doctor-delivered; paramedic CE is a weak fit |

Most countries are hybrids, but the takeaway holds: paramedic-focused education only has a strong buyer in the Anglo-American cluster. In Franco-German systems the clinical decision-maker is a physician, so a paramedic CE/LMS product addresses a much smaller, differently-shaped need.

Training hours also vary wildly — European median EMT-P training ~2000 hours; UK/Finland/Netherlands require ~4-year paramedic degrees, while Ireland is ~1009 hours. This matters for how we'd pitch level/depth of content per country.

## 2. Realistic addressable market (priority order)

1. **United Kingdom** — by far the largest. ~30,000+ HCPC-registered paramedics. English. Paramedic-led. Degree-based profession with mandatory CPD. **This is the prize.**
2. **Ireland** — small (a few thousand practitioners) but English, paramedic-led, structured CPC framework, and **host of EMS2027** — ideal beachhead/scouting ground.
3. **Long-tail** — Netherlands, Finland, Malta, Nordics: paramedic-led and high English fluency, but smaller and each needs its own clinical localization.

Franco-German core (Germany/France/Italy/Spain) = **deprioritize** for a paramedic product.

## 3. Competitive field (UK/Ireland)

Fragmented, much of it basic or free:

- **College of Paramedics** (UK professional body) — CPD hub + partnership with NHS "eLearning for Healthcare" (eLfH). Official, partly free.
- **NHS eLearning for Healthcare (eLfH)** — free, bite-sized CPD; the default baseline competitor.
- **Ambulance CPD** — subscription; portfolio builder, CPD diary/log tools, used across UK + globally.
- **Geeky Medics** — online CPD (ECG/investigations/drug calcs), progress tracking, downloadable certificates.
- **CPD Online College** — generic CPD courses with certificates.

**Gap:** none of these is a modern, instructor-first LMS with adaptive testing / cohort tooling. The field is course-library and certificate-tracker shaped. That gap aligns with MedEdPrep's thesis.

## 4. Harmonization tailwind (long-term, not actionable now)

The EU-funded **EPaCur** project (Erasmus+, Nordic-led, 2019–2022) pushed a harmonized European paramedic *bachelor's* curriculum framework. It's slow and academic, but signals the market is drifting toward the standardization that favors a cross-border platform play. Watch, don't act.

# 30-Day Action Plan — 501(c)(3) Formation

*Start date: 2026-04-06 (Day 1)*

This plan covers the first 30 calendar days after deciding to proceed with forming the Georgia 501(c)(3). Each item includes who does it, estimated time, cost, dependencies, and relevant form/website links.

**Key:** "Founder" means the MedEdPrep founder acting as incorporator. "Board" means the Board of Directors once constituted. Items marked with a star (*) run in parallel with the grant strategy 30-day action items from grant-strategy-comprehensive.md.

## Week 3: Days 15-21 (April 20 - April 26, 2026)

### Organizational Meeting and Initial Governance

| Day | Action | Who | Time | Cost | Dependencies | Links/Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 15-17 | **Distribute organizational meeting materials.** Send to all confirmed directors: meeting agenda, draft Bylaws, draft COI Policy, blank Annual Disclosure Statements, proposed officer slate, draft budget for Year 1. Provide at least 5 days advance notice. | Founder / Secretary-designate | 2 hours | Free | All governance documents finalized, board candidates confirmed |
| 20-21 | **Hold the organizational meeting.** This is the most important meeting. The Board must formally take the following actions, each by separate resolution documented in the minutes: | Board | 2-3 hours | Free | All directors confirmed and present (in person or by phone/video) | O.C.G.A. section 14-3-820(b) permits meeting by any means where all can hear simultaneously |

**Organizational meeting agenda (required actions):**

1. Call to order and establish quorum
2. **Adopt Bylaws** -- vote to adopt the Bylaws as the governing document of the Corporation
3. **Elect officers** -- elect President, Secretary, and Treasurer. Recommended: the founder should NOT be President. The founder may serve as a director but should hold a non-executive board seat to strengthen the independence narrative.
4. **Adopt Conflict of Interest Policy** -- vote to adopt the COI Policy
5. **Complete Annual Disclosure Statements** -- every director and officer signs the Annual Disclosure Statement on the spot. The Secretary collects and files them.
6. **Approve initial programs** -- present and approve the three program areas (EMS Education Access, Workforce Development, End-of-Life Care Education) with their measurable outcomes
7. **Approve initial budget** -- adopt the Year 1 operating budget
8. **Authorize bank account** -- ratify the bank account opened in Week 2; designate authorized signers and check signing authority thresholds
9. **Authorize Form 1023 preparation** -- resolve to prepare and file the Form 1023 application for tax-exempt status
10. **Set regular meeting schedule** -- establish the quarterly meeting dates for the remainder of the fiscal year
11. **Designate fiscal year** -- confirm December 31 fiscal year end
12. **Appoint registered agent** -- confirm the registered agent and registered office
13. Adjournment

## Critical Path Items

These items are on the critical path -- delays here push everything else back:

1. **Board recruitment (Week 1).** Cannot hold the organizational meeting without confirmed directors. Cannot file Form 1023 without a constituted Board with an adopted COI Policy.
2. **Filing Articles of Incorporation (Week 2).** Cannot obtain EIN without a formed entity. Cannot open a bank account without an EIN. Cannot register on SAM.gov without a bank account.
3. **SAM.gov registration (Week 2).** 4-8 week processing time. Cannot apply for ANY federal grant (HRSA, FEMA AFG, etc.) without an active SAM registration. Start the day you have an EIN and bank account.
4. **Organizational meeting (Week 3).** Cannot begin Form 1023 preparation without adopted Bylaws, adopted COI Policy, and meeting minutes documenting these actions.

## ADDITIONAL ARTIFACTS NEEDED

The following items are referenced in the formation design, grant strategy, or dual-entity framework documents but have not yet been drafted. These should be produced during weeks 5-12 as Form 1023 preparation progresses.

### Governance and Compliance Documents

1. **Articles of Incorporation (final draft)** -- the formation design provides the purpose clause and dissolution clause language, but the full Articles document (with all required provisions per O.C.G.A. section 14-3-202) has not been drafted
2. **Organizational Meeting Minutes template** -- a structured template ensuring all required resolutions are documented with the specificity needed for Form 1023
3. **Board Resolution templates** -- standard templates for: (a) approving a Related Entity transaction; (b) approving officer compensation; (c) adopting a new program; (d) authorizing a grant application
4. **Annual Compliance Calendar** -- a month-by-month calendar of all recurring obligations: Form 990 filing, state annual registration, charitable solicitation renewal, COI annual disclosure collection, board meeting schedule, audit engagement
5. **Whistleblower Policy** -- IRS Form 990 Part VI asks whether the organization has a written whistleblower policy. Not legally required but expected by best-practice standards and some grantors.
6. **Document Retention and Destruction Policy** -- IRS Form 990 Part VI also asks about this. Should specify retention periods for: tax returns (permanent), board minutes (permanent), financial records (7 years), grant records (per grantor requirements, typically 3-7 years), personnel records (7 years after termination), COI disclosure statements (7 years).
7. **Gift Acceptance Policy** -- defines what types of gifts the Corporation will and will not accept, and the process for evaluating non-cash gifts. Relevant given the potential for in-kind software donations from MedEdPrep.

### Financial Documents

8. **Year 1 Operating Budget** -- projected revenue and expenses for the first fiscal year, needed for the organizational meeting and for Form 1023 Part VIII
9. **3-Year Financial Projections** -- required for Form 1023 Part VIII. Must include: projected revenue by source (grants, donations, program fees), projected expenses by category (program, management, fundraising), and the public support test calculation
10. **Fair Market Value Documentation Framework** -- a template/methodology for documenting FMV for each type of transaction with MedEdPrep: software license pricing comparability, shared services cost allocation methodology, data access agreement terms

### Grant-Related Documents

11. **Grant-Ready Program Descriptions** -- polished, one-page descriptions of each of the three programs with measurable outcomes, target populations, and impact metrics. Referenced in the grant strategy 30-day action items.
12. **Perkins V Positioning Sheet** -- a one-pager for CTAE coordinators mapping MedEdPrep products to Perkins V Section 135 allowable uses. Referenced in the grant strategy.
13. **HRSA Consortium Partnership Framework** -- template letter of commitment and partnership agreement for rural EMS agencies, high schools, and health centers joining the HRSA consortium. Needed before the RHNDP NOFO drops.

### Operational Documents

14. **Shared Services Agreement template** -- if any office space, staff, technology, or administrative services will be shared between the Corporation and MedEdPrep, a written SSA with FMV cost allocation methodology must be in place before any sharing occurs
15. **Data Access Agreement template** -- terms under which the Corporation may access anonymized, aggregate data from Company E (The Vault) for research purposes under Program 2
16. **Software License Agreement template** -- standard terms for the Corporation's purchase of MedEdPrep Education access at FMV for distribution to beneficiary populations under Program 1
17. **Advisory Board Invitation Letter template** -- per the formation design, a simple letter stating the advisory role, that it is non-governing, and what the Corporation is asking for

## 1. EMSC (Emergency Medical Services for Children)

**NOFO:** HRSA-23-063 (most recent cycle)
**Eligible applicants:** State governments and accredited schools of medicine ONLY. One award per state.
**Funding:** ~$205,000/year per state (~59 awards, ~$12M total)
**Period:** 4 years

**Assessment: NOT eligible as lead applicant.** Georgia's EMSC State Partnership (administered through DPH) could be a partner or subcontractor. Worth building that relationship.

---

## 2. Telehealth Network Grant Program (TNGP)

**Most recent NOFO:** HRSA-20-036
**Award:** Up to $300,000/year for 4 years (~29 awards, ~$8.7M total)
**Status:** Program cycle concluded (Sept 2020 - Aug 2024). No new NOFO posted.

**Eligibility:** Rural or urban nonprofit entities providing services through a telehealth network to rural areas. 501(c)(3) nonprofits are eligible.

**Successor programs to monitor:**
- Behavioral Health Integration Evidence Based Telehealth Network Program (BHI EB-TNP): HRSA-24-010
- CFDA 93.211 — set alerts

**Assessment:** STRONG conceptual fit, but no current opportunity. Monitor for successor NOFOs.

---

## 3. Rural Health Network Development Planning Program (RHNDP) — TOP PRIORITY

**Most recent NOFO:** HRSA-25-037
**Funding:** Up to $100,000 per award (35 awards, $3.5M total)
**Period:** 1 year
**Last deadline:** February 19, 2025
**Next cycle:** Expected late 2026/early 2027

**Eligibility:** Domestic public or private, nonprofit or for-profit entities. **New organizations can apply.** Must form a network of 3+ entities, with at least one in an HRSA-designated rural area. No cost sharing required.

**Why this is #1:** Designed for organizations that are just getting started building networks. A planning year to connect the nonprofit with rural Georgia high schools, EMS agencies, community colleges, and workforce boards. Produces a strategic plan for scaling EMS education access in underserved rural counties.

---

## 4. Rural Health Network Development Program (RHND)

**Most recent NOFO:** HRSA-23-030
**Funding:** Up to $300,000/year for 4 years (~44 awards)
**Not posted annually** — 2023 was the most recent cycle.

**Eligibility:** Same as RHNDP. Must demonstrate experience or capacity to serve rural underserved populations. No cost sharing required.

**Assessment:** The implementation counterpart to RHNDP. Best pursued after a planning grant year establishes track record.

---

## 5. Rural Health Network Advancement Program (RHNAP) — New Pilot

**NOFO:** HRSA-26-082
**Funding:** $500,000 per award (6 awards, $3M total)
**Application posting:** April 24, 2026
**Deadline:** June 24, 2026
**Expected award:** August 1, 2026

**Assessment:** More hospital/clinic-focused than education-focused. If EMS education connects to rural hospital EMS readiness, could be explored. But fewer awards and higher competition.

---

## 6. Rural Health Care Services Outreach Program — HIGH PRIORITY

**Most recent NOFO:** HRSA-25-038
**Funding:**
- Regular Track: Up to $250,000/year (~40 awards)
- Healthy Rural Hometown Initiative Track: Up to $300,000/year (~10 awards)
- 4-year period of performance
- FY2025 total: $15M to 58 awardees

**Last deadline:** January 27, 2025. Next cycle expected ~Q4 2026 or Q1 2027.

**Eligibility:** Domestic public or private nonprofit or for-profit entities. Must form a consortium of 3+ healthcare providers, with at least 66% in HRSA-designated rural areas. Must consult Georgia State Office of Rural Health. No cost sharing required.

**Why this fits:** EMS education IS healthcare outreach. A consortium with rural Georgia EMS agencies, high schools, and community health centers delivering EMS education, test prep, and certification exams is exactly what this program supports.

## 7. Other HRSA Programs (Not Directly Applicable)

- **AHEC (Area Health Education Centers):** Lead applicants must be accredited medical/nursing schools. But Georgia's existing AHECs could be consortium partners.
- **GWEP (Geriatrics Workforce Enhancement):** Not relevant to EMS.
- **NURSE Corps:** Individual scholarships, not organizational grants.
- **Flex Program EMS Supplement:** State-level recipients only (State Flex Programs). Georgia's program could subcontract.

## Application Process

### Registration (Start NOW)
1. EIN from IRS (immediate)
2. Wait 5 weeks after EIN before SAM registration
3. Register at SAM.gov (7-10 business days processing)
4. Create Grants.gov account (1 day after SAM, 7-10 days to process)
5. Register in HRSA Electronic Handbooks (EHB)

### Typical HRSA Application Components
- SF-424 (standard federal application form)
- Project narrative (60-80 pages max including attachments)
- Work plan with measurable objectives
- Budget and budget justification (SF-424A)
- Organizational capacity statement
- Letters of support/commitment from consortium partners
- Needs assessment data for target communities
- Logic model

### Timeline Reality
- Application preparation: 40+ hours (HRSA's own estimate)
- NOFOs give 45-60 days from posting to deadline
- Review process: 3-6 months
- New org challenges: Reviewers score organizational capacity. Mitigate with strong partnerships, experienced staff/consultants, and well-developed governance.

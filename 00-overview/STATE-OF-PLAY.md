## Strategic Assets

Things MedEdPrep has that competitors don't:

1. **10,600 items with 1.55M responses** — Real psychometric data at scale. No competitor in EMS has this dataset.
2. **Adaptive testing engine** — Live since Nov 2025. IRT-based. Not "adaptive" as marketing speak.
3. **Bifactor psychometric model** — First mover in credentialing. Pass/fail + diagnostic feedback from the same items.
4. **CAPCE organizational accreditation** — Already held. CE pipeline is ready to activate.
5. **CoAssist** — No integrated compliance tool exists for EMS programs. Vacuum.
6. **Cognitive process taxonomy** — 6-level EMS-specific framework. No validated equivalent exists in the industry.
7. **Full-stack ownership** — Jeramey built all of it. No vendor dependencies for core platform. ~$2,500/month total infrastructure cost.
8. **Nationwide connections incoming** — Heather and Richard Rhodes bring state-level EMS relationships across the country. Richard's background as a former OEMST official gives MedEdPrep direct insider knowledge of state regulatory infrastructure.

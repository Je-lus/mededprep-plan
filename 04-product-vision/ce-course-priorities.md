## Executive Summary

This document prioritizes the first 20 CE course topics for MedEdPrep's Pulse CE platform, sequenced for maximum strategic impact. The ordering is driven by three forces:

1. **Georgia state mandates** — 8 hrs pediatric, 4 hrs cardiovascular, 4 hrs trauma required
2. **NCCP domain coverage** — ensuring MedEdPrep can serve as a complete recertification solution
3. **Competitive gaps** — zero competitors offer adaptive/psychometric CE; topic selection amplifies that advantage

Each topic decomposes into 3-5 individually CAPCE-accredited micro-modules (0.25-1.0 CEH each). The 20 topics produce 76+ catalog items at launch scale.

## NCCP Framework — How Recertification Hours Work

The NREMT's National Continued Competency Program (NCCP) structures CE into three components. All hours are per 2-year recertification cycle.

### Hour Requirements by Certification Level

| Level | National | Local/State | Individual | Total |
|-------|----------|-------------|------------|-------|
| **Paramedic (NRP)** | 30 hrs | 15 hrs | 15 hrs | **60 hrs** |
| **AEMT** | 25 hrs | 12.5 hrs | 12.5 hrs | **50 hrs** |
| **EMT** | 20 hrs | 10 hrs | 10 hrs | **40 hrs** |
| **EMR** | 8 hrs | 4 hrs | 4 hrs | **16 hrs** |

### Component Definitions

**National Component (50% of total)**
CAPCE-approved distributive education covering NCCP topic areas. This is MedEdPrep's primary addressable market. As of 2023, NREMT permanently removed all limits on distributive (online) education — all National Component hours can be completed 100% online.

Required NCCP topic distribution for National Component (Paramedic example, 30 hrs):

| NCCP Topic Area | Required Hours | % of National |
|-----------------|----------------|---------------|
| Airway, Respiration & Ventilation | 3.0 hrs | 10% |
| Cardiovascular | 4.5 hrs | 15% |
| Trauma | 3.0 hrs | 10% |
| Medical | 4.5 hrs | 15% |
| Operations (incl. EMS research, quality improvement) | 3.0 hrs | 10% |
| Individual topics (provider's choice from NCCP areas) | 12.0 hrs | 40% |

*Note: EMT/AEMT hours are proportionally scaled. Pediatric content must be integrated across clinical domains — NREMT requires a minimum percentage of pediatric-specific items within applicable domains.*

**Local/State Component (25% of total)**
Agency-directed education on local protocols, state-specific requirements, and regional priorities. MedEdPrep can serve this through agency admin features — training officers assign specific modules to their roster.

**Individual Component (25% of total)**
Provider-selected CE on topics of their choice. This is MedEdPrep's secondary entry point — the "browse and learn what interests you" pathway. Any CAPCE-approved CE qualifies.

### Strategic Implication

MedEdPrep's catalog must cover all NCCP required topic areas to be a complete recertification solution. A provider using only MedEdPrep should be able to fulfill their entire National Component (20-30 hrs) plus Individual Component (10-15 hrs) without going elsewhere. That means **30-45 hrs of content** per certification level.

**1. Pediatric Respiratory Emergencies & Airway Management**
- **CEH:** 2.0 (4 x 0.5 CEH modules)
- **NCCP Domain:** Airway, Respiration & Ventilation (Pediatric)
- **Georgia:** Counts toward 8-hr pediatric mandate
- **Format:** 0.5 CEH assessment-only modules: (a) Pediatric airway anatomy & assessment, (b) Pediatric respiratory distress vs failure, (c) Pediatric airway interventions, (d) Neonatal resuscitation essentials
- **Why first:** Georgia requires 8 hrs peds. Pediatric airway is the highest-anxiety, highest-stakes topic. Zero competitors offer adaptive pediatric CE with diagnostic feedback. Providers are terrified of peds calls — this is emotional, not just regulatory.
- **SME:** Samantha (critical care paramedic) or Gene (flight paramedic) + Dr. Tim Thomas for medical oversight
- **Competitive gap:** No competitor provides per-objective diagnostic feedback on pediatric competency. A provider finishes and sees "strong on assessment, weak on BVM technique" — that is a differentiator nobody else delivers.

**2. 2025 AHA Guidelines Update**
- **CEH:** 2.0 (2 x 1.0 CEH modules OR 4 x 0.5 CEH)
- **NCCP Domain:** Cardiovascular
- **Georgia:** Counts toward 4-hr cardiovascular mandate
- **Format:** 1.0 CEH full course preferred (comprehensive guidelines review warrants depth); also offer 0.5 CEH "key changes only" quick assessment
- **Why second:** Time-sensitive content that every EMS provider in the country needs. **This is the free lead magnet** — offered at no cost to drive platform adoption. Universal relevance creates the largest possible top-of-funnel.
- **Free lead magnet strategy:** 1 free CEH on AHA Guidelines changes. Provider creates account, takes adaptive assessment, sees diagnostic report showing their strengths and gaps against the new guidelines. The diagnostic report is the "aha moment" that makes them tell their partner on the truck. We eat the CAPCE cert cost (~$0.31/certification) as marketing spend.
- **SME:** Richard (deep NREMT/NASEMSO connections, AHA liaison awareness) + Dr. Tim Thomas
- **Competitive gap:** Every competitor will offer AHA updates as boring slide decks. MedEdPrep offers it as an adaptive assessment that tells you exactly which guideline changes you've already internalized and which you haven't.

**3. STEMI Recognition & 12-Lead ECG Interpretation**

- **SME:** Gene (critical care/flight paramedic) + Dr. Tim Thomas
- **Competitive gap:** Every competitor teaches 12-lead via static images and MCQs. MedEdPrep uses interactive item types (drag-and-drop, classification tables) against an adaptive engine. The diagnostic report shows exactly which rhythms and presentations the provider struggles with.

## Free Lead Magnet Strategy

### The Play

The AHA Guidelines Update CE course launches as a **permanent free offering** (not rotating — always free). This is the platform's primary acquisition engine.

### Why AHA Guidelines

1. **Universal need** — Every EMS provider in the country needs this content
2. **Time-sensitive** — 2025 guidelines changes create urgency
3. **Credibility signal** — First interaction with MedEdPrep is clinically relevant, not a sales pitch
4. **Diagnostic hook** — The adaptive assessment produces a diagnostic report that shows exactly which guideline changes the provider has and hasn't internalized. This is the "tell your partner on the truck" moment.

### Funnel Mechanics

```
Free AHA CE (2.0 CEH)
  --> Account creation required
    --> Adaptive assessment taken
      --> Diagnostic report generated
        --> "You're weak in post-ROSC care, strong in BLS changes"
          --> Recommended: Cardiac Arrest Management course ($5)
            --> Repeat across domains
```

### Cost Analysis

| Item | Cost |
|------|------|
| CAPCE certification fee per completion | ~$0.31 |
| Infrastructure cost per assessment | ~$0.02 |
| Total cost per free user | ~$0.33 |
| Target free completions Year 1 | 5,000 |
| Total marketing spend on free CE | ~$1,650/year |
| Equivalent cost per lead | **$0.33** |

Compare to: Google Ads for "EMS CE" keywords ($3-8/click), Facebook ads ($1-3/click), conference booth ($5,000-10,000 per event). Free CE at $0.33/lead is the most efficient acquisition channel by 10x.

### Rotating Free Monthly Topic

After AHA Guidelines (always free), one additional topic per month rotates as free:

| Month | Free Rotating Topic | Strategic Rationale |
|-------|--------------------|--------------------|
| Launch | AHA Guidelines (permanent) | Universal need, largest funnel |

Each free rotation:
- Different topic creates a reason to return monthly

- That is 20-25% of EMT recertification requirements — enough to hook, not enough to satisfy
- Every free assessment generates diagnostic data that points to paid modules

# Competitive Intel: Pocket Prep — Psychometrician Presence

**Company:** Pocket Prep, Inc. (pocketprep.com) — Seattle, WA. Mobile/web exam-prep platform founded 2011, 11–50 employees. Covers many certification/licensure categories incl. EMT, Paramedic, EPPP, SHRM, HRCI, nursing.
**Research date:** 2026-06-06
**Researcher note:** All LinkedIn evidence below is from Google-indexed snippets only — the LinkedIn auth wall prevented direct profile/job browsing, so the people-side picture is necessarily incomplete.

## Verdict

- **Q1 — Has Pocket Prep ever posted a psychometrician (or measurement/assessment-science) job?**
  **No public evidence found.** No such posting appears in current openings or in any indexed historical/job-board result. (Confidence: **Medium** — absence of evidence, not proof of absence; job-archive indexing is patchy.)

- **Q2 — Does/did a psychometrician work there?**
  **No public evidence found.** Their full public team roster and indexed LinkedIn affiliations show no one in a psychometrics/measurement/assessment-science role or with that background. (Confidence: **Medium** — based on a complete public "About" roster plus searches; small private/contractor roles could be unindexed.)

## 1. Evidence found (positive)

There is **no positive evidence** of a psychometrician role or person. The positive findings below instead characterize how they *do* describe their content/quality process, which is editorial/SME-driven rather than psychometric.

- **Full public team roster (no psychometrician).** The About page lists named staff across Leadership, Product/Design, Engineering, Education/Content, Marketing. The Education & Content group is: Michelle Marlowe (Director of Education), Emily Lehmann (Senior Education Specialist), Caitlin Khullar (Nursing Educator), Kyle Atkins (Senior EMS Curriculum Specialist). No psychometrician, measurement scientist, assessment scientist, or research-scientist title appears. Notably, AI/engineering is well-staffed (Staff AI Architect, Staff Full Stack/AI Engineer, Senior AI Engineer). Source: https://www.pocketprep.com/about/
- **Stated content methodology is editorial, not psychometric.** Their "sourcePrep" process is described as: review the exam at the source → SMEs write questions + explanations → editors vet → "review our content to ensure it's up-to-date and improve accuracy with data." "All of our practice questions are written by industry experts." This is SME + editorial review with light data feedback, not formal item analysis/IRT/equating language. Source: https://www.pocketprep.com/about/
- **Director of Education background is education/content, not psychometrics.** Michelle Marlowe (Director of Education) is described as focused on test-prep content development and SME support; no psychometric/measurement background surfaced (UCF alum). A separately-named "Kailie Marlowe — Psychometric Assessment Coordinator" is a *different person at a different company* (Homewood Ravensview) — do not conflate. Source: https://www.linkedin.com/in/michellecmarlowe/

## 3. Signals of psychometric sophistication (without a named role)

- **Adaptive learning is marketed, but described in lay terms.** Search snippets indicate "Pocket Prep's adaptive system customizes the experience to maximize recall." This reads as spaced-repetition / personalization marketing, not a documented CAT (computerized adaptive testing) or IRT-calibrated engine. No public description of item calibration, b-parameters, equating, DIF, or validity studies was found.
- **No validity/IRT publications** authored by anyone Pocket-Prep-affiliated were found via Scholar/ResearchGate searches.
- **Strong AI/engineering bench** (multiple AI architect/engineer titles) — suggests data-driven item improvement is more likely to flow through engineering/AI than a dedicated psychometrics function.
- **Net read:** Their public posture is *editorial/SME quality control + light data feedback + AI*, not formal psychometric measurement science. No evidence of in-house psychometric rigor (which is plausibly a differentiation gap for a competitor or a hire to lean into).

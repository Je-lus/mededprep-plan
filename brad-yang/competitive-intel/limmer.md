## SOPHISTICATION SIGNALS (public)

Limmer demonstrates *applied literacy* in psychometric concepts, but frames psychometrics as something **the NREMT does**, not an in-house capability:

- Product marketing claims "**adaptive quizzes**" / "realistic test experience" (EMT Review Plus). Adaptive UX claim present; no IRT/CAT engine technical claim.
- Blog "How Close Am I to Passing the NREMT?" (Jun 2024) explicitly defines **"psychometrics (the science of assessment and measurement)"** and describes the NREMT's "complex algorithm," question difficulty, and scaled scoring (100–1500, 950 cut) — but attributes all of it to the NREMT ("a recipe only the NREMT knows").
  - Source: https://limmereducation.com/article/how-close-am-i-to-passing-the-nremt
- In comments, Stephanie Limmer notes their **PASS products use cut scores, not percentage scoring** ("The PASS products are not percentage scoring but are cut scores") — shows they design to a competency cut-score model, a psychometric concept applied in product, consistent with ex-NREMT design input.
- Dan Limmer is publicly positioned as an NREMT test-taking/question-writing expert ("a guy who writes EMS tests").
- **Net:** Sophistication is *content/item-writing + ex-regulator credibility*, NOT quantitative psychometrics (no public IRT calibration, equating, DIF, item-analysis pipeline, or validity-study claims of their own).

## BOTTOM LINE

Limmer is an EMS content/exam-prep company whose psychometric credibility runs through **former NREMT exam personnel** (Todd Vreeland, ex-Coordinator of Examinations + IBSC item-writing co-chair; Bill Brown, former NREMT Executive Director) rather than a credentialed/quantitative psychometrician. They speak the language of cut scores and adaptive testing and apply it in product, but show **no in-house psychometrics/measurement-science hire and no such job posting** in any public source checked. This matches the "smaller, author-led company — outsources/borrows exam-construction expertise rather than employing a psychometrician" hypothesis.

# Competitive Intel: Platinum Educational Group & FISDAP

**Research date:** 2026-06-06
**Question 1:** Have they ever posted a job for a psychometrician (or measurement/assessment-science role)?
**Question 2:** Does/did a psychometrician work there, searchably?

**Corporate note:** FISDAP (founded 1997, Minneapolis/St. Paul, MN; ~5 employees, ~$2.3M revenue) was acquired and now operates under **Public Safety Group (PSG)**, a division of **Jones & Bartlett Learning**. So FISDAP and Platinum are NOT the same company and did NOT merge with each other — they are separate EMS testing vendors. (The task framing of a Platinum/FISDAP merger appears to be inaccurate; FISDAP merged under PSG/Jones & Bartlett, not under Platinum.) Both are treated separately below.

## FISDAP (fisdap.net / psglearning.com)

### Verdict lines
- **Q1 (posted a psychometrician job?):** No public evidence found. **Confidence: Low** (small private company, no surfaced careers page or archived posting).
- **Q2 (a psychometrician worked there?):** **YES — strong positive evidence.** **Confidence: High.**

### Q2 — Positive evidence (a psychometrician worked at FISDAP)

1. **Mike (Michael) Bowen — Director of Testing at FISDAP, Sep 2009 – Aug 2019 (~10 yrs). He is a psychometrician.**
   - His LinkedIn (via Exa) shows current role: **"Manager of Psychometrics at Meazure Learning,"** and the profile is explicitly tagged **"Mike Bowen – Psychometrician."** Prior role listed: **"Director Of Testing at Fisdap, Sep 2009 – Aug 2019."** Education: BEmergHealth (Hons), Monash University.
   - URL: https://linkedin.com/in/mike-bowen-b5ab9a47
   - Corroboration: described as "Fisdap Director of Testing Mike/Michael Bowen" in the JEMS write-up of FISDAP's NAEMSE award-winning research. URL: https://www.jems.com/news/fisdap-sponsored-project-wins-best-resea/
   - **Significance:** The person who ran FISDAP's testing operation for a decade now holds a Manager-of-Psychometrics title at a major testing firm — i.e., FISDAP had a psychometrically-trained testing director in-house.

2. **Luke Stanke, PhD — psychometrician on FISDAP research.**
   - Repeatedly described as "a psychometrician" who worked with "Fisdap Director of Testing Michael Bowen" and educator David Page on the award-winning 2014 NAEMSE Best Research project ("Warning Signs: Increased Neuroticism is Associated with Poor Professional Behavior during Paramedic Student Contacts"). URL: https://www.jems.com/news/fisdap-sponsored-project-wins-best-resea/
   - PhD, Educational Psychology – Quantitative Methods – Measurement, University of Minnesota. Now Product Evangelist at Sigma; prior phData, Minneapolis Public Schools, U of MN College of Pharmacy.

   - **Caveat:** His LinkedIn employment history does NOT list FISDAP as an employer. He was the measurement specialist on FISDAP-sponsored research, most likely as a UMN-affiliated/contracted researcher rather than a FISDAP W-2 employee. Still, a credentialed psychometrician was embedded in FISDAP's research output.

3. **Adisack Nhouyvanisvong, PhD — psychometrician, FISDAP research co-author (collaborator, not employee).**
   - Listed among authors on the FISDAP research page (fisdap.net/research). PhD Psychology, Carnegie Mellon; career psychometrician (former Senior Director of Psychometric Quality at Data Recognition Corp; Pearson VUE; co-founder/President of Naiku; now at ARRT).
   - URLs: https://www.fisdap.net/research ; https://www.linkedin.com/in/adisacknhouyvanisvong/ ; https://www.crunchbase.com/person/adisack-nhouyvanisvong

   - **Rachael Rosen — Marketing & Content Strategist at Fisdap (2009–2015)** and the named research/operations contact; appears as author on FISDAP research. (Not a psychometrician.) URL: https://linkedin.com/in/rachaelrosen
   - **Michael Johnson — Founder/President of FISDAP (1997–2017).** Background in software/predictive analytics, not psychometrics. URL: https://linkedin.com/in/michael-johnson-4854959

### Q1 — Where I came up empty (FISDAP)
- No FISDAP/PSG/Jones & Bartlett job posting for "psychometrician," "measurement scientist," "research scientist," "test development," or "data scientist (assessment)" surfaced on Google, LinkedIn Jobs, Indeed, ZipRecruiter, or Exa.
- FISDAP has no independent public careers page surfaced; hiring would route through Jones & Bartlett / PSG corporate. Absence here is weak evidence given the tiny headcount (~5).

### Sophistication signals (FISDAP) — HIGH
- **Dedicated research arm.** Hosts a "Research Summit" (first held 2006, with Dr. Baxter Larmon of UCLA Prehospital Care Research Forum), maintains a research-data program since 2004, publishes "Advancing EMS Through Research" volumes, and offers a free "EMS Research 101" course.
  - URLs: https://www.fisdap.net/research ; https://www.fisdap.net/past-research ; https://info.psglearning.com/fisdap-research-101
- **Explicit psychometric methods:** uses Classical Test Theory and **Item Response Theory (Rasch analysis)**; exam scores correlated with first-attempt National Registry exam results. (Per fisdap.net/testing.)
- **Validated affective/personality instruments:** Entrance Exams embed an adapted **General Self-Efficacy Scale** (EMT) and adapted **M5-50 personality inventory** (Paramedic) — "valid and reliable psychometric scale." URL: https://www.fisdap.net/entrance-exams
- **Peer-reviewed/award output:** NAEMSE Best Research 2014; multiple multi-thousand-N studies (e.g., n=4,111 institution-type study). FISDAP is widely described as "a leader in EMS educational research."
- **Marketing language:** "validated, predictable, and prescriptive testing solutions."

### Confidence + caveats (FISDAP)
- Q2 = **High** that a psychometrician/psychometrically-trained person worked there (Bowen as Director of Testing → now Manager of Psychometrics; Stanke embedded in research).
- Q1 = **Low** — could not confirm or refute a formal job posting; tiny company, hiring via parent. Absence ≠ proof.
- Distinguish employees (Bowen, Tilden, Johnson, Rosen) from research collaborators (Stanke, Nhouyvanisvong, plus external co-authors David Page, Brett Williams, Paul Jennings, Nigel Barr, etc.).

## Platinum Educational Group (platinumed.com; runs EMSTesting.com, NursingTesting.com)

**Company:** Founded 2002, Grandville, MI. Products: Platinum Planner, Platinum Tests, EMSTesting.

### Verdict lines
- **Q1 (posted a psychometrician job?):** No public evidence found; the only test-related hiring observed is for **item writers / content creators**, not psychometricians. **Confidence: Medium** (their careers page was reachable and showed no such role).
- **Q2 (a psychometrician worked there?):** No public evidence of a named psychometrician/measurement scientist. **Confidence: Medium-Low** (no team/leadership names tied to a measurement-science credential surfaced).

### Q1 — Evidence / where I came up empty (Platinum)
- **Careers page** (https://www.platinumed.com/jobs/) reachable but lists no specific positions — only an EEOC statement. No psychometrician/measurement role.
- The only concrete hiring solicitation found is for **item writers/content creators**: "If you are interested in being an item writer/content creator for Platinum Educational Group, please send a letter of interest and your CV to spencer@platinumed.com." — John Spencer, **Director of Education** (June 2022 test-bank update). URL: https://sales.platinumed.com/update-on-our-test-banks-june-2022
- No psychometrician/measurement/research-scientist postings found on Indeed, Glassdoor, LinkedIn, or Exa for Platinum.

### Q2 — People (Platinum)
- **John Spencer — Director of Education.** Runs test-bank development and item writing. No psychometrics credential surfaced. (Validation work attributed generically to "content experts, educators, and editors" plus a "Medical Director.")
- **No named psychometrician, measurement scientist, statistician, or PhD-credentialed measurement person** publicly tied to Platinum was found. Their validity messaging is institutional ("we") with no individual attribution.

### Sophistication signals (Platinum) — MODERATE, mostly Classical Test Theory
- Claims a large response corpus for validation: **"over 25.8 million responses of our 4,000+ question bank" (as of June 2015)**; elsewhere cited as 5,000+ questions / 31M+ responses. URL: https://news.platinumed.com/makes-platinum-content-tests-valid/
- Validation methods named are **Classical Test Theory**, not IRT:
  - Cut scores via **Nedelsky method**, reviewed after >100 candidates.
  - **Difficulty** and **Discrimination** via upper/lower-group formulas (requires >40 results).
  - **KR-20 reliability** (>4 students).
  - **Notably absent on that page:** Item Response Theory, point-biserial, or any named psychometrician.
- However, marketing/other pages **claim IRT competence**: "a deep understanding of mathematical evidence-based research and the science of item response theory," and they offer **computer adaptive testing** plus blueprinting to the "Registry Practice Analysis," Medical Director review, and program piloting. (EMSTesting.com "How We're Different" series; platinumed.com/testing.)
- Net: Platinum publicly advertises "valid and reliable" testing and claims IRT/CAT capability, but the **operational detail it publishes is CTT-based**, and no in-house measurement scientist is named — a weaker psychometric footprint than FISDAP's.

### Confidence + caveats (Platinum)
- Q1 = **Medium** (careers page seen, no such role; only item-writer solicitations). Could have unposted/internal roles.
- Q2 = **Medium-Low** (no public named psychometrician; doesn't prove none exists internally — small/private firm, LinkedIn not fully accessible without auth).

## Cross-company summary

| | FISDAP | Platinum Educational Group |
|---|---|---|
| **Q1: posted psychometrician job?** | No evidence found (Low conf.) | No evidence found; only item-writer hiring (Med conf.) |
| **Q2: psychometrician worked there?** | YES — Mike Bowen, Director of Testing (now Mgr of Psychometrics at Meazure); Luke Stanke (research); Adisack Nhouyvanisvong (collaborator) (High conf.) | No named psychometrician found (Med-Low conf.) |
| **Methods disclosed** | CTT + **IRT/Rasch**; validated personality/self-efficacy instruments | **CTT** (Nedelsky, KR-20, difficulty/discrimination); claims IRT/CAT but publishes CTT detail |
| **Research arm** | Yes — Research Summit (2006), data program since 2004, peer-reviewed/award-winning output, "EMS Research 101" | No standalone research department surfaced; education team owns content |
| **Overall psychometric sophistication** | High (the data/assessment leader, as expected) | Moderate; marketing-forward, CTT-anchored |

**Bottom line:** FISDAP is the more psychometrically sophisticated of the two and demonstrably had a psychometrician in a senior testing role (Mike Bowen) plus credentialed measurement researchers. Neither company shows a *publicly posted* psychometrician job opening. Platinum shows no publicly named psychometrician at all and leans on Classical Test Theory in its disclosed validation, despite marketing claims of IRT/adaptive-testing competence.

### Methodology / limitations
- Searched Google/WebSearch, Exa people+company search, and direct page fetches. LinkedIn profiles only partially accessible without authentication, so exact FISDAP employment dates for some collaborators (Stanke, Nhouyvanisvong) could not be fully confirmed. No archived job-board snapshots (e.g., Wayback careers pages) were located. Absence of a posted psychometrician role is not proof one was never posted.

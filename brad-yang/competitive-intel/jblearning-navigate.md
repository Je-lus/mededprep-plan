# Jones & Bartlett Learning (JBLearning) / Navigate / Ascend Learning — Psychometrician Intel

**Research date:** 2026-06-06
**Subject:** Jones & Bartlett Learning (jblearning.com), the Navigate eLearning + TestPrep platform, and parent company **Ascend Learning**. JBL publishes major EMS titles incl. the AAOS "Emergency Care and Transportation of the Sick and Injured" (Orange Book) and runs Navigate TestPrep for NREMT prep.

## VERDICTS

**Q1 — Has JBL/Ascend ever posted a psychometrician job?**
**YES — High confidence (corporate-level, EMS-inclusive).** Ascend Learning (JBL's parent) has posted multiple "Psychometrician" and "Associate Psychometrician" reqs. The job description **explicitly names "emergency services"** as one of the assessment program areas the role supports — alongside nursing, allied health, and fitness. This is a shared/corporate psychometrics function, not an EMS-dedicated posting, but it covers EMS by its own text.

**Q2 — Does a psychometrician work / has one worked there, searchably?**
**YES — High confidence (corporate).** Multiple named, currently-employed measurement professionals are publicly findable at Ascend Learning, including a VP-level psychometrician and a Senior Director with an ETS background. **EMS-specific attribution: Low confidence** — no individual is publicly tied specifically to EMS/Navigate psychometrics, though a published JBL predictive-validity study on Navigate TestPrep: EMT proves the function touches the EMS product.

**Competitive read:** Of the publishers in the comparison set, Ascend/JBL is by far the most psychometrically capable. They already validate EMS test prep against NREMT outcomes. The capability is centralized at the corporate level and shared across healthcare/EMS/fitness, with EMS not getting a dedicated specialist — which is both a strength (deep bench) and a gap (EMS is not anyone's sole focus).

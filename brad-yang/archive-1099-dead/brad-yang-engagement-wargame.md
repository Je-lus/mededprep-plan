## The pivot map

Go in on Plan A (1099). Here's every realistic thing that forces a pivot, why, and the exact move:

| Trigger (what Brad / his school says) | Why it forces a pivot | Your move |
|---|---|---|
| "Just need it for my advisor / department records" | No work-authorization layer — pure internal approval | **No pivot.** 1099 letter as-is. Done. |
| "I need CPT, and our office requires an employer–employee relationship" | Some DSOs won't authorize CPT for a contractor | **Convert to W-2 employee for the summer.** You'd run payroll (Gusto/Stripe/etc.) and withhold — but it's a 10-week W-2, very manageable. | Tax: he's a nonresident alien (F-1, < 5 yrs) | Pay to an NRA can trigger 30% federal withholding unless the US–China treaty exempts | **Get his W-8BEN; quick word with the accountant** on treaty Article 20 (students). If withholding is required and messy, that's another nudge toward W-2. |
| CPT not authorized by June 15 | He legally can't start until it clears (can take a week+) | **Slide the start date** — don't start him unpaid/unauthorized. Better a few days late than an immigration violation. Pre-empt this in the reply. |

**The through-line:** every pivot road leads to the same fork — if the school won't take a
contractor, you go **W-2 for the summer** instead of converting later. That's the whole risk, and
it's a known, bounded, totally survivable move. Worst case is "run payroll for 10 weeks." Nothing
here can blow up the hire.

## Refinements worth knowing before you hit a branch

These sharpen two rows above:

1. **NRA tax is not just "a different form" — it's a different regime.**
   If Brad is a nonresident alien performing services, you do **not** issue a 1099 at all. It's
   **Form 1042-S**, with potential **30% federal withholding** unless the US–China treaty reduces
   it. (Treaty Article 20 only exempts ~$5,000 of student personal-services income, so it won't
   fully cover a summer at $50/hr.) Practically: collect the W-8BEN, let the accountant settle
   withholding — and if it's messy, that's a real push toward W-2.

2. **The likely friction for a June 15 start is CPT *timing*, not which form.**
   CPT has to be authorized *before* day one, is tied to his enrollment/program, and the turnaround
   can be a week-plus. If CPT is in play, "slide the start date" is the **probable** outcome, not an
   edge case — plan for it rather than treating it as a surprise.

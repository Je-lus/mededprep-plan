# Brad Yang — W-2 Onboarding Checklist (Summer 2026)

**Goal:** Get Brad set up as MedEdPrep's first W-2 employee (temporary, seasonal) so he can start **June 15, 2026** on CPT work authorization.

**The gating item is the signed offer letter** — he needs it to get CPT authorized by his school. Send `brad-yang-employment-letter.html` first; everything else can run in parallel.

---

## 0. Do these FIRST (they have lead time)

- [ ] **Send the signed W-2 offer letter** to Brad (he submits it to his DSO for CPT). Nothing else moves until he has this.
- [ ] **Confirm Brad has a U.S. Social Security Number (SSN).** You cannot run payroll without it. If he doesn't have one, he needs to apply *now* — it can take 2–4 weeks, which would threaten the 6/15 start.
- [ ] **Confirm with Brad his CPT is authorized** (DSO signs his I-20, page 2). His work can't legally begin until the CPT start date on the I-20.

---

## 1. Register as an employer in Illinois (the unavoidable chore)

Brad works from Illinois, so payroll tax follows him there — not Georgia.

- [ ] **IL income tax withholding account** — register at **MyTax Illinois** (Illinois Dept of Revenue). You'll get an IL withholding account number.
- [ ] **IL unemployment insurance account** — register with **IDES** (Illinois Dept of Employment Security). You'll get a UI account number.
- [ ] **IL New Hire Reporting** — Brad must be reported within 20 days of hire. *(QuickBooks Payroll usually files this for you automatically.)*

> You already have your federal **EIN**, so the hardest piece is done.

---

## 2. Turn on QuickBooks Payroll

- [ ] In QuickBooks, open the **Payroll** tab and activate a plan (**Core** is plenty for one employee).
- [ ] Enter company info: legal name (MedEdPrep, LLC), EIN, Georgia business address, principal officer.
- [ ] **Add Brad as an employee:**
  - Name, IL home address, **SSN**, hire date **6/15/2026**
  - Pay type: **Hourly, $50.00/hr**
  - Pay schedule: **Biweekly** (matches the two-week period in his letter)
  - **Work location = Illinois** ← this is the step that triggers IL tax setup
- [ ] **Mark FICA exemption:** in Brad's tax setup, set him **exempt from Social Security & Medicare** (F-1 nonresident-alien student). Saves ~15% combined. *(Have your CPA / QB support confirm this flag.)*
- [ ] Enter the **IL withholding + IL unemployment account numbers** from Step 1.
- [ ] Collect Brad's **direct deposit** info.
- [ ] Run the **first payroll** after the first two-week period closes.

---

## 3. Onboarding forms to collect from Brad

- [ ] **Form I-9** (employment eligibility) — Brad completes Section 1 by day 1; you complete Section 2 within **3 business days** of his start. Remote option: an authorized representative inspects his original documents on your behalf (or use E-Verify's remote process).
  - F-1 CPT documents: **foreign passport + Form I-94 + I-20 with CPT endorsement**
- [ ] **Form W-4** (federal) — nonresident aliens complete this a specific way per IRS Notice 1392 (generally "Single," limited claims). QB/CPA handles.
- [ ] **Form IL-W-4** (Illinois withholding).
- [ ] **Copy of CPT-endorsed I-20 and SSN card.**
- [ ] **Signed NDA — BEFORE any data access.** (Sam is helping with this.)

---

## 4. After summer

- [ ] **Cancel QuickBooks Payroll** after August if you're not keeping W-2 employees (you can re-activate later).
- [ ] QuickBooks issues Brad's **W-2** in January 2027.

---

## The one thing worth a pro's eyes

A single 15-minute call with a payroll-savvy **CPA** (or QuickBooks Payroll support) to confirm:
1. The **FICA exemption** flag for an F-1/CPT student, and
2. Whether any **tax treaty** (US–China student article) affects his withholding.

Everything else, QuickBooks does for you.

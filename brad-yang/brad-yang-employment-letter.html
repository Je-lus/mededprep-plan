  2. Employment Status. This is a temporary, seasonal, W-2 position. Your employment with MedEdPrep is at-will, which means that either you or MedEdPrep may end the employment relationship at any time, with or without cause and with or without notice, although we will each endeavor to provide reasonable notice. Nothing in this letter creates a contract of employment for any fixed period or guarantees employment for the full term described below. As a temporary position, it does not include eligibility for employee benefits.

You are a non-exempt, hourly employee; hours over 40 in a workweek require advance approval but, if worked, will be paid at one-and-one-half times your regular rate, as required by the Fair Labor Standards Act and Illinois law.
  5. Compensation. You will be paid $50.00 per hour for hours worked, less applicable tax withholdings, through MedEdPrep’s regular payroll. You will record your hours and a brief description of the work performed through MedEdPrep’s internal portal; recorded hours will be totaled at the close of each two-week pay period and paid by direct deposit no later than seven days after the close of that period, consistent with applicable wage-payment law. As a W-2 employee, MedEdPrep will withhold and remit taxes as required by applicable law and will issue an IRS Form W-2 for the year; certain taxes may not apply depending on your tax-residency and visa status, and withholding will be set accordingly.

This offer is contingent on your completing required onboarding documentation, including Form I-9 (employment eligibility verification) and Form W-4, and on your providing and maintaining valid work authorization. We understand this position is being undertaken as Curricular Practical Training (CPT) or equivalent authorization through your academic institution. You agree to provide your CPT authorization and Form I-20 endorsement, naming MedEdPrep, prior to your start date. You may not work on any day for which you do not hold valid CPT authorization; if your authorization is delayed, denied, or ends before the dates above, your start and end dates will adjust accordingly, and the term is automatically limited to the dates of your valid authorization. You represent that the identity and work-authorization documents you provide are true and valid, and you agree to notify MedEdPrep promptly of any change in your work-authorization status.

This obligation, together with your confidentiality obligations, survives the end of your employment.
